<p rend="gatha1">Karuṇā <pb ed="M" n="0.0001" /><pb ed="P" n="0.0001" /><pb ed="V" n="0.0001" /> viya sattesu, paññā yassa mahesino;</p><p rend="gathalast">Ñeyyadhammesu sabbesu, pavattittha yathāruci.</p>
大仙之智慧，于诸有情犹如悲心；于一切所知法中，随欲而转。
As was His compassion for beings, so was the wisdom of the Great Sage; it proceeded through all knowable things according to His wish.
