<p rend="gatha1">Mātaraṃ pamukhaṃ katvā, tassā paññāya tejasā;</p><p rend="gathalast">Abhidhammakathāmaggaṃ, devānaṃ sampavattayi.</p>
以其母为首，凭借其智慧威力，为诸天众开示阿毗达摩(Abhidhamma)论道。
Placing His mother at the forefront, by the power of His wisdom, He set in motion the path of the Abhidhamma discourse for the gods.
