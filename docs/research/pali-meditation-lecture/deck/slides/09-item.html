<p rend="gatha1">Evamevāhaṃ <pb ed="V" n="0.0037" /> imaṃ kāyaṃ, nānākuṇapapūritaṃ;</p><p rend="gathalast">Chaḍḍayitvāna gacchissaṃ, vaccaṃ katvā yathā kuṭiṃ.</p>
我亦复如是，此身充满种种不净，我将舍之而去，犹如用后离厕。
So too, I will discard this body, filled with various kinds of corpses, like leaving excrement in a hut.
