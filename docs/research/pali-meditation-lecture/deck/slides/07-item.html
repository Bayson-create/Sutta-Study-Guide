<p rend="gatha1">Kammaṭṭhānāni sabbāni, cariyābhiññā vipassanā;</p><p rend="gathalast">Visuddhimagge panidaṃ, yasmā sabbaṃ pakāsitaṃ.</p>
一切业处、行、神通与观；此等皆已于《清净道论》(Visuddhimagga)中阐明。
All meditation subjects, conduct, higher knowledges, and insight—since all this has indeed been explained in the Visuddhimagga,
