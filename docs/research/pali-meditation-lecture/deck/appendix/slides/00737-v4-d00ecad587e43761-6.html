ases of knowledge, the great diamond-like knowledge pervading twenty-four trillion attainments, the knowledges for teaching, investigating, and reviewing the all-pervading conditional relations with its infinite methods, and the knowledge discerning the inclinations and so forth of infinite beings in infinite world-systems—and so forth. Tracing and recollecting such Buddha-qualities of inconceivable might in accordance with the Dhamma, he saw no end or measure to them. For even the elder himself, reflecting on his own virtues, could not perceive their limit or measure—how much less could he perceive the measure of the Blessed One’s virtues?
