声形论（词源篇） Saddanītippakaraṇaṃ(Padamālā) · 783

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 巴利语法汇集 Byākaraṇa-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 7 / 8 页

jjhe kesarasīho viya asambhīto nesaṃ nesaṃ vādaṃ maddesi, mahante ca ne atthe patiṭṭhāpesi, evaṃvidhena bhagavatā vohārakusalena yasmā ‘‘kāyo anicco’’ti ca ‘‘kāyo dukkho, mano anicco, mano dukkho’’ti ca evamādinā vuttā <hi rend="bold">mano</hi>saddassa pulliṅgabhāvasūcanikā bahū pāḷiyo dissanti, tasmā <hi rend="bold">mano</hi>saddo pulliṅgoyevāti sārato paccetabboti. Evaṃ vuttā te niruttarā appaṭibhānā maṅkubhūtā pattakkhandhā adhomukhā pajjhāyissanti.</p>
Saccaka the Nigaṇṭha, could not match the Blessed One in debate; on the contrary, the Blessed One, like a lion with a mane amidst a herd of intoxicated elephants, fearlessly crushed their respective doctrines and established them in great matters. Since many Pāli passages indicative of the masculine nature of the word 'mano' are seen, spoken by such a Blessed One, skilled in expression, such as 'the body is impermanent,' and 'the body is suffering, the mind is impermanent, the mind is suffering,' therefore, it should be accepted in essence that the word 'mano' is indeed masculine.
