And the knowledge and vision called Noble Path-knowledge itself, being purified from the stain of delusion, is called purity of knowledge and vision (ñāṇadassanavisuddhi). And here, it should be understood that although inferential knowledge, established through learning, etc., is also knowledge, rejecting that, the term 'vision' (dassana) is used everywhere to mean grasping only direct experiential knowledge of the reality.
