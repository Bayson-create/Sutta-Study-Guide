. 'Endowed with that' refers to persons endowed with those immeasurable states. 'Always with a heart of friendliness' means at all times, with a peaceful and cool-hearted nature. The word 'Brahma' is also an expression for the highest, as in phrases like 'generates supreme merit' and so on. 'To be eaten' (asitaṃ) refers to the five kinds of food. 'To be drunk' (pītaṃ) refers to the eight kinds of drink. 'To be chewed' (khāyitaṃ) refers to various kinds of solid food such as cakes, sweets, and fruits.
