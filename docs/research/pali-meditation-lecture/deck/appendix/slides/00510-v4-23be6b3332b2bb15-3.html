'Patthitaṃ' means long desired, that is, what was wished and yearned for for a long time by the virtuous one: that the elder might compose such a treatise. 'Khuddasikkhāṭīkāyaṃ' refers to an ancient subcommentary. The meaning is that it was known as Somavihāra in that island. In the verse, 'athā' means 'at that time.' 'Etthā' means 'in this city.'
