Therefore, both should not be expanded. As for what is said, 'With a mind imbued with loving-kindness, one dwells pervading one direction,' etc. (Vb. 663), this is stated in terms of encompassing, not in terms of expanding the sign. Moreover, in the divine abodes, there is no counterpart sign at all—what would one expand? The immeasurability of the object here should be understood merely in terms of encompassing.
