The same principle applies to ‘with mindfulness and clear comprehension,’ etc. ‘Universal meditation subjects’ are mindfulness of the Buddha, loving-kindness, mindfulness of death, and contemplation of repulsiveness. For this set of four, when practiced by a meditator, is called ‘universal meditation subjects’ because they serve as a protection for the continuous application of meditation everywhere, requiring unbroken guarding through the power of mindfulness and clear comprehension.
