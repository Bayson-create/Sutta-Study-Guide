its nature of continuity. The turns of reviewing, which are to be produced immediately after the path-consciousness by the exhaustion of defilements, are complete. By reviewing the abandoned defilements in a complete manner, they are worthy of being called the turns of the exhaustion of defilements. Therefore, it is said that by making the 'head of defilements,' which is the exhaustion of ignorance, and the 'head of continuity,' which is the exhaustion of the life faculty, equal, the non-anteriority and non-posteriority of the exhaustion of defilements and the exhaustion of life should be understood by this equality of turns.
