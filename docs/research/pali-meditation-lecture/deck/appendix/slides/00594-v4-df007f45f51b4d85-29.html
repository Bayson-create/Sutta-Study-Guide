界清净读本 Sīmavisodhanīpāṭha · 225

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 杂项文献汇编 Pakiṇṇaka-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 29 / 34 页

t. For if someone prepares a donation, saying, 'I will give a gift to the Sangha,' and goes to the monastery and says, 'Venerable sirs, give me one elder for the Sangha,' and then, having received a novice from the Sangha, thinks, 'I have received a novice,' and changes his intention, his donation is not a gift to the Sangha. Even if he receives a great elder and generates joy, thinking, 'I have received a great elder,' it is still not a gift to the Sangha. His donation is not a gift to the Sangha.
