such an essence. For it has no strength or power to arise without a combination of conditions, or having arisen, to persist even for a moment without the support of conditions. And that which arises only by the power of the combination of conditions, without its own strength or power, and persists only by the support of conditions—that, having generated thousands of sufferings from conditioning, certainly always performs the function of the opposite of self, oppresses, and afflicts.
