一集义注 Ekakanipāta-Aṭṭhakathā · 382

义注 · 义注 Aṭṭhakathā / 经藏义注 Suttapiṭaka-Aṭṭhakathā / 增支部义注 Aṅguttaranikāya-Aṭṭhakathā(Manorathapūraṇī) · 第 3 / 3 页

do ye naṃ bahulīkaronti, this means: for those who practice the first jhāna frequently, who practice it again and again, what is there to say? Concerning them, there is nothing to be said. The remainder here should be understood in the manner stated previously.
