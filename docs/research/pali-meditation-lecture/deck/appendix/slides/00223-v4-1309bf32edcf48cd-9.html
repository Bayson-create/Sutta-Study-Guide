But the knowledge that contemplates the arising aspect or the passing away aspect in terms of a group or a continuity is the knowledge of arising and passing away (udayabbayañāṇa). Among these two aspects, the arising portion becomes increasingly evident, while the passing away portion remains obscure. And that (passing away) is the ultimate limit of impermanence; thus, the knowledge that contemplates it more clearly is the knowledge of dissolution (bhaṅgañāṇa).
