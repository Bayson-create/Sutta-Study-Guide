Some take a large sign from a bloated corpse or a skeleton, while others take a small one. Thus, in this way, for some, the jhāna has a limited object, while for others, it has an immeasurable object. Or, for one who expands it without seeing this lack of benefit, the immeasurability of the object is spoken of in reference to him.
