随灯论诵 Anudīpanīpāṭha · 168

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 31 / 31 页

he moment of performing agricultural work, the field, land, seeds, rain, plows, etc., agricultural implements, cattle, and laborers are the present decisive support. The performance of agricultural work is the conditioned phenomenon of that. The parents and other ancestors of the one who accomplishes the work are the past decisive support condition for that work. That work is the conditioned phenomenon of that. The same applies to the rest. The rest is easy to understand.
