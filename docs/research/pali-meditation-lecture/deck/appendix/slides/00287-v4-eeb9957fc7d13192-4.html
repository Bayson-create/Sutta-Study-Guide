of arising in that way, by way of attrition, thus achieving attenuation. The meaning is that attenuation is achieved by way of infrequent arising and by way of weak obsession. Here, 'by way of infrequent arising' means by way of arising only occasionally and after a long time. And 'by way of weak obsession' means by way of a weaker obsession even for those that arise after a long time. However, the Elder Mahāsīva held that even for them, the occasion of sexual intercourse is possible. This is refuted in the Great Commentary.
