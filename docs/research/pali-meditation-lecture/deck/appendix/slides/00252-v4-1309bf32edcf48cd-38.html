For they are not able to attain arising or cessation in such a short moment. Therefore, it should be understood that here, contemplation in terms of time and continuity is what is intended. By 'for one who thoroughly contemplates the three characteristics' means for one who, having applied the three characteristics to those phenomena, the three-worldly formations, repeatedly touches and polishes those phenomena.
