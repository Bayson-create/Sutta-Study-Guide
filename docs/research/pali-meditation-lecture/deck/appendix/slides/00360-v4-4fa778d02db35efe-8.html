And 'beginning' can also be called 'root' and 'head'—to show this alternative meaning, 'athavā' (or) and so on, is stated. 'Āgamanasambhāresū' means in the resources for coming to this existence from a past existence. The explanation is that craving is spoken of as the head. 'Tesaṃ antare' means between ignorance and formations. Why is it said that there is no place for craving? Surely, when ignorance exists, craving certainly arises.
