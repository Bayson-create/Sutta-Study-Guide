胜义显扬 Paramatthadīpanī-Saṅgahamahāṭīkāpāṭha · 1407

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 7 / 7 页

hich have become defilements of insight, and by means of that comprehension having completely overcome the delight in them, the knowledge that arises which defines the characteristics of path and not-path is called the purification by knowledge and vision of what is path and what is not-path. This is because it is purified from the stain of perceiving the path in what is not the path—illumination and the rest—and from the stain of delight as described. For that knowledge, with delight suppressed, is indeed called thoroughly purified from the impediment of illumination and the rest.
