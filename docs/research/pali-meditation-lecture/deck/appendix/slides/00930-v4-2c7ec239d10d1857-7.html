Illumination, etc.: that is, illumination, knowledge, joy, tranquility, happiness, resolution, exertion, equanimity, mindfulness, and delight. Herein, resolution is faith; `upaṭṭhāna` is mindfulness; equanimity is neutrality in that context. Here, the nine beginning with illumination are called corruptions because they are the basis for the defilement of craving, which is called 'delight.'
