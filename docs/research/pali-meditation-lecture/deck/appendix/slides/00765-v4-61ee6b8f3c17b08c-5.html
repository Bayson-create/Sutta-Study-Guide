大义释 Mahāniddesapāḷi · 1506

根本三藏 · 三藏经文 Tipiṭaka Mūla / 经藏 Suttapiṭaka / 小部 Khuddakanikāya · 第 5 / 5 页

for the Buddhas, the Blessed Ones, that arises at the foot of the Bodhi tree together with the attainment of the knowledge of omniscience. He is not subject to craving, not given to indulgence, not full of longing—thus is the Blessed One.
