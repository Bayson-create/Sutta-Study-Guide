Thus, when the Blessed One had taught the Buddhavaṃsa, the Venerable Dhammasenāpati reflected: "Ah, the perfection of causes for the Buddhas! Ah, the perfection of accumulation! Ah, the fulfillment of the great aspiration! Truly, the Blessed One has done what is difficult, fulfilling the perfections for such a long time. It is only fitting that such an accumulation of the requisites for enlightenment bears such fruit—namely, omniscience, mastery over the powers, such great might, and such great majesty!"
