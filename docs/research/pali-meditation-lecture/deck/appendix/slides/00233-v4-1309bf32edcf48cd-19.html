Here, having divided internal phenomena into two, 'this is only name, not form; this is only form, not name; name is one thing, form another,' and having clearly seen directly that there is no other self, life, being, person, human, god, or Brahma apart from these two, such grasping is called brief grasping. For a person of superior knowledge can suppress identity-view even with this much, along with the distortions of perception and thought. For there are three distortions.
