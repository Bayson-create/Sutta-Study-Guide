波罗夷义注 Pārājikakaṇḍa-Aṭṭhakathā · 1527

义注 · 义注 Aṭṭhakathā / 律藏义注 Vinayapiṭaka-Aṭṭhakathā(Samantapāsādikā) · 第 2 / 2 页

yaṃ cittaṃ assasissāmi passasissāmīti sikkhatī’’ti. Evaṃ cittānupassanāvasena idaṃ catukkaṃ bhāsitanti veditabbaṃ.</p>
-self; from delight through the contemplation of disenchantment; from lust through the contemplation of dispassion; from arising through the contemplation of cessation; and from grasping through the contemplation of relinquishment—breathing in and breathing out. Therefore it is said: “One trains thus: ‘Releasing the mind, I will breathe in; I will breathe out.’” In this way, it should be understood that this tetrad is spoken by way of the contemplation of the mind.
