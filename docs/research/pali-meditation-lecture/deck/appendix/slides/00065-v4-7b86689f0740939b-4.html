阿毗达摩论母 Abhidhammamātikāpāḷi · 1921

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 论藏复注 Abhidhammapiṭaka-Ṭīkā · 第 4 / 4 页

g, restraining, gladdening, and observing with equanimity; avoiding unbalanced persons and associating with balanced persons (two); reflecting on jhāna and liberation, and being inclined towards that. Therein, “skill in signs” means proficiency in grasping kasiṇa and other meditation objects. Five things conduce to the seventh factor: avoiding and associating with persons who delight in worldly activities, and avoiding and associating with moderate persons (four in total); and being inclined towards that. This is the summary here; the detailed explanation, however, should be taken from the Vibhaṅga commentary, the Sammohavinodanī. This is the division of causes here.
