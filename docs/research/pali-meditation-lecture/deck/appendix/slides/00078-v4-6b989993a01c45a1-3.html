根本三藏 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 清净道论 Visuddhimagga · 第 3 / 5 页

kāyakoṭṭhāsanimittārammaṇāya satiyā etamadhivacanaṃ. Ānāpāne ārabbha uppannā sati <hi rend="bold">ānāpānassati,</hi> assāsapassāsanimittārammaṇāya satiyā etamadhivacanaṃ. Upasamaṃ ārabbha uppannā anussati <hi rend="bold">upasamānussati,</hi> sabbadukkhūpasamārammaṇāya satiyā etamadhivacanaṃ.</p>
s a designation for mindfulness that has as its object the qualities of one's own generosity, beginning with open-handed generosity. The recollection that arises with the deities as its object is recollection of the deities (devatānussati); this is a designation for mindfulness that has as its object one's own qualities, beginning with faith, having placed the deities in the position of a witness. The recollection that arises with death as its object is recollection of death (maraṇānussati); this is a designation for mindfulness that has the cutting off of the life faculty as its object.
