'Lakkhitabba dhammehī' means by phenomena to be characterized, such as consciousness, etc. 'Pathamena dutīyassā' means it should be connected with the statement 'there is one connection of the second summary with the first summary', and so on. 'Lokassa pākaṭa vohārenā' means defilement-phenomena and kamma-phenomena are known in the world as causes. Resultant phenomena, however, are known as fruits. Thus, by the common usage in the world. Here, however, whether they are defilement-phenomena or kamma-phenomena, or resultant phenomena, whatever stands on the side of conditions is indeed to be called a cause.
