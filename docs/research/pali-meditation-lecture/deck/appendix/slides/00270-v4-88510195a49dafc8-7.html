To show that after that there is a lapse into the life-continuum, he said, “After that,” and so on. Just as when a person sees some form with the sensitive eye or the divine eye, those eyes, having made that form directly present, and then having made it manifest as directly present to that person, cease. But at the moment of their arising, that form is not yet clear to him as “I see this thing.” Later, however, only when the reviewing processes are occurring, do that form and its various associated aspects become manifest to him.
