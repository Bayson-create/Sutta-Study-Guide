The 'equal-headed' (samasīsī) is of three kinds: the 'equal-headed in posture' (iriyāpathasamasīsī), the 'equal-headed in illness' (rogasamasīsī), and the 'equal-headed in life' (jīvitasamasīsī). Among these, one who, in the postures such as standing, etc., begins insight practice in a particular posture, and attains Arahantship and final Nibbāna in that same posture, is called the 'equal-headed in posture.' One who, having contracted an illness, establishes insight practice within that illness, and attains Arahantship and final Nibbāna with that same illness, is called the 'equal-headed in illness.'
