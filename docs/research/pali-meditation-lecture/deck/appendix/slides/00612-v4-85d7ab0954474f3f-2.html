But in the eight kasiṇas, the brahmavihāra of equanimity, mindfulness of breathing, limited space, and the four immaterial attainments—in these fifteen instances, he entered the fourth jhāna. This too is just a brief account. When entering the city of Nibbāna, the Blessed One, the Master of the Dhamma, having entered all the attainments numbering two hundred and forty million and one hundred thousand, like one embracing relatives when going to a foreign land, having fully experienced the bliss of all attainments, entered.
