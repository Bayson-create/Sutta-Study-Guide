藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 杂项文献汇编 Pakiṇṇaka-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 33 / 34 页

engaged in banditry for twenty-five years. Even in his old age, he continued to commit banditry by cutting off ears and noses with just a breath from his nose for thirty years. Thus, fifty-five years had passed for him while he was engaged in banditry. Lying on his deathbed, he suffered greatly, making a loud noise as if reborn in a great hell by the power of his kamma. Frightened by his sound, people on both sides abandoned their houses and fled. At that time, Venerable Sāriputta, surveying the world with his divine eye, saw him and, out of compassion for him, stood at his house door.
