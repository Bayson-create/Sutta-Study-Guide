on-returner and the functional state for the arahant, namely, the javana of the sphere of neither-perception-nor-non-perception. And at the time of emerging from cessation, because it is merely the outflow of cessation, only a single javana occurs. It is said, 'And at the time of emergence, etc.' There, 'whether it is the fruition of the non-returner or the fruition of the arahant' means in the fruition of the non-returner or in the fruition of the arahant. This is a locative statement indicating the characteristic of being. Therefore, he said, 'when ceased'. 'In all cases of the attainment process' means in the entire attainment process, whether jhāna attainment or fruit
