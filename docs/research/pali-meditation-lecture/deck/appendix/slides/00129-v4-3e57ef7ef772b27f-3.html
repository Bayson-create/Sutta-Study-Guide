nflicts even with the commentaries. Therefore, because many wise ones do not accept it, that view is called 'the view of some' by the Elder. And since, for the preliminary cultivation of all mundane and supramundane jhānas, when near absorption, the preliminary stages of joy-jhānas are accompanied by joy, and the preliminary stages of equanimity-jhānas are accompanied by equanimity; but when not near absorption, their preliminary stages may sometimes be accompanied by joy and sometimes by equanimity. Therefore, that view is rightly considered 'the view of some'. And here, all serenity and insight meditation minds would mostly be accompanied by equanimity at the beginning.
