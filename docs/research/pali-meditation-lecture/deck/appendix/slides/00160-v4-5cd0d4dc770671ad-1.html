胜义显扬 Paramatthadīpanī-Saṅgahamahāṭīkāpāṭha · 1234

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 1 / 1 页

<p rend="bodytext">Aṭṭhakathāyaṃpi nakevalaṃ balavavipassanā maggaphalesueva bojjhaṅge uddharanti. Vipassanāpādaka kasiṇajjhāna ānāpāṇā suka brahmavihārajjhānesupi uddharantīti vuttaṃ. [Bodhipakkhiyasaṅgaho]</p>
于义注(aṭṭhakathā)中亦云：不仅强有力之观(vipassanā)于道果中能引生觉支，作为观之基础的遍处禅(kasiṇajjhāna)、安那般那禅(ānāpāṇajjhāna)、乐俱禅(sukhajhāna)、梵住禅(brahmavihārajjhāna)等亦能引生觉支。[菩提分法集(Bodhipakkhiyasaṅgaho)]
In the commentary also, it is said that the enlightenment factors are brought forth not only by strong insight in the paths and fruits, but also in insight-supporting kasiṇa jhānas, mindfulness of breathing, and the pleasant abidings of brahma-vihāra jhānas. [The Compendium of the Requisites of Enlightenment]
