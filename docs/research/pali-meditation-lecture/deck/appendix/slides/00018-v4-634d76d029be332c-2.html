He resorts to a secluded lodging: a forest, the foot of a tree, a mountain, a glen, a hillside cave, a charnel ground, a forest thicket, the open air, a heap of straw—a place that is quiet, free from noise, secluded from people, private, and suitable for solitary retreat. Having gone to the forest, or to the foot of a tree, or to an empty hut, he sits down, folds his legs crosswise, holds his body erect, and establishes mindfulness before him.
