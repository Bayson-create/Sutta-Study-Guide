Now, the turn has come for the explanation of “and also the hearing of the True Dhamma.” Therein, the hearing of the True Dhamma is indeed rare in the world. For indeed, the True Dhamma is of three kinds: the Dhamma of learning, the Dhamma of practice, and the Dhamma of penetration. Among these, the Dhamma of learning is the Tipiṭaka, the Buddha’s word. The Dhamma of practice consists of the thirteen ascetic practices, the duties of the eighty sections of the Vinaya, the rules of proper conduct, and so forth.
