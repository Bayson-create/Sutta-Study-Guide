But for one who has not yet gained proficiency, in jhāna attainment, from the very beginning, two, three, four, etc., jhāna javanas arise successively, increasing in number. But for those who have gained proficiency, for those of the nature of great persons, from the time of having attained the ability, they are always in a state of practiced mastery.
