Even among these ten remaining meditation subjects, a large object, of the measure of a threshing floor or a circle, is suitable for one of deluded temperament, for in a constricted space, the mind becomes even more deluded. A small object, of the measure of a span or four fingers, is suitable only for one of speculative temperament, for a large object is a cause for the wandering of thoughts. Here, the word 'only' in 'only for one of speculative temperament' applies to the term 'small.' For one of speculative temperament, only a small object is suitable, not a large one.
