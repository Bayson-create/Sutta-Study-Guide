he, while the Omniscient Buddha was still alive, conceived the intention to commit adultery with another's wife and even attempted to take another's life. Hearing the cries of those in hell saying 'du-sa-na-so,' he became utterly confused and, setting aside the Omniscient Buddha, consulted a heretical brahmin. Following his advice, he caused suffering to all people for the sake of a sacrifice—what more need be said about when no Buddha has arisen? Similarly, after the dispensation of the Blessed Kassapa had disappeared and the world had become blind, he was reborn as a king in Bārāṇasī.
