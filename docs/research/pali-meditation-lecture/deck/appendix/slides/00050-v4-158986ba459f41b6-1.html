821. Having thus shown it by way of transcending, he then explains it by way of expanding and not expanding, saying, 'Only the ten kasiṇas,' and so on. Among these forty meditation subjects, only the ten kasiṇas should be expanded. And not... the foulness meditations, etc.; the remaining foulness meditations, etc., however, should not be expanded. Why? Because they appear with a limited form and because there is no benefit. For instance, the ten foulness meditations and mindfulness of the body appear with a limited form due to their confined location.
