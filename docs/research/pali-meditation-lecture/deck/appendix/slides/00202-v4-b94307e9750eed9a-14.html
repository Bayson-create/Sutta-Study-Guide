For indeed, the preparatory consciousness, well concentrated by the power of the basic jhana, becomes of the nature of direct knowledge consciousness in its own sphere. At the time of recollecting past lives, with that very consciousness, one knows the phenomena that occurred in that existence, up to the rebirth-linking. Thus, in the commentary, the knowledge associated with that is called preparatory concentration knowledge. It is also called 'knowledge of the past' (atītaṃsañāṇa).
