Therefore, having seen their momentary dissolution clearly, by means of repeated contemplation such as “form is impermanent, feeling is impermanent,” they release that sign. This is the meaning. “Craving-aspiration” means craving itself, due to attachment, is the placing or firm establishment of the mind on an object; thus, it is aspiration. This is craving-aspiration.
