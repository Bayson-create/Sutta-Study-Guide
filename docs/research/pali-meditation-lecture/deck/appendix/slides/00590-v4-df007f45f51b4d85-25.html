In this section on the 'equal-headed,' since a separate death-proximate thought-process is not obtained, and Nibbāna is attained by the life-continuum consciousness right after the review, seven impulsion consciousnesses are to be expected in the complete manner. This is our view, which should be examined and accepted. Or, if a more suitable explanation is found, it should be sought. Thus, by way of the ultimate Sangha, combining the four pairs of persons, the eight individuals, the forty individuals, and the 108 individuals, it should be understood that even the Sangha is rare in this world.
