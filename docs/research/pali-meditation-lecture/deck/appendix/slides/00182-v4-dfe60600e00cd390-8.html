Equanimity, however, must be developed by taking a completely neutral stance toward beings, and thus it never attains absorption without the feeling of equanimity—hence, it is said that equanimity is of the fifth jhāna. And the four formless states are the four formless objects, such as the sphere of infinite space derived from the removal of the kasiṇa.
