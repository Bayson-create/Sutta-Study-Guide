阿毗达摩入门古复注 Abhidhammāvatāra-Purāṇaṭīkā · 2273

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 论藏复注 Abhidhammapiṭaka-Ṭīkā · 第 2 / 2 页

a tattha saṅkhārā sammasitabbā, atha ākiñcaññāyatanaṃ samāpajjitvā vuṭṭhāya nānābaddhāvikopanaṃ saṅghapaṭimānanaṃ satthupakkosanaṃ addhānaparicchedoti catubbidhaṃ pubbakiccaṃ kātabbaṃ.</p>
rst jhāna. Having emerged from it, the formations therein should be seen with insight as impermanent, suffering, and non-self. Then, having entered the second, third, and fourth jhānas, the base of infinite space, and the base of infinite consciousness and emerged from them, the formations therein should be contemplated in the same way. Then, having entered the base of nothingness and emerged from it, the fourfold preliminary duty should be performed: determination regarding unconnected belongings, awaiting a summons from the Saṅgha, awaiting a summons from the Teacher, and the determination of the duration.
