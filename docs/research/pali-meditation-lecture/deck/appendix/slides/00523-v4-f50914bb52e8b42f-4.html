ed a determination of gender. How? 'Cattāro' is masculine, 'mahābhūtā' is neuter. Therefore, in 'patitaṃ asubhesu munissa mano' also, there can be the determination that 'patitaṃ' is neuter and 'mano' is masculine. Hearing this, they will become silent. Then, to those who have become silent, this should be said: Since appositional words for words occurring in the 'mano'-group are sometimes to be construed in the neuter, therefore, for the word 'mano' itself, which is chief in the 'mano'-group, appositional words are also sometimes construed in the neuter.
