藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 34 / 42 页

' that is called self. What, then, should be grasped in that way? That which always proceeds entirely according to its own will, without the aforementioned activities of conditioning, merely by its own desire, or will, or intention, or command, constantly without failing. That should be grasped in that way. For such a phenomenon indeed performs the function of self for beings who constantly desire happiness and are averse to suffering.
