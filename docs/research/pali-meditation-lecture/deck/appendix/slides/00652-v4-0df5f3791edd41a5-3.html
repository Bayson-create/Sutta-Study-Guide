根本五十经复注 Mūlapaṇṇāsa-Ṭīkā · 458

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 经藏复注 Suttapiṭaka-Ṭīkā / 中部复注 Majjhimanikāya-Ṭīkā · 第 3 / 3 页

nantaraṃ uppajjanato.</p>
ifically the cause for the arising of phenomena that have objects; therefore, he said, 'of all signs,' meaning 'of all objects such as form,' and so forth. 'There is no conditioned sign here,' thus it is signless; 'it is the unconditioned element,' thus he said, 'of the Nibbāna-element, which is devoid of all signs.' He refers to the attention that arises together with the fruition-attainment, not to the attention of adverting. For in this case, there is no possibility of that, since it arises immediately after conformity.
