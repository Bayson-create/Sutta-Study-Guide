法集论义注 Dhammasaṅgaṇi-Aṭṭhakathā · 1193

义注 · 义注 Aṭṭhakathā / 论藏义注 Abhidhammapiṭaka-Aṭṭhakathā · 第 1 / 2 页

<p rend="bodytext">Yathā pana kasiṇānaṃ nissando āruppā, samādhīnaṃ nissando nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṃ, vipassanānaṃ nissando phalasamāpatti, samathavipassanānaṃ nissando nirodhasamāpatti, evaṃ purimabrahmavihārattayassa nissando ettha upekkhābrahmavihāro. Yathā hi thambhe anussāpetvā tulāsaṅghāṭaṃ anāropetvā na sakkā ākāse kūṭagopānasiyo ṭhapetuṃ, evaṃ purimesu tatiyajjhānaṃ vinā na sakkā catutthaṃ bhāvetuṃ. Kasiṇesu pana uppannatatiyajjhānassapesā nuppajjati visabhāgārammaṇattāti.</p>
又，譬如遍处（kasiṇa）的成果是无色定，诸定（samādhi）的成果是非想非非想处，诸观（vipassanā）的成果是果定（phalasamāpatti），止观（samatha-vipassanā）的成果是灭尽定（nirodhasamāpatti）；如是，于此，前三梵住的成果即是舍梵住。譬如不竖立诸柱、不架设横梁（tulāsaṅghāṭa），则不能于空中安置屋顶椽木（kūṭagopanasiyo）；如是，于前三（梵住），若无第三禅，则不能修习第四（梵住）。然而，对于依遍处而生起第三禅者，此（舍）亦不生起，因所缘不同故。
Furthermore, just as the result of the kasiṇas is the formless attainments, the result of concentrations is the base of neither-perception-nor-non-perception, the result of insights is fruition attainment, and the result of serenity and insight is cessation attainment, so too, herein, the result of the triad of the former divine abidings is the divine abiding of equanimity. For just as it is not possible to place rafters in the sky without erecting pillars and placing the main timbers, so too, it is not possible to develop the fourth divine abiding without the third jhāna in the former ones.
