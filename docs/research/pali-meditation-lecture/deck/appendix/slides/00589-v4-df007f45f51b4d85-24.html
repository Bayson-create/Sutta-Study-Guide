But in the case of undeluded death, it allows for six or seven times. And the impulsion consciousnesses in the sense-sphere are of indeterminate number, being complete even in strong cases. Thus, it is stated: 'In strong cases, sense-sphere impulsion consciousnesses occur seven or six times; in slow occurrence, such as at the time of death, only five times.
