阿毗达摩论母 Abhidhammamātikāpāḷi · 1445

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 论藏复注 Abhidhammapiṭaka-Ṭīkā · 第 4 / 5 页

f-lineage, one attains the wholesome path and the first three fruitions; after a functional change-of-lineage, one attains the functional path and the Arahant fruition. There too, from a state accompanied by joy, one attains only that which is accompanied by joy; and from a state accompanied by equanimity, one attains only that which is accompanied by equanimity. Herein, the Arahantship fruition and functional impulsions are only for the non-trainees. The remaining supramundane ones are only for the trainees, each according to their level. The wholesome and unwholesome are for trainees and ordinary people. Absorptions are only for those with three roots. This is the rule
