ya avijjāya heṭṭhānīvaraṇesu saṅgahadassanatthanti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Kiñcāpi paṭhamajjhānūpacāreyeva dukkhaṃ, catutthajjhānūpacāreyeva sukhaṃ pahīyati, atisayappahānaṃ pana sandhāyāha <hi rend="bold">‘‘catutthajjhānaṃ sukhadukkhe’’</hi>ti.</p>
n great insights. For the perceptions of impermanence, etc., succeed in one who is established in the knowledge of discernment by dispelling the delusion that obstructs the comprehension of name-and-form and the overcoming of doubt. Similarly, in the jhāna attainments, through gladness which is a sign of non-delight, when that non-delight is dispelled, there is the attainment of jhāna and so forth; thus, insight through attainments is a means for removing discontent and splitting asunder ignorance.
