<p rend="bodytext">Atha vā cakkhu dukkhasaccaṃ, tassa mūlakāraṇabhāvena samuṭṭhāpikā purimataṇhā samudayasaccaṃ, ubhinnaṃ appavatti nirodhasaccaṃ, nirodhapajānanā paṭipadā maggasaccanti evaṃ ekekapaduddhārenāpi sabbadhamme sammā sāmañca buddho. Esa nayo sotaghānajivhākāyamanesu. Eteneva ca nayena rūpādīni cha āyatanāni, cakkhuviññāṇādayo cha viññāṇakāyā, cakkhusamphassādayo cha phassā, cakkhusamphassajā vedanādayo cha vedanā, rūpasaññādayo <pb ed="M" n="0.0162" /> cha saññā, rūpasañcetanādayo cha cetanā, rūpataṇhādayo cha taṇhākāyā, rūpavitakkādayo cha vitakkā, rūpavicārādayo cha vicārā, rūpakkhandhādayo pañcakkhandhā, dasa kasiṇāni, dasa anussatiyo, uddhumātakasaññādivas
又或者，眼是苦谛(dukkhasacca)，作为其根本原因而生起的前行渴爱(taṇhā)是集谛(samudayasacca)，此二者之不转起是灭谛(nirodhasacca)，了知此灭之道(paṭipadā)是道谛(maggasacca)。如是，仅以逐一抉择一法，佛陀亦能自正等觉一切法。此理亦通于耳、鼻、舌、身、意。并应以此理配合色等六处(āyatana)、眼识等六识身(viññāṇakāya)、眼触等六触(phassa)、眼触所生受等六受(vedanā)、色想等六想(saññā)、色思等六思(cetanā)、色爱等六爱身(taṇhākāya)、色寻等六寻(vitakka)、色伺等六伺(vicāra)、色蕴等五蕴(khandha)、十遍处(kasiṇa)、十随念(anussati)、膨胀想等十想(saññā)、发等三十二身分、十二处、十八界(dhātu)、欲有等九有(bhava)、初禅等四禅(jhāna)、慈等四无量心(appamaññā)、四无色定(arūpasamāpatti)、以及逆观老死等、顺观无明等缘起支(paṭiccasamuppādaṅga)。
Alternatively, the eye is the truth of suffering; the preceding craving that is its root cause is the truth of origin; the non-occurrence of both is the truth of cessation; and the path that is the understanding of cessation is the truth of the path. Thus, by analyzing each term, he understood all phenomena rightly and by himself. This same method applies to the ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind.
