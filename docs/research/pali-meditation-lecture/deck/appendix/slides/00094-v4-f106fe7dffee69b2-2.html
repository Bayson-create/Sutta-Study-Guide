Or, the Bhagavā resorted to forest and woodland thickets, remote dwellings with little sound, little clamor, secluded from people, suitable for people’s privacy, and fit for seclusion (bhaji vā bhagavā araññavanapatthāni… paṭisallānasāruppāni); therefore, he is called ‘Bhagavā’. Or, the Bhagavā is a partaker of robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicinal requisites for the sick (bhāgī vā bhagavā cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānapaccayabhesajjaparikkhārānaṃ); therefore, he is called ‘Bhagavā’.
