Herein, learning is the Tipiṭaka, the Buddha’s word. So long as it, the Pāli together with its commentary, stands, learning is complete. As time passes, unrighteous kings arise in the Kali Age. When those kings, ministers, and so on are unrighteous, the people of the country and districts also become unrighteous. Due to their unrighteousness, the rains do not fall properly, so crops do not flourish. When these do not flourish, supporters cannot give requisites to the Sangha of bhikkhus.
