819. ‘能引生三禅的’，是说依四法系统能引生三禅，依五法系统则能引生四禅。慈、悲、喜，因其与喜俱行，故不成为第五禅（的业处）。因为已达安止的慈心等，无喜则不转起。应连接（前文）说‘以及第四梵住’。梵住，即‘诸最上梵天之住处’，或‘已成梵之住处’。彼（第四梵住）通过修习舍，是为第四禅（业处）。于此，彼（舍心）亦仅使已由慈等证得四禅者安止，而非他人。为何？因其是慈等的流溢。犹如诸遍的流溢是无色定，犹如止观的流溢是灭尽定，如是，慈等的流溢即是舍。‘无色定是第四禅（业处）’是说，因禅支等同，无色定亦被视为第四禅的一种，故作是说。
819. 'Bringing the three jhānas' means that according to the fourfold method, they bring three jhānas, but according to the fivefold method, they bring four jhānas. For loving-kindness, compassion, and sympathetic joy, since they have as their objects only those accompanied by joy, are not 'pertaining to the fifth jhāna.' Indeed, loving-kindness and so on, having reached absorption, do not proceed without joy. The connection is also with the fourth divine abode. A divine abode is the dwelling of supreme Brahmās, or a dwelling that has become sublime; this is a divine abode. And this, by way of the development of equanimity, pertains to the fourth jhāna.
