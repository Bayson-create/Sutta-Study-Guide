大义释 Mahāniddesapāḷi · 2199

根本三藏 · 三藏经文 Tipiṭaka Mūla / 经藏 Suttapiṭaka / 小部 Khuddakanikāya · 第 1 / 1 页

<p rend="bodytext"><hi rend="bold">Kalyāṇapītī</hi>ti buddhānussativasena uppajjati pīti pāmojjaṃ <note>pāmujjaṃ (syā.)</note> – kalyāṇapītīti. Dhammānussati saṅghānussati sīlānussati cāgānussati devatānussati <pb ed="P" n="2.0492" /> ānāpānassati maraṇassati kāyagatāsativasena upasamānussativasena uppajjati pīti pāmojjaṃ – kalyāṇapītīti – paññaṃ purakkhatvā kalyāṇapīti.</p>
‘善喜’(kalyāṇapīti)者：依佛随念(buddhānussati)而生起喜与悦，是为‘善喜’。依法随念(dhammānussati)、僧随念(saṅghānussati)、戒随念(sīlānussati)、舍随念(cāgānussati)、天随念(devatānussati)、入出息念(ānāpānassati)、死随念(maraṇassati)、身至念(kāyagatāsati)、寂止随念(upasamānussati)而生起喜与悦，亦是‘善喜’。此即‘以慧为前导具足善喜’。
“Wholesome joy” means: through recollection of the Buddha, rapture and gladness arise—this is wholesome joy. Through recollection of the Dhamma, recollection of the Sangha, recollection of virtue, recollection of generosity, recollection of devas, mindfulness of breathing, recollection of death, mindfulness directed to the body, and through recollection of peace, rapture and gladness arise—this is wholesome joy. This is the meaning of “placing wisdom foremost, wholesome joy”.
