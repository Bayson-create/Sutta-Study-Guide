Making firm the meaning already stated, he then says, 'For in that case,' and so forth. Having thus first shown skill in learning in serenity meditation, he now, showing the method of cultivation from the beginning, says, 'How?' and so forth. 'For one learning the sign' means for one grasping and observing the object's established appearance, making it clearer and clearer in the mind.
