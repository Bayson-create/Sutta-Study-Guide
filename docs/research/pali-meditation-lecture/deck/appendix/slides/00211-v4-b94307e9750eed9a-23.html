藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 23 / 26 页

or hidden. But because of its resemblance to the divine ear, this direct knowledge is also called 'divine ear'. Or, because it is attained through the divine abiding, which is called kasina jhana, and because it is itself included in that divine abiding, it is called the divine ear. 'They know another's mind with this' (parassa cittaṃ vijānanti etena) is the knowledge of knowing another's mind (paracittavijānanaṃ). That same knowledge is also called 'knowledge of penetration of minds' (cetopariyañāṇa) because 'they know by delimiting with this' (paricchijja jānanti etena). 'Past abode' (pubbe nivāso) is said to be the phenomena that have come into view and are well known
