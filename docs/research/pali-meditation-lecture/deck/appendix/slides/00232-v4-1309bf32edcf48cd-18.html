藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 18 / 42 页

vuttikatādassanato ahetukadiṭṭhi pahīyati. Attano anurūpapaccayasāmaggiyā vinā aññathā pavattiyā adassanato issaranimmāna kālanimmānādikā visamahetudiṭṭhica pahīyati. Hetusambhārapaccayehi kammappavattiyā vipākappavattiyāca aññassa kammakārakassavā vipāka vedakassavā kattuno abhāvadassanato kāraka vedaka diṭṭhi pahīyati. Anamataggeca saṃsāre tīsu addhāsu hetuphala paraṃparāvasena kevalassa nāmarūpamattasseva pavattidassanato ahosiṃ nukho ahamatītamaddhānanti evamādikāya puggalasatta visayāya soḷasavidhāya kaṅkhāyaca atikkamo hoti. Sabhāvadhammapavattiyāca suṭṭhu diṭṭhāya desanādhammassa sudhammatā dassanato buddhasubuddhatañca disvā satthari kaṅkhati dhamme kaṅkhatītiādin
the intrinsic nature of phenomena is called the characteristic, like the heat of fire. The function (rasa) is the task to be accomplished by that nature, or the attainment called the quality obtained by the accomplishing condition, like the cooking and illuminating of fire. That very attainment, or the result called the effect, is called the manifestation (paccupaṭṭhāna), like the smoke of fire. The proximate cause (padaṭṭhāna) is the proximate cause, which is called the uncommon condition for its own arising. By means of these, the grasping of name and form, by defining them with knowledge, is the discernment of name and form (nāmarūpavavatthāna), which is purity of vie
