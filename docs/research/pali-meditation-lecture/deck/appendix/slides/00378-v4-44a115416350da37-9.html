'Those' means lust, etc., which are unwholesome and serve as the strong decisive support for the arising of wholesome phenomena of the four planes—this is the application. 'Through the means of overcoming': in the Paṭṭhāna, in the analysis of the question, 'An unwholesome phenomenon is a condition for a wholesome phenomenon by way of decisive support condition,' it is said: 'Having killed a living being, for the sake of counteracting that, one gives a gift, undertakes virtue, performs the observance-day duty, develops jhāna, develops the path, develops direct knowledge.'
