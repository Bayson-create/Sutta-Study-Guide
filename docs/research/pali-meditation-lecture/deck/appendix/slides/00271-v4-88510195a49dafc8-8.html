藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 8 / 20 页

niddesato pubbe pathamaṃ chinnamanupassane paññāvimutti ñāṇanti evaṃ visuṃ vimuttiñāṇassa āgatattā vuttaṃ. Tattha chinnanti tena tena maggena samucchinnaṃ kilesajātanti attho. Pāḷi anu sārena vuccamāne pana pathamaṃ pahīnaṃ kilesānaṃ paccavekkhanaṃ. Tato paccekaṃ maggaṅgabojjhaṅgādīnaṃ paccavekkhanaṃ. Tato tesaṃ ārammaṇabhūtassa nibbānassa paccavekkhanaṃ. Tato phaladhammānaṃ paccavekkhanaṃ. Tato tesaṃ ārammaṇabhūtassa nibbānassa paccavekkhananti vattabbaṃ hoti. Pāḷiyañhi paccavekkhanañāṇaniddese nibbānaṃ maggavārepi phalavārepi dvikkhattuṃ āgatanti. Iti nibbānapaccavekkhanaṃ nāma itaresaṃ paccavekkhanānaṃ ādimhivā majjhe antarantarāvā avasānevā yatthakatthaci pavattatīti
when the reviewing processes are occurring, do Nibbāna and its associated things, such as the path and fruition, become manifest. It is said that the life-continuum then... proceeds. Now, to show the basis of the knowledge of reviewing, he spoke the verse beginning, “The path, fruition, and Nibbāna.” Therein, “the wise one” is a fruition-attainer endowed with noble wisdom. “In the inferior” means the abandoned defilements. “In the remaining” means the unabandoned defilements to be cut off by a higher path.
