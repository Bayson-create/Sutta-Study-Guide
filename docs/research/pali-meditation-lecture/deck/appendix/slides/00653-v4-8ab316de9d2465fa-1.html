“于此处”，即于此处所说灭尽定之初、中、后三阶段中，取其最后阶段。“以二力”，即以止力与观(vipassanā)力。“三行”，即身行等三种行。“以十六智行”，即以无常随观、苦随观、无我随观、厌离随观、离贪随观、灭随观、舍离随观、还灭随观、须陀洹道……乃至阿罗汉果定，这十六种智行。“以九定行”，即以初禅定等九种定行。若个人(puggala)于如前所说诸行有自在力，此自在力即是慧。此即是为它而说，这是其关联。“决择说”(Vinicchayakathā)，即依决择而转起的义注(aṭṭhakathā)。因此，在《清净道论》(Visuddhimagga 2.868-869)及其释义(taṃsaṃvaṇṇanā)即《清净道论大疏》(Visuddhimagga Mahāṭīkā 2.868)中，应以所说之理趣了知。
'In this place' means in this context where the beginning, middle, and end of the cessation spoken of here have been grasped, specifically at this point where the end is grasped. 'By two powers' means by the powers of serenity and insight. 'And the three formations' means the three formations, beginning with the bodily formation. 'By sixteen modes of knowledge-conduct' means by these sixteen modes of knowledge-conduct: contemplation of impermanence, suffering, non-self, disenchantment, dispassion, cessation, relinquishment, contemplation of turning away, the path of stream-entry... up to... the attainment of the fruition of arahantship.
