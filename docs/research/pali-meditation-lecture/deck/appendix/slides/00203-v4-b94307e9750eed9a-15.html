For those who have attained mastery in the direct knowledges, there is no need for the basic jhana again; therefore, it should be understood that it is not included here. Here, in 'applies towards the six objects—form and so forth—as appropriate,' the knowledge of psychic powers operates on the six objects that exist in the three times.
