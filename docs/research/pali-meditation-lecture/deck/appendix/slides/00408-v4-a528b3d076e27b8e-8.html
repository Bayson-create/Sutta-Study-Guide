'By characteristics and so on' means by characteristics, constituents, and so on. In the phrase 'moves with lust' (rāgena carati), the word 'with lust' (rāgena) is in the instrumental case, indicating the means, or it is a term indicating the characteristic of being such. In the instrumental sense, 'moves with lust' means that in the four postures, one mostly moves with a lustful mind; it is said that one wanders, being led by a lustful mind.
