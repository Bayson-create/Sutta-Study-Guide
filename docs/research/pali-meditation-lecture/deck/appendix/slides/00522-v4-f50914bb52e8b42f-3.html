An appositional word sometimes follows the gender of the principal word, and sometimes it does not. Therefore, it is not an absolute authority for indicating a specific gender. Only specific forms such as 'mātugāmo,' 'orodho, āvuso Visākha,' 'ehi Visākhe,' 'cittāni aṭṭhīnī' are the authority.
