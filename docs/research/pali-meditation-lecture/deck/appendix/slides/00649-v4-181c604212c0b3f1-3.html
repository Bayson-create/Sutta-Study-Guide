根本五十经复注 Mūlapaṇṇāsa-Ṭīkā · 671

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 经藏复注 Suttapiṭaka-Ṭīkā / 中部复注 Majjhimanikāya-Ṭīkā · 第 3 / 3 页

avesu nibbattiatthāni.</p>
unification of mind' means for the purpose of mental concentration, that is, for the purpose of dwelling happily in this very life. For dwelling happily in this very life is stated under the heading of unification of mind, and this is said with reference to dry-insight practitioners and arahants. Therefore, it is said: 'With a unified mind, we will dwell happily for the day.' 'For the purpose of arising in existence' means for the purpose of being reborn in realms of existence.
