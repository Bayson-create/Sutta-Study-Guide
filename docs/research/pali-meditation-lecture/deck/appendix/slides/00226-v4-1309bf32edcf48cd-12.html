so. Tathārūpena parisuddhena bhikkhācārādinā payogena paṭiladdhānaṃ paccayānaṃ paṭiggahaṇa paribhuñjanakālesu sammohassa gedhassa madassa pamādassaca pahānatthaṃ taṃtaṃpayojanamariyādaṃvā paṭikūlalakkhaṇaṃvā dhātumattalakkhaṇaṃvā pariggahaṇa paccavekkhanavasena pavattaṃ paccayasevane paṭisaṅkhāñāṇaṃ <hi rend="bold">paccaya sannissitasīlaṃ</hi>nāma. Parisujjhatīti parisuddhi. Parisuddhieva pārisuddhi. Sā eva sīlanti samāso. Cattāri pārisuddhisīlānīti <hi rend="bold">catupāri suddhisīlaṃ</hi>. Cittavisuddhīti ettha cittasaddena samādhi niddiṭṭhoti vuttaṃ <hi rend="bold">duvidhopisamādhi cittavisuddhi</hi>nāmāti. <hi rend="bold">Lakkhaṇarasapaccupaṭṭhānapadaṭṭhāna vasenā</hi
abandoned fear and delight in formations, is called the knowledge of equanimity toward formations (saṅkhārupekkhāñāṇa). The insight knowledge endowed with strength and power that is in conformity with the arising of the path, by virtue of its ability to lead to the higher path, is called knowledge of conformity (anulomañāṇa). Or it is in conformity with the lower knowledges because of its clearer discernment of the three characteristics. Or it is in conformity with the higher path because it is its preliminary work, and because it is its proximate cause; thus it is called knowledge of conformity. The path that is liberated from the bonds of defilements in the aggregates
