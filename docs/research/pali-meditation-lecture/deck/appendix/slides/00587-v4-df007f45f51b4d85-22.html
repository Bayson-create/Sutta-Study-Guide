But due to the swift change of consciousnesses, the destruction of influxes and the exhaustion of life occur as if in a single moment. And in the Sub-commentary, it is said: 'Two consciousnesses' refers to the death-consciousness and the path-consciousness. 'That' refers to the lack of conditions. 'Complete' refers to the sevenfold occurrence of impulsion consciousnesses. 'Incomplete' refers to the fivefold occurrence.
