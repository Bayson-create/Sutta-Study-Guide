Likewise, in the passage, “For feelings... liberated through non-clinging,” the Blessed One’s five Dhamma-aggregates beginning with virtue, etc., and the wings to awakening such as the foundations of mindfulness are revealed; thus, the mode of entry should also be understood through this approach. “That too is conditioned by contact”—for the designation of the view, the mode of entry by way of impermanence is shown by indicating dependence on conditions. Similarly, by the nature of things, the mode of entry is shown through dependent origination.
