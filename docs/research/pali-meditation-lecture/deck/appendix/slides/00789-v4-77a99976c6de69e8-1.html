“随其所乐住于林野”：犹如象王随其所乐住于林野，独觉佛也随其所乐住于林野。他以初禅随其所乐住于林野，以第二禅……以第三禅……以第四禅随其所乐住于林野；以慈心解脱随其所乐住于林野，以悲心解脱……以喜心解脱……以舍心解脱随其所乐住于林野；也以空无边处定随其所乐住于林野，以识无边处定……以无所有处定……以非想非非想处定……以灭尽定……以果定随其所乐住于林野——“随其所乐住于林野，当如犀牛角独行”。因此，那位独觉佛说：
'He should dwell in the forest as he likes': Just as that great elephant dwells in the forest as he likes, so too the Paccekasambuddha dwells in the forest as he likes. He dwells as he likes in the forest by means of the first jhāna, by means of the second jhāna… by means of the third jhāna… by means of the fourth jhāna. He dwells as he likes in the forest by means of the liberation of mind through loving-kindness, by means of the liberation of mind through compassion… by means of the liberation of mind through sympathetic joy… by means of the liberation of mind through equanimity.
