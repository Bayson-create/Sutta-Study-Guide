'Of the basis for resultants' means the basis-materiality for resultants. In the Paṭṭhāna text, when the object, dependence, pre-nascence, dissociation, presence, and non-disappearance conditions are combined, three answers are obtained—this is the application. Three answers means the basis is a condition for the wholesome aggregates by six conditions; the basis for the unwholesome aggregates; and the basis for the indeterminate aggregates.
