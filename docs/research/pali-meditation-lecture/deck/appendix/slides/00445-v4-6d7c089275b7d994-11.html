r ‘there is no self in this.’ ‘Yañca’ means ‘and whatever form.’ ‘Pīḷeti’ means ‘it oppresses that individual who possesses it.’ By this, it is shown that when the nature of non-self is established, the nature of suffering is also established. ‘The division of time should be applied according to the three periods’: the form occurring in the first period does not reach the second period; it ceases in the first period itself—thus, the division included within the present time should be applied. ‘By way of the continuity of homogeneity’: the continuity unified by the nature of homogeneous phenomena is called the homogeneous continuity.
