Because it is not to be overpowered by faithlessness and the like, in the sense of being unshakable (akampiyaṭṭhena), it is a 'power' (bala). Both of these are fivefold by way of faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, and wisdom. Therefore, they are called the 'five faculties' and the 'five powers.'
