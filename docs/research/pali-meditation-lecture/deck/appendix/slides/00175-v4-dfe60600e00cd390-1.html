Thus, when this is the case, one should address a person of intellectual temperament. Indeed, the qualities of the Buddha and others are exclusively objects for the intellectual temperament. But it is said that these specifically strengthen faith—hence, the recollections of the Buddha and so forth are mentioned for one of faithful temperament. From the ultimate standpoint, subtle phenomena such as death are indeed the domain only of those with vast intellect—thus, the meditation on death is mentioned for one of intellectual temperament.
