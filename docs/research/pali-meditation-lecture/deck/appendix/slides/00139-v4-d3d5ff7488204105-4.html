Tattha <hi rend="bold">anāgāmi phalaṃvā arahattaphalaṃvā</hi>ti anāgāmiphalevā arahattaphalevā. Bhāvena bhāvalakkhaṇe hetaṃ pa
the path javanas, having accomplished their task of abandoning particular defilements, there being nothing further to do, occur only once. It is said, 'The four (path moments) are of a single moment of consciousness.' Alternatively, because the path consciousness is immediately followed by fruition, there is no opportunity for the path consciousness to arise again, as the turn for fruition has arrived with the single arising of the path. Therefore it is said, 'The four (path moments) are of a single moment of consciousness.' By nature, the arising of javana is limited to a maximum of seven moments.
