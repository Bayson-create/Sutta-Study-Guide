Purity of virtue (sīlavisuddhi) is simply constant virtue itself, being purified from the stain of misconduct, and thereby purifying body and speech from it. Purity of mind (cittavisuddhi) is the well-developed continuity of mind itself, being purified from the stain of hindrances, and thereby purifying the individual endowed with it from them.
