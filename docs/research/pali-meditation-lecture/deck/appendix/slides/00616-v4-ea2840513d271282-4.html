Attano dhammatāyāti (by their own nature) means by their own inherent quality, without another's instruction. Tattha tattha guḷakajāta (knots are formed here and there) means a knot of thread (suttagaṇṭhi) that has formed in various places. Therefore, it is called gaṇṭhibaddha (bound by knots) or baddhagaṇṭhika (having knots tied). Paccayesu pakkhalitvā (stumbling over conditions) means stumbling over conditioned phenomena, which have the nature of impermanence, suffering, and non-self, by grasping at permanence and so on.
