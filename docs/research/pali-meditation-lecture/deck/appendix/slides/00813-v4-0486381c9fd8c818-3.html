'Right striving' (sammappadhāna) means striving that is not mistaken, causal striving, striving that is the means, and striving with proper attention. The one and only energy, having been made fourfold by way of function, is called 'right strivings' (in the plural). The four bases of spiritual power are: the base of spiritual power of zeal, the base of spiritual power of mind, the base of spiritual power of energy, and the base of spiritual power of investigation.
