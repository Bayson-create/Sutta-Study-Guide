藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 11 / 26 页

tha iddhividhañāṇaṃ tāva tekālikabhūtesu chasu ārammaṇesu pavattati. Tañhi rūpādīsu chasu ārammaṇadhammesu yaṃ yaṃ nipphādeti. Taṃ taṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā pavattati. Pādakajjhānacittaṃ pana kāyagatikaṃ adhiṭṭhahitvā dissamānenakāyena ākāsegamanakāle atītaṃ pādakajjhānacittaṃ ārabbha pavattati. Kāyaṃ cittagatikaṃ adhiṭṭhahitvā adissamānena kāyena gamanakāle paccuppannaṃ rūpakāyaṃ ārabbha pavattati. Tathāpavattamānassa pana tassa ñāṇassa kiñcirūpaṃ ārammaṇa mattaṃ hoti. Kiñci ārammaṇapurejātaṃ. Kiñci vatthārammaṇa purejātaṃ hotīti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Anāgate idaṃnāma hotūti adhiṭṭhāna kāle anāgataṃ taṃtaṃrūpadhammaṃ ārabbha pavattatīti. fifth form-sphere jhana, occurring by way of direct knowledge, is applied towards the six objects—form and so forth—as appropriate. 'Having emerged from the fifth jhana that is the basis for direct knowledge' means that the fifth form-sphere jhana, which is of the nature of direct knowledge, is attained first of all for the purpose of being a basis for direct knowledge. Then, having emerged by way of the occurrence of the life-continuum. 'To be determined' (adhiṭṭhātabbaṃ) is that which is to be determined (adhiṭṭheyyaṃ), namely, a created form with a basis, such as a hundred forms, a thousand forms, and so forth.
