Therefore, it should be understood that 'if he does not attain it, he goes to the Brahma-world; he is called a jhāna-attainer' is stated as a general rule (yebhuyyavasena). The dry-insight practitioner, however, is of three kinds: the once-seeded, the clan-to-clan, and the seven-times-at-most. Among these, one who, whether in the human world or in one of the six deva worlds, takes one rebirth and attains Arahantship and attains final Nibbāna, is called the 'once-seeded.'
