linations and so forth of infinite beings in infinite world-systems—such and other countless, immeasurable, unique, unsurpassed portions of excellence and divisions of virtue are found and exist in him. Therefore, because he possesses these portions of excellence with their described divisions, while the term should be 'Bhāgavā' (possessor of portions), the long vowel is shortened and he is called 'Bhagavā'. Thus, in this way, he is Bhagavā in the sense of 'one who possesses portions'.
