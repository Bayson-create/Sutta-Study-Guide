藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 杂项文献汇编 Pakiṇṇaka-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 19 / 34 页

‘kāsāvakaṇṭhānaṃ saṅghe dinne dakkhiṇāpi guṇāsaṅkhyāya asaṅkhyeyyā’’ti vuttaṃ. Saṅghagatadakkhiṇā hi saṅghe cittīkāraṃ kātuṃ sakkontassa hoti. Mahātheraṃ labhitvā pi ‘‘mahāthero me laddho’’ti somanassaṃ uppādentassāpi na hotiyeva. Tassa dakkhiṇā saṅghagatā na hoti. Yo pana sāmaṇeraṃ upasampannaṃ vā daharaṃ vā theraṃ vā bālaṃ vā paṇḍitaṃ vā yaṃkiñci saṅghato labhitvā nibbema

Thus, having established oneself in the nineteen reviewing knowledges, one descends into the life-continuum and attains final Nibbāna. By this equality of turns, the exhaustion of both these 'heads' is said to be equal. And in the Arahantship path, the path, emerging from continuity, exhausts the 'head of continuity,' which is the life faculty, by making it cease to arise after death, even if it does so.
