'Ardent energy' means the energy by which meditators scorch themselves or the defilements. That is called 'ardent energy.' Even when lifted, it remains in space. Even when meditating to produce higher jhānas, the fifth jhāna arises repeatedly. Since the object is not transcended, the jhāna is also not transcended. 'There' refers to that space kasiṇa.
