t is stated 'from here seven, from there seven, they wander in saṃsāra,' they speak of the seven-times-at-most by way of seven rebirths in the human world and seven rebirths in the deva world. That is incorrect, because it contradicts the Pāḷi: 'Even if they are extremely heedless, they do not take an eighth existence.' Moreover, a Once-Returner, who is limited to two existences, might come to the human world twice; for him too, the turn of 'one there, one here' could be obtained.
