divine abidings, four formless states, one perception, and one analysis. Among them, the ten kasiṇas are: the earth kasiṇa, the water kasiṇa, the fire kasiṇa, the air kasiṇa, the blue kasiṇa, the yellow kasiṇa, the red kasiṇa, the white kasiṇa, the light kasiṇa, and the limited-space kasiṇa. The ten foulnesses are: a bloated corpse, a discolored corpse, a festering corpse, a split corpse, a gnawed corpse, a scattered corpse, a hacked and scattered corpse, a bloody corpse, a worm-infested corpse, and a skeleton.
