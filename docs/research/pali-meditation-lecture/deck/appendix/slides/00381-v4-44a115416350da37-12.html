This is explained: 'of external trees, grass, etc.' Here, the earth-essence, water-essence, rainwater, and seeds are the strong decisive support for trees and grass. Roots, medicines, etc., are the strong decisive support for the internal continuity—this is the application. 'Of what is produced' means of the kamma of production, etc., done first.
