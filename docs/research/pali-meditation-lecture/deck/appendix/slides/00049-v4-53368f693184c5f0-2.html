阿毗达摩入门古复注 Abhidhammāvatāra-Purāṇaṭīkā · 819

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 论藏复注 Abhidhammapiṭaka-Ṭīkā · 第 2 / 2 页

havipassanānissandā nirodhasamāpatti, evaṃ mettādinissandā upekkhā. <hi rend="bold">Āruppā catutthajjhānikā</hi>ti aṅgasamatāvasena āruppāpi catutthajjhānasseva pabhedāti katvā vuttaṃ.</p>
to absorption only for one who has obtained the four jhānas by way of loving-kindness and so on, not for another. Why? Because it is the outflow of loving-kindness and so on. Just as the formless attainments are the outflow of the kasiṇas, and just as the attainment of cessation is the outflow of serenity and insight, so too is equanimity the outflow of loving-kindness and so on. The statement 'the formless attainments pertain to the fourth jhāna' is made because, due to the equality of their factors, the formless attainments are considered a subdivision of the fourth jhāna.
