藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 20 / 23 页

faith. Having made impermanence, etc., fit for contemplation by wisdom, and having seen more perfectly, one attains the state of a Dhamma-follower and the state of one who is view-attained and liberated by wisdom. Energy, however, is not even an opponent of view and doubt. On the contrary, it is their ally and also what bears the yoke. For energy has the characteristic of exertion. It exerts faith, it exerts wisdom, it exerts view, and it even exerts doubt. How then could it, by its own nature, be able to abandon view and doubt?
