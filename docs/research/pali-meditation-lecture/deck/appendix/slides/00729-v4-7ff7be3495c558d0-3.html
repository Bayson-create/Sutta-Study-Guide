Then the Blessed One willed, “Let that lotus wither.” When he emerged from jhāna and saw that withered, dark-colored lotus, he gained the perception of impermanence, thinking, “This radiant form is crushed by decay.” Then he applied this perception inwardly as well. Then, reflecting, “What is impermanent is suffering; what is suffering is non-self,” he saw the three realms of existence as if ablaze.
