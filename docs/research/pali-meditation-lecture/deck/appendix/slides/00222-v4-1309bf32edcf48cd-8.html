As long as the phenomena in the body are discerned by one's own knowledge as possessing the three characteristics, so long is the repeated touching, re-touching, and thorough touching of them by way of distinguishing the three characteristics. The knowledge that proceeds by way of thorough touching is thorough-touching knowledge (sammasanañāṇa). Arising (udaya) is said to be the appearance of new phenomena in accordance with their respective moments and conditions, and their continuous growth.
