'Having transcended the domain of sensual craving'—by this, the transcending of the sensual realm and sensual existence, and the entering of the form realm and form existence, are stated. Mastery is power, ability. This is the meaning. 'Having attained mastery' means having become masterful. Mastery itself is 'mastership,' just like the perfections.
