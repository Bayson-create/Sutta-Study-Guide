Having secured one's own bodily requisites, [one resolves]: 'May my various belongings, such as robes and other requisites, not be destroyed by dangers like fire'; or, 'If the Sangha is performing a formal act within a single boundary and requires my presence, then I will arise'; or, 'If the Teacher sends for me for some reason, then I will arise'—this is the act of resolving.
