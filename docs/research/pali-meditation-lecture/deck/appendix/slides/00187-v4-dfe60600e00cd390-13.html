d by meditation' means accomplished solely by the power of the meditative mind. 'The object' means that very well-learned sign-object becomes settled and fixed in the mind; that is, having become settled and steadily established in the mind-door thought-process, it is well-entered and inserted. The connection is that it is called the sign-object. Because the appearance of purity and clarity of that sign-object, like that of the moon's disk and so forth, occurs solely by the power of the mind that has attained purity and clarity through the suppression of defilement-stains and has generated radiance.
