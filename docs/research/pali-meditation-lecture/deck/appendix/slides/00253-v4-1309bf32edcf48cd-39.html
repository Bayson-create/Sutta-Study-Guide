nature of those phenomena very evident in knowledge. But especially in this knowledge, the seeing of the characteristic of impermanence is primary. When that is seen, the others are seen in sequence. And when that is not seen, the others are not seen. Therefore, it is intended that the clear manifestation of the characteristic of impermanence is primary from the beginning. 'In those' means in the three-worldly formations themselves. By 'in terms of conditions' means, in the aggregate of form, by way of the conditions called ignorance, craving, clinging, kamma, and nutriment.
