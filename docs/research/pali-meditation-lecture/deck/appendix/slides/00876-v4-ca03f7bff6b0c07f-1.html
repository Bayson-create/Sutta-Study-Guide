波罗夷义注 Pārājikakaṇḍa-Aṭṭhakathā · 1527

义注 · 义注 Aṭṭhakathā / 律藏义注 Vinayapiṭaka-Aṭṭhakathā(Samantapāsādikā) · 第 1 / 2 页

<p rend="bodytext"><hi rend="bold">Vimocayaṃ citta</hi>nti paṭhamajjhānena nīvaraṇehi cittaṃ mocento vimocento, dutiyena vitakkavicārehi, tatiyena pītiyā, catutthena sukhadukkhehi cittaṃ mocento vimocento. Tāni vā pana jhānāni samāpajjitvā vuṭṭhāya <pb ed="P" n="2.0434" /> jhānasampayuttakacittaṃ khayato vayato sammasati. So vipassanākkhaṇe aniccānupassanāya niccasaññāto cittaṃ mocento vimocento, dukkhānupassanāya sukhasaññāto, anattānupassanāya attasaññāto, nibbidānupassanāya nandito, virāgānupassanāya rāgato, nirodhānupassanāya samudayato, paṭinissaggānupassanāya ādānato cittaṃ mocento vimocento assasati ceva passasati ca. Tena vuttaṃ <pb ed="M" n="2.0035" /> – ‘‘vimoca
‘解脱心’，即以初禅使心从诸盖中解脱，以二禅使心从寻与伺中解脱，以三禅使心从喜中解脱，以四禅使心从乐与苦中解脱。或者，进入那些禅那后出定，审察与禅那相应之心的坏灭与消亡。他在观(vipassanā)的刹那，以无常随观使心从常想中解脱，以苦随观使心从乐想中解脱，以无我随观使心从我想中解脱，以厌离随观使心从耽乐(nandi)中解脱，以离贪随观使心从贪欲中解脱，以灭随观使心从集起中解脱，以舍遣随观使心从执取中解脱，他入息、出息。因此说：‘他学习：“我将解脱着心而入息，我将解脱着心而出息。”’应当知道，这四法是依心随观而说。
“Releasing the mind” means releasing and thoroughly releasing the mind from the hindrances with the first jhāna; from initial thought and sustained thought with the second; from rapture with the third; and from pleasure and pain with the fourth. Or alternatively, having attained those jhānas and emerged, one contemplates the jhāna-associated mind as subject to destruction and vanishing.
