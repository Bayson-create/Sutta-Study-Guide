胜义显扬 Paramatthadīpanī-Saṅgahamahāṭīkāpāṭha · 1401

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 26 / 26 页

thinking 'Having passed away from there, where will they be? I will see their future course,' the knowledge of the future, having their future aggregates as object, occurs. And like the knowledge of past-abode recollection, all future phenomena are indeed its object. For it is said in the commentary that in its own sphere, it is of the nature of omniscient knowledge.
