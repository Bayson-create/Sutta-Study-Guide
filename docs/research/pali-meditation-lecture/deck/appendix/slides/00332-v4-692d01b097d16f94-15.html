Here, 'volitional virtue' refers to the volition associated with jhāna. And it should be understood that it is called virtue because it is of the nature of virtue. 'Appanāpatta' is the extraction of the word. 'Jhānabhedenā' should be said. For wholesome form-sphere consciousness is fivefold by the distinction of jhāna, and jhāna itself is fivefold by the distinction of jhāna factors.
