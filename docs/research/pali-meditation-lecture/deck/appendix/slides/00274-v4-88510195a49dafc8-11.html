us, it is established that the reviewing of Nibbāna occurs somewhere—at the beginning, in the middle, in between, or at the end—of the other reviews. Likewise, the reviewing of defilements occurs either at the beginning of or after the reviewing of the path and fruition. When this is so, the verse “The path, fruition, and Nibbāna” is also not contradicted. And having done so, it is said in the commentaries on the Adhimānaniddesa: “For he, with gladdened mind born of reviewing the path, fruition, Nibbāna, abandoned defilements, and remaining defilements, is without doubt in the realization of the noble qualities.” And here, for a stream-enterer there are five reviews.
