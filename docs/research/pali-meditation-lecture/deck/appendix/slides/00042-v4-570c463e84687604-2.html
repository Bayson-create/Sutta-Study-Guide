. Sabbadukkhūpasamabhūtassa nibbānassa guṇānussaraṇaṃ <hi rend="bold">upasamānussati</hi>. Jīvitindriyupacchedabhūtassa maraṇassa anussaraṇaṃ <hi rend="bold">maraṇānussati</hi>. Kesādikāyakoṭṭhāse gatā pavattā sati <hi rend="bold">kāyagatāsati</hi>. Ānañca apānañca ānāpānaṃ, assāsapassāsā, tadārammaṇā sati <hi rend="bold">ānāpānassati</hi>.</p>

Recollecting one’s own qualities such as faith, by establishing deities as witnesses and thinking, "The qualities such as faith, with which the deities were endowed and thereby attained divinity, exist in me," is recollection of deities (devatānussati). Recollecting the qualities of Nibbāna, which is the cessation of all suffering, is recollection of peace (upasamānussati). Recollecting death, which is the cutting off of the life faculty, is recollection of death (maraṇānussati).
