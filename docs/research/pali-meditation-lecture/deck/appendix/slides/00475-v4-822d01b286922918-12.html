indeed accomplished. If you say that when going is being accomplished, there is no fault in the momentary breaking, there is a fault. For momentary death is indeed what is called a charnel ground for a being. When a danger has come in between or when one is stumbling, a going being may at some time or other meet with death or with suffering equivalent to death. The meaning of the word “sign” (nimitta) is that it produces or incites defilements. “Sign of distortion” (vipallāsanimitta) is the functional sign that has appeared by way of distorted phenomena.
