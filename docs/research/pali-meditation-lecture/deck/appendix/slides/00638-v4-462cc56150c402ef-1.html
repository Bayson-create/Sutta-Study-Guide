戒蕴品新复注-1 Sīlakkhandhavagga-Abhinavaṭīkā-1 · 1293

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 经藏复注 Suttapiṭaka-Ṭīkā / 长部复注 Dīghanikāya-Ṭīkā · 第 1 / 3 页

<p rend="bodytext">Tathā sīlaṃ pāṇātipātādīhi viraticetanā, abyāpādādicetasikadhammā ca, pāṇātipātādayo cetanāva, tesaṃ, tadupakārakadhammānañca lajjādayādīnaṃ saṅkhārakkhandhadhammāyatanādīsu saṅgahato purimanayeneva khandhādimukhena otaraṇaṃ vibhāvetabbaṃ. Esa nayo ñāṇadiṭṭhivedanāavijjātaṇhādiggahaṇesupi. Nissaraṇānupādāvimuttiggahaṇesu pana asaṅkhatadhātuvasenapi dhātumukhena otaraṇaṃ vibhāvetabbaṃ, tathā ‘‘vedanānaṃ…pe… anupādāvimutto’’ti etena bhagavato sīlādayo pañcadhammakkhandhā, satipaṭṭhānādayo ca bodhipakkhiyadhammā pakāsitā hontīti taṃmukhenapi otaraṇaṃ veditabbaṃ. ‘‘Tadapi phassapaccayā’’ti sassatādipaññāpanassa paccayādhīnavuttitādīpanena aniccatāmukhena ot
同样，戒(sīla)是远离杀生等的离思(viraticetanā)，以及无瞋等心所法；而杀生等只是思(cetanā)。对于这些戒法，以及惭(lajjā)等对戒有助益的法，因其摄属于行蕴、法处等中，故应只依前述方法，以蕴等为门来阐明其导入。此方法亦适用于把握智、见、受、无明、爱等。然而，在把握出离(Nissaraṇa)、无取解脱(anupādāvimutti)时，亦应依无为界(asaṅkhatadhātu)之力，以界门阐明其导入。同样，由‘诸受……无取解脱’可知，此中开示了世尊的戒等五法蕴，及念处(satipaṭṭhāna)等菩提分法(bodhipakkhiyadhammā)，故亦应以此门了知其导入。‘彼亦缘触’一句，通过阐明施设常见等是依缘而有，是以无常门导入；同样，由于法性如此，是以缘起门导入。因无常者为苦、为无我，故是以无愿门、空门导入。于其余诸句，理路亦然。此即是以缘起等为门，具有导入经义之相，名为导入理趣(otaraṇahāra)。诚然，如是所说：‘缘生法，以及根、蕴、界、处’等（《导论》4.12）。
Likewise, virtue is the volition of abstinence from killing living beings and so on, and mental factors such as non-ill-will. Killing living beings and so on are volition itself. Because these states, and their supporting factors such as shame and compassion, are included in the aggregate of formations, the phenomena-base, and so on, the entry by way of the aggregates and so on should be clarified by the previous method. This is the method also for grasping knowledge, view, feeling, ignorance, craving, and so on. But in the case of grasping escape and liberation without clinging, the entry by way of the element should be clarified even by way of the unconditioned element.
