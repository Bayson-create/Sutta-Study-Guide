resultant consciousness, even at the time of occurrence, it does not produce material form—he says, "there" and so on. Some, however, say that at the moment of arising of the rebirth-linking consciousness, material form does not obtain the post-nascence condition. At the moment of stability, material form obtains the post-nascence condition from the subsequent life-continuum consciousness. That should not be accepted—to show this, he says, "for not by oneself" and so on. A condition that exists by itself and is supportive is the presence condition. Post-nascence is a part of that.
