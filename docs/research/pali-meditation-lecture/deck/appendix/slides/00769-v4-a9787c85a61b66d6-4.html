大义释 Mahāniddesapāḷi · 1529

根本三藏 · 三藏经文 Tipiṭaka Mūla / 经藏 Suttapiṭaka / 小部 Khuddakanikāya · 第 4 / 4 页

ven by ascetics and brahmins, not given by deities. It is a designation for the Buddhas, the Blessed Ones, that is the culmination of liberation, a designation made manifest at the root of the Bodhi tree along with the attainment of omniscient knowledge—that is, 'Bhagavā'. This is the meaning of 'Bhagavā' in the phrase 'the root of conceptual proliferation... the Blessed One'.
