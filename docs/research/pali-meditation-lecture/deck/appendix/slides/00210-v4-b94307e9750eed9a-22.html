藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 22 / 26 页

one's natural appearance and by way of displaying oneself having made oneself into the appearance of a youth, a naga, and so forth, is called transformation power (vikubbaniddhi). The psychic power that occurs by way of creating another body entirely similar to oneself inside one's own body and drawing it forth and displaying it, is called mind-made power (manomayiddhi). 'Divine' (dibbaṃ) means that which exists in the divine realm. This is the meaning of 'born in the divine world'. 'And that is divine and that is ear' (dibbañca taṃ sotañca) is the divine ear (dibbasota). The sensitive ear produced by the superior kamma of devas, capable of receiving objects even far away
