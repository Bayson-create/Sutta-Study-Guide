大义释 Mahāniddesapāḷi · 1506

根本三藏 · 三藏经文 Tipiṭaka Mūla / 经藏 Suttapiṭaka / 小部 Khuddakanikāya · 第 3 / 5 页

He is called the Blessed One. Or, because He was a partaker of the four jhānas, the four immeasurables, and the four formless attainments, He is called the Blessed One. Or, because He was a partaker of the eight liberations, the eight spheres of mastery, and the nine successive dwelling attainments, He is called the Blessed One. Or, because He was a partaker of the ten developments of perception, the ten kasiṇa attainments, the concentration of mindfulness of breathing, and the attainment of foulness, He is called the Blessed One.
