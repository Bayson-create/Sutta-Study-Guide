harge. “Analysis of the four elements” (catudhātuvavatthāna) is the thorough discernment of the four great elements—earth, water, fire, and air—within the body, distinguishing their characteristics individually. It is called catudhātuvavatthāna because by it the four elements are established, discerned, and noted. “Perception of foulness” (asubhasaññā) is to be developed for abandoning lust—thus, the ten foulness meditations are said to be suitable for one of lustful temperament.
