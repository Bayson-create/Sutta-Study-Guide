And it should be seen that by the word “path” here, the fourfold path knowledge is taken, and by way of proceeding in conformity with it, the fruition knowledge is also taken. “Here” means in the insight meditation subject. “Therein” means in those contemplations, path, and fruition. Adherence is `abhinivesa`. The meaning is the proceeding by grasping firmly onto the internal aggregates, like the foot of Mount Sineru planted in the rock of the earth below.
