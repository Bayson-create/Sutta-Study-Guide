四护卫禅明灯论 Caturārakkhadīpanī · 19

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 处世格言集 Nīti-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 1 / 1 页

<p rend="gatha1"> Buddhānussati <pb ed="M" n="0.0004" /> maraṇā, bhubhā mettāca bhāvanā;</p><p rend="gathalast">Appamādāya ārakkhā, catasso mānitā sataṃ.</p>
佛随念、死随念、不净观与慈的修习；此四种护卫能带来不放逸，为贤者所称赞。
Recollection of the Buddha, mindfulness of death, meditation on repulsiveness, and the cultivation of loving-kindness; these four protections for heedfulness are honored by the good.
