随灯论诵 Anudīpanīpāṭha · 168

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 25 / 31 页

n the Paṭṭhāna, the word 'presence' has the meaning of existing in the present state, not in the sense of an ultimate reality, nor in the sense of worldly convention. For not even past and future phenomena, though they are ultimate realities, attain the designation of 'presence' here. Nor does a concept. 'In a mutually bound space' means in the sense-sphere and form-sphere existences not bound in a state of mutual dependence. 'From which' means from which rebirth-linking consciousness. 'From which' or 'from which head, from which seed it arises'—this is the application. 'Like a great tree from a seed' means like the arising of a great tree’s continuity, such as a sprout,
