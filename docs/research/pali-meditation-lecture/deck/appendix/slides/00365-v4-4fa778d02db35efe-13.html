or by birth alone) is stated. 'Iccevaṃ' means thus. There, the connection is that the Great Sage established it by the method of 'ignorance conditions formations' and so on. According to the teacher's view, 'iccevaṃ' means by the method of 'infatuation with aging and death' and so on. The connection is 'the bondage is beginningless'. Here too, taking that same connection, he explains: 'thus they bind. (La.) Again and again, the bondage'. Ignorance is stated at the beginning. Therefore, the objection is that it, being itself without cause or condition, becomes the primary condition of the cycle. He counters that with 'vaṭṭassā' and so on. 'Vaṭṭakathāya sīsabhūtattā' means
