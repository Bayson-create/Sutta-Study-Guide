And from that point on, it does not receive the name “insight.” “Overcoming the lineage of the worldling” means by this, one overcomes the lineage, thus one is a “change-of-lineage” being. And here, “lineage” refers to the state of a worldling, described as the condition of being conjoined with the full range of defilements; this is the meaning it shows. “And attaining the noble lineage” means by this, one attains the lineage, thus one is a “change-of-lineage” being.
