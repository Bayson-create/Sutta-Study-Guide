胜义显扬 Paramatthadīpanī-Saṅgahamahāṭīkāpāṭha · 1395

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 14 / 14 页

found in conceptual phenomena. Therefore, when the counterpart sign arises, the suppression of the obstructive phenomena called hindrances is accomplished—thus, 'from then on,' and so forth, is said. Obstructions, that is, hindrances, are those that obstruct, that is, they cause the mind inclined to liberation to fall. 'Freed from obstructions' means that by this, obstructions are especially abandoned. 'For one cultivating it well' means for one cultivating it thoroughly. The meaning is, for one cultivating it repeatedly through meditation, having fulfilled the sevenfold method of protecting the sign, guarding it thoroughly like the embryo of a wheel-turning monarch.
