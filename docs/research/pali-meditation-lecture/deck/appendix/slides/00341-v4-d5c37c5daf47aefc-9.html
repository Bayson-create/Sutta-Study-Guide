not this text state the absence of in-breath and out-breath at the time of death? It does not state the absence of other consciousness-born material phenomena. Therefore, from this text, it is understood that the death consciousness of all does not produce in-breath and out-breath, not other consciousness-born material phenomena—this is the objection. Addressing that, he says, "for not" and so on. For there is indeed no production of bodily formation by consciousness that produces material form—this is the connection.
