Herein, that which is the aggregate of concentration, this is the faculty of concentration. The aggregate of wisdom is the faculty of wisdom; this should be seen in the four right efforts. Thus, he who strives for the non-arising of unarisen unwholesome states, this is the first right effort. That concerning the arisen, this is the second.
