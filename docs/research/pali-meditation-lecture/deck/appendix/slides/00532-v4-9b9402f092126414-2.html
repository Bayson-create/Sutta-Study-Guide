易解修辞学注 Subodhālaṅkāraṭīkā · 361

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 巴利语法汇集 Byākaraṇa-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 2 / 3 页

yo byabhicārino tasmiṃyeva byabhicārino bhavanti. Iha puttaviyogādianiṭṭhappattiyā ca sīladhammādiiṭṭhanāsanena ca uttamānaṃ soko uppajjati, desacāgādianiṭṭhappattiyā ca dhanādiiṭṭhanāsanena ca nīcānaṃ. Aññesaṃ dhanādivināsanena pana mahākaruṇikattā uttamasseva uppajjati. Tasmiṃ visaye so soko sesānaṃ nuppajjati. Tasmā yathārahaṃ payogo kātabbo. Sokassa rūpo sabhāvoti ca, aniṭṭhassa pattīti ca, iṭṭhassa nāsoti ca, aniṭṭhappatti ca iṭṭhanāso ceti ca, ruditañca palayo ca thambhako ceti ca, te ādi yesamiti ca, visādo ca ālasyañca maraṇañca cintā ceti ca, tā ādi yesamiti ca vākyaṃ. Imassa karuṇārasassa lakkhiyamevaṃ daṭṭhabbaṃ –</p>
, for the superior, sorrow arises from the occurrence of the undesirable, such as separation from a son, and from the destruction of the desirable, such as virtue and the Dhamma. For the inferior, it arises from the occurrence of the undesirable, such as abandoning one’s country, and from the destruction of the desirable, such as wealth. But regarding the destruction of the wealth of others, it arises only for the superior one, due to great compassion. In that matter, that sorrow does not arise for the others.
