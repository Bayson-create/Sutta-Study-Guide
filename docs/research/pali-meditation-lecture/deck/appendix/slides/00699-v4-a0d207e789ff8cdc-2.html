alādidhammānaṃ yāthāvanicchayaṃ, ‘‘sapaccayanāmavavatthāna’’ntipi vadanti. Evaṃ kāmacchandādinīvaraṇappahānena ‘‘abhijjhaṃ loke pahāyā’’tiādinā vuttassa paṭhamajjhānassa pubbabhāgapaṭipadāya bhāvanārāmataṃ pahānārāmatañca dassetvā idāni saha upāyena aṭṭhasamāpattīhi aṭṭhārasahi ca mahāvipassanāhi taṃ dassetuṃ <hi rend="bold">‘‘ñāṇa’’</hi>ntiādimāha. Nāmarūpapariggahaṇakaṅkhāvitaraṇānañhi vibandhabhūtassa mohassa dūrīkaraṇena ñātapariññāya ṭhitassa aniccasaññādayo sijjhanti. Tathā jhānasamāpattīsu anabhiratinimittena pāmojjena tattha anabhiratiyā vinoditāya jhānādīnaṃ samadhigamoti samāpattivipassanaṃ arativinodanaavijjāpadālanaupāyo. Uppaṭipāṭiniddeso pana nīvaraṇasabhāvā
s the perception of light that has arisen through making it distinct and attending to it mentally. 'Non-distraction' means concentration.
