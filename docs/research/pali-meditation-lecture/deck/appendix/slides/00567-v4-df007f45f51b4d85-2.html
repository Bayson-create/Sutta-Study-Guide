When a meditator thinks, 'Oh, may my path be like the first jhāna, having five factors, or like one of the second jhānas, etc., having four factors, etc.,' such an inclination that arises is called the 'individual's inclination' (puggalajjhāsayo). In this regard, if one has attained any of the jhānas, from the first jhāna onwards, and then, rising from it, has contemplated various formations and thereby generated the path, then that path is said to be like that particular foundational jhāna, such as the first jhāna.
