Yo hi ito uddhaṃ mayhaṃ abbhantarimataronāma natthīti evaṃ paramajjhattabhāvena pariggahito hoti. So attāti vuccati. Katamo pana tathāpariggahi tabboti.
Paṭṭhāna, as divided in the compendium of conditions below, grasps in detail. Here too, grasping by the Paṭṭhāna method is not the domain of beginners. But for one who grasps conditions, by seeing that all name and form, as they are established, are entirely dependent on conditions, the rootless view is abandoned. And by seeing that there is no other arising without its own appropriate combination of conditions, the distorted-cause view, such as divine creation, time creation, etc., is abandoned.
