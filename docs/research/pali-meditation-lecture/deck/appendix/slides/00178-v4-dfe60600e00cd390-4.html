Dutīyacatutthāruppajjhānānica pathamatatīyāruppa saṅkhāte sabhāvadhammeti. Here, by 'due to their profundity,' it is shown that because each quality-term is profound, the concentration pertaining to it, like a hare in the great ocean, cannot, having reached absorption there, remain steadily established. And by 'due to the inclination to recollect diverse kinds of qualities,' it is shown that because these qualities are of an insatiable nature, one's mind cannot even restrain itself, thinking, 'I will attain absorption by remaining on just this much of a quality-term.'
