Regarding the statement 'The phrase beginning with infatuation with aging and death should not be said': this was stated because the phrase 'infatuation with aging and death' is taken as a statement connected with forward dependent origination. 'Uparivaṭṭamūla dhammappaṭipādakānaṃ' means of those who apply the root phenomena of the cycle, namely ignorance and craving, in the second future existence and so on, above.
