波逸提 Pācittiyapāḷi · 278

根本三藏 · 三藏经文 Tipiṭaka Mūla / 律藏 Vinayapiṭaka · 第 2 / 2 页

e catto, moho ca me catto, vanto, mutto, pahīno, paṭinissaṭṭho, ukkheṭito samukkheṭito, rāgā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ, dosā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ, mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇa’’nti bhaṇantassa āpatti pācittiyassa.</p>
-returning, the fruit of non-returning, and arahantship; and by me lust has been abandoned, and by me hatred has been abandoned, and by me delusion has been abandoned, disgorged, released, relinquished, given up, uprooted, completely uprooted; and my mind is free from lust, and my mind is free from hatred, and my mind is free from delusion," for one who speaks thus, there is an offense of pācittiya.
