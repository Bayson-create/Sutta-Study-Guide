清净道论-1 Visuddhimagga-1 · 1408

根本三藏 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 清净道论 Visuddhimagga · 第 2 / 2 页

’’ti. Evaṃ <hi rend="bold">cittānupassanāvasena</hi> idaṃ catukkaṃ bhāsitanti veditabbaṃ.</p>
plation of disenchantment; from lust through the contemplation of dispassion; from the origin through the contemplation of cessation; and from grasping through the contemplation of relinquishment, he breathes in and breathes out. For this reason it is said: ‘He trains thus: “I shall breathe in liberating the mind; I shall breathe out liberating the mind.”’ In this way, this tetrad should be understood as spoken by way of the contemplation of the mind.
