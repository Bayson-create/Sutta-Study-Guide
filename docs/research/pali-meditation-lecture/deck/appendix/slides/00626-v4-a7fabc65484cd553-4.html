大品复注（长部） Mahāvagga-Ṭīkā(Dīgha) · 142

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 经藏复注 Suttapiṭaka-Ṭīkā / 长部复注 Dīghanikāya-Ṭīkā · 第 4 / 4 页

tiyeva. <hi rend="bold">Sammadevā</hi>ti suṭṭhuyeva yathā āsavānaṃ lesopi nāvasissati, evaṃ sabbaso <hi rend="bold">āsavehi vimuccati</hi>. Aggamaggakkhaṇañhi sandhāyetaṃ vuttaṃ.</p>
and absorption factors, and of great benefit for being the cause of the distinction of the seven kinds of persons worthy of offerings. “Having stood in which wisdom” means having stood in that wisdom, whether insight wisdom or the wisdom of serenity and insight. For one following the path of serenity, even wisdom accompanied by concentration serves as a special condition for attaining the path. “Perfectly” means quite perfectly—so thoroughly that not even a trace of the taints remains; thus one is completely liberated from the taints. This is said with reference to the moment of the supreme path.
