Cittañhi jānanto taṃ sampayuttadhammaṃpi icchanto jānissatiyeva. Evañca katvā paṭṭhāne kusalā khandhā ceto pariyañāṇassa yathākammupagañāṇassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayoti ñāṇadvayaṃ ekato katvā vuttaṃ. 'Having adverted and prepared' (āvajjitvā parikammaṃ karontassa): here, in the case of psychic powers, first of all, whatever created form, such as a hundred, or created action, such as appearing, one wishes to produce, one adverts to that, reflects on it, and does the preliminary work, thinking: 'May I be a hundred, may I be a thousand,' or 'May it be a hundred, may it be a thousand,' or 'May this and that be thus and thus'.
