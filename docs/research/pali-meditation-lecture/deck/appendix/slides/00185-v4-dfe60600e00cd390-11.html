cultivation, because it is established in the mode of increasing by repeated doing. Having said, 'It is well learned,' to show how that which is well learned is called 'well learned,' it is said, 'It has come into the range of the mind-door as if seen with the eye.' Here, 'it has come into the range of the mind-door' means that for one attending, whether with eyes closed or while staying in another place, it appears in the mind-door as if seen with the eye. This is called the learning sign, because it is grasped without being lost. And that cultivation becomes concentrated, but it does not yet reach the stage of another cultivation—this is the meaning.
