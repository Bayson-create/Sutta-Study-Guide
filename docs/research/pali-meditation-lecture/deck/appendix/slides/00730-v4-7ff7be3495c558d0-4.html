To him, the lotuses in the water appeared like flames of fire in a reed thicket, the falling petals appeared as if entering a chasm, and the withered tips of the lotuses placed on the ground appeared as if burnt by fire. Then, as he contemplated all phenomena in accordance with that, the three realms of existence appeared to him even more intensely as a burning house, without refuge. Then the Blessed One, while seated in the Perfumed Chamber, released a radiance from his body over that bhikkhu. It enveloped his very face.
