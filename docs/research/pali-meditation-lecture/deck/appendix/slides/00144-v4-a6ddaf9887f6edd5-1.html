Nowhere in the Pāli canon is it said that only perceptions and formations are distinguished as "dhammas." For in the Pāli, the six hindrances are first called "dhammas," then the five aggregates subject to clinging, then the twelve sense bases, then the seven factors of enlightenment, then the four truths. Thus, since by stating one kind of contemplation of dhammas the others are also established, only one foundation of mindfulness should be taught, not four.
