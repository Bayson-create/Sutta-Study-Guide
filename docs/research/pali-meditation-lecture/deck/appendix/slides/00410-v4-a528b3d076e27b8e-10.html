It is said, 'for one of a hateful temperament.' 'Due to the absence of a basis for hate' means due to the absence of a basis for the arising of hate and the absence of the nature of being a cause. It is said that the four kasiṇas, such as blue, are for one of a hateful temperament. Why, then, is equanimity not separately mentioned here as a suitable cause for one of a hateful temperament?
