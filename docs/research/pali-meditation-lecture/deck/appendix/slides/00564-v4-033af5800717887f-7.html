In the second case, emerging from the jhāna, reviewing the factors of jhāna, and sinking into the life-continuum, final Nibbāna occurs right there—this is called 'immediately after the reviewing.' It should be understood that the Blessed One attained final Nibbāna immediately after the reviewing, not immediately after the jhāna. Hence it is said: 'The Blessed One, having entered jhāna, emerged, reviewed the factors of jhāna, and attained final Nibbāna with a life-continuum consciousness, which is indeterminate, by means of the truth of suffering.'
