for which it is explained, he speaks the verse: 'One should cultivate it' (bhāvetabbaṃ). Here, 'to be practiced' (paṭipajjitabbaṃ) is practice (paṭipatti). Or, that by which one progresses higher and higher is practice. This refers to the Dhamma beginning with virtue. That which is tasted, that which is experienced by way of experiencing spiritual happiness, is 'flavor' (raso). This refers to the Dhamma of attainment, which is the jhāna, path, and fruition; or, just the fruition of arahantship; or, the joy and gladness arisen dependent on both.
