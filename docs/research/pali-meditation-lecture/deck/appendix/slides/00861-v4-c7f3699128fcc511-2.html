复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 经藏复注 Suttapiṭaka-Ṭīkā / 小部复注 Khuddakanikāya-Ṭīkā · 第 2 / 2 页

ca bhinditvā vimokkhamukhāni yojitānīti.</p>
is a special condition for the contemplation of non-self; thus, insight dependent on it is called “the liberation door of emptiness.” In this way, here, the doors of liberation are connected by distinguishing them according to their mode of occurrence, their support for insight, and their function.
