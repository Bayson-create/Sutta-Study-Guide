Because it is established (upaṭṭhānato) by entering into (okkantitvā) and plunging into (pakkhanditvā) those objects, it is a 'foundation' (upaṭṭhāna). Mindfulness itself is the foundation; hence, 'foundation of mindfulness' (satipaṭṭhāna). Furthermore, its division is fourfold because it proceeds with respect to the body, feelings, mind, and dhammas by way of grasping the characteristics of foulness, suffering, impermanence, and non-self, and by way of accomplishing the task of abandoning the perceptions of beauty, pleasure, permanence, and self.
