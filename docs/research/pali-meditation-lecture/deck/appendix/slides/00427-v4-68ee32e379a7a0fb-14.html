What is the use of concentrating the preliminary mind? It is said, 'for then,' and so on. 'The method should be applied to the other direct knowledges as is appropriate'—the method is to be applied by way of the description: 'in the preliminary work for the divine ear, with that very ear of preliminary concentration and knowledge, one hears a sound that is a divine object even when it is near,' and so on.
