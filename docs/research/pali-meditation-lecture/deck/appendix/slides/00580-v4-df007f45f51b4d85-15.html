He too is of three kinds: the jhāna-attainer, the dry-insight practitioner, and the 'equal-headed' (samasīsī). Among these, the jhāna-attainer who has come by the path itself is called one who has attained the analytical knowledges (paṭisambhidāppatto). Another, who, being a jhāna-attainer in the continuum of a worldling or a trainee, makes jhāna the basis and generates the path, is also a jhāna-attainer.
