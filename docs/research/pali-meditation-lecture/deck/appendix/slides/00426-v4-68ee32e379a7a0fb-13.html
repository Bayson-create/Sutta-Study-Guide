The rest, however, refers to other kinds of manifestation, such as hearing sounds, seeing forms, knowing others' minds, or recollecting past lives. In the commentary text: 'Resolves' means one resolves with direct knowledge. It is said to be 'producing' or 'generating.' There are two kinds of resolve: resolve by preliminary work and resolve by direct knowledge. Here, resolve by direct knowledge is mentioned.
