458. The fourth jhāna of the form sphere, when it proceeds by way of cultivating dispassion for form, is a formless jhāna. The General of the Dhamma, in explaining the base of neither-perception-nor-non-perception, did so with the words, "by the abandoning of pleasure," and so forth. The abandonments of pleasure, etc., are conditions for departure by means of departing and ceasing. But the conditions for attainment are the fourth jhāna of the form sphere in the kasiṇas and the three lower formless attainments.
