藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 3 / 3 页

l of them are found together in a single moment, in a single consciousness, and all are supramundane. Regarding the phrase `Navāsaṅkappa pītiyo` ('Not always intention and joy'), here the word `vā` has the meaning of `kvaci` (sometimes). The meaning is that intention is sometimes not present in the supramundane states of the second jhāna and so on, and joy is sometimes not present in the supramundane states of the fourth jhāna and so on.
