Why, then, does it not proceed according to its own will, not perform the function of self, and perform the function of the opposite of self? He says: 'in the sense of being without essence'. Here, 'essence' is called a special strength and power, which is the ability to arise merely by one's own intention without a combination of conditions, or having arisen, to persist as one wishes without the support of conditions.
