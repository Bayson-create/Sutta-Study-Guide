by that development of the mass of beings, there is no benefit whatsoever, as there is in the development of the earth kasiṇa and so on. By 'by way of grasping' is meant: by way of grasping the object of meditation that has not yet been grasped, not by way of developing the sign. Therefore, he said, 'by one dwelling, two dwellings,' and so on. 'Here' means: in the development of the divine abodes. 'Whatever' means: whatever counterpart sign this yogi... For here, the arising of access concentration is by the breaking of the boundary only, not by the arising of the sign. If so, how can the jhāna have a limited object, etc.?
