戒蕴品义注 Sīlakkhandhavagga-Aṭṭhakathā · 276

义注 · 义注 Aṭṭhakathā / 经藏义注 Suttapiṭaka-Aṭṭhakathā / 长部义注 Dīghanikāya-Aṭṭhakathā(Sumaṅgalavilāsinī) · 第 3 / 3 页

to the mode of conditions practiced by all Buddhas; having produced the fourth jhāna of mindfulness of breathing, and making just that the basis, having developed insight; by means of the fourth path attained in due succession of the paths, having exhausted all defilements, he penetrated all the qualities of a Buddha—this is his function of wisdom. Thus, concentration does not attain to wisdom.
