Having grasped a few beings, loving-kindness and so on, which proceed, have a limited object; having grasped many, they have an immeasurable object—this is the meaning. Space, being merely what is revealed by the removal of the kasiṇa, should not be developed—this is the connection. Or, it should be connected with what will be said. Only a conceptually constructed object can be developed, not another—thus he said, 'for it is not possible to develop a natural phenomenon.' The limited or immeasurable objectivity of the formless attainments should be understood by their proceeding in the space revealed by the removal of a limit
