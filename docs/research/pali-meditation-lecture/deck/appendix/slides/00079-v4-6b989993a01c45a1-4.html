And since that mindfulness is kāyagatā, it should be called kāyagatasati; but without shortening the vowel, it is called kāyagatāsati. This is a designation for mindfulness that has as its object the sign in the bodily parts, beginning with the hair of the head. The mindfulness that arises with the in-breaths and out-breaths as its object is mindfulness of breathing (ānāpānassati); this is a designation for mindfulness that has the sign of in-breaths and out-breaths as its object.
