戒蕴品复注 Sīlakkhandhavagga-Ṭīkā · 193

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 经藏复注 Suttapiṭaka-Ṭīkā / 长部复注 Dīghanikāya-Ṭīkā · 第 2 / 2 页

abuddhānaṃ āciṇṇamevāti vadanti. Chattiṃsakoṭisatasahassamukhena mahāvajirañāṇagabbhaṃ gaṇhāpento <hi rend="bold">vipassanaṃ</hi> <hi rend="bold">vaḍḍhetvā.</hi> Dvattiṃsadoṇagaṇhanappamāṇaṃ <pb ed="P" n="1.0084" /> kuṇḍaṃ <hi rend="bold">kolambo.</hi> Daribhāgo <hi rend="bold">kandaro.</hi> Cakkavāḷapādesu mahāsamuddo <hi rend="bold">cakkavāḷamahāsamuddo.</hi></p>
ard to 'the fourth jhāna based on mindfulness of breathing,' here too the phrase 'practiced by all Buddhas' should be connected by changing the case ending. For they say that this too is indeed practiced by all Buddhas. Having developed insight, causing it to grasp the womb of the great diamond knowledge through the thirty-six hundred thousand koṭi gateways. A `kuṇḍa` or `kolamba` is a vessel with a capacity of thirty-two `doṇa`s. A mountain cleft is a cave. The great ocean at the foot of the world-sphere is the world-sphere’s great ocean.
