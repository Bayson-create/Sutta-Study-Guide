There is momentary dissolution; it breaks up moment by moment. A being, however, is born only once at the beginning of an existence and dies only once at the end. In between, for a hundred or a thousand years, there is no death so-called. The course of momentary phenomena is one thing; the course of a being is another. This being so, how could momentary phenomena be a being’s self in the sense of a core?
