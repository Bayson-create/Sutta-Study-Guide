e the root of the planes of misery. Here, faith and doubt are two direct opposites. In whatever object faith settles, in that there is no doubt. Where doubt settles, in that there is no faith. Similarly, wisdom and view are direct opposites. Where there is wisdom, there is no view. Where there is view, there is no wisdom. Moreover, faith certainly demolishes view in its own domain. As for wisdom demolishing doubt, there is nothing to be said.
