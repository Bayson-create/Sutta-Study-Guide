art-base form—this should be understood. Regarding the knowledge of others' minds, there are discussions by the commentary and subcommentary teachers. To show this, it is said, 'first in the commentary,' and so on. 'In the commentary' refers to the Dhammasaṅgaṇī commentary and the Visuddhimagga commentary. 'Momentarily' means in the present moment. The connection is: 'when advertence has ceased.' 'One mind' refers to another's mind. They grasp that past mind. 'Those' refer to the impulsions. 'Of those' refers to the impulsions.
