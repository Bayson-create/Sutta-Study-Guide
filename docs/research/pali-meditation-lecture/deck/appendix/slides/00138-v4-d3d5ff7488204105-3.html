development. The first arising of appanā is the first appanā. In that first appanā, the initial appanā process is meant. At that time, indeed, all the sublime javanas are very weak due to the lack of repeated practice, so they occur only once. But the higher knowledge javanas, such as the exercise of psychic power and transformation, are applied solely for the accomplishment of the task. Since there is nothing further to be done after the accomplishment of the task, even in the initial period and also when mastery has been attained, it has been said that they occur only once, and that higher knowledge javanas always do so.
