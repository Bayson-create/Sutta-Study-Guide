根本三藏 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 清净道论 Visuddhimagga · 第 2 / 5 页

ṃ. Sīlaṃ ārabbha uppannā anussati <hi rend="bold">sīlānussati,</hi> akhaṇḍatādisīlaguṇārammaṇāya satiyā etamadhivacanaṃ. Cāgaṃ ārabbha uppannā anussati <hi rend="bold">cāgānussati,</hi> muttacāgatādicāgaguṇārammaṇāya satiyā etamadhivacanaṃ. Devatā ārabbha uppannā anussati <hi rend="bold">devatānussati,</hi> devatā sakkhiṭṭhāne ṭhapetvā attano saddhādiguṇārammaṇāya satiyā etamadhivacanaṃ. Maraṇaṃ ārabbha uppannā anussati <hi rend="bold">maraṇānussati,</hi> jīvitindriyupacchedārammaṇāya satiyā etamadhivacanaṃ. s as its object the qualities of the Dhamma, beginning with its being well-proclaimed. The recollection that arises with the Sangha as its object is recollection of the Sangha (saṅghānussati); this is a designation for mindfulness that has as its object the qualities of the Sangha, beginning with their practicing the good way. The recollection that arises with virtue (sīla) as its object is recollection of virtue (sīlānussati); this is a designation for mindfulness that has as its object the qualities of one's own virtue, beginning with its being unbroken.
