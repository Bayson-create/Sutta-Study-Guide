六处品 Saḷāyatanavaggapāḷi · 1186

根本三藏 · 三藏经文 Tipiṭaka Mūla / 经藏 Suttapiṭaka / 相应部 Saṃyuttanikāya · 第 4 / 4 页

assa saññā ca vedanā ca paṭippassaddhā honti. Khīṇāsavassa bhikkhuno rāgo paṭippassaddho hoti, doso paṭippassaddho hoti, moho paṭippassaddho hotī’’ti. Paṭhamaṃ.</p>
yed, lust has subsided, hatred has subsided, and delusion has subsided. There are these six tranquilities, monk. For one who has attained the first jhāna, speech is stilled. For one who has attained the second jhāna, initial application and sustained application are stilled. For one who has attained the third jhāna, rapture is stilled. For one who has attained the fourth jhāna, in-breathing and out-breathing are stilled. For one who has attained the cessation of perception and feeling, perception and feeling are stilled. For a monk whose taints are destroyed, lust is stilled, hatred is stilled, and delusion is stilled.”
