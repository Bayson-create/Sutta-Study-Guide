o are desired to be seen by the great populace. 'Mahiddhikā' means very powerful. 'Adhivasanti etthāti adhivāso' means 'they dwell here'—thus, 'adhivāso' (dwelling). 'Dhaññānaṃ adhivāsoti samāso' is a compound meaning 'the dwelling of the fortunate ones.' 'Ajjhāvasiṃsu etthāti ajjhāvutthaṃ' means 'they lived here'—thus, 'ajjhāvutthaṃ' (inhabited). 'Kittiguṇavasena udenti uggacchantīti uditā' means 'they rise and ascend by way of fame and virtue'—thus, 'uditā' (risen). 'Uditānaṃpi uditoti' is the analysis of the phrase 'risen even among the risen.' Therefore, it is said, 'nabhamajjhe' (in the middle of the sky) and so on. 'Sārajjanaṃ sārado' means agitation.
