th disk. The term “kasiṇa” is indeed a synonym for “whole,” as in “the kasiṇa of Jambudīpa.” Therefore, one must mentally pervade the entire area where the sign is grasped, not just a part of it. Hence, it is said: “The earth kasiṇa is the surface of an earthen disk fully pervaded.” This method applies to the remaining kasiṇas as well. Here, the earth kasiṇa is the shape concept of the earth’s undifferentiated color and unified surface. Similarly, the water kasiṇa and fire kasiṇa.
