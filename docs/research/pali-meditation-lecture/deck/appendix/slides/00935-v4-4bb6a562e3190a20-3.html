Furthermore, because this path is void of lust and so on, it is 'empty.' Due to the absence of the sign of form and so on, or of the sign of lust and so on, it is 'signless.' Due to the absence of aspiration for lust and so on, it is called 'desireless'—this is its naming by its quality. Because it takes as its object Nibbāna—which is void of lust and so on, signless, and desireless—it is called 'empty,' 'signless,' and 'desireless'—this is its naming by its object.
