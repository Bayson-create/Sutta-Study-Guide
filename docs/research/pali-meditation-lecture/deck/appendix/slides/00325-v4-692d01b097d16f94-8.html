ha siddhattā' means how is it established by wearing the robe? Wearing the robe means abandoning householder's clothes and wearing the saffron robe. And the saffron robe is indeed the banner of arahantship. And it is not proper for one who wears the banner of arahantship to eat at improper times, even if one has not undertaken the training rules. Similarly, to watch dances, and so forth, to wear garlands, and so forth, to dwell in high beds, and so forth, to accept gold and silver, and so forth.
