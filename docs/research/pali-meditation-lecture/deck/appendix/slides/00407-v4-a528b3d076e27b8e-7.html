mach. 'Repulsiveness by undigested state' (apakkato paṭikūlatā) is the repulsiveness due to the arising of various diseases in the body when it is undigested and unassimilated. 'Repulsiveness by result' (phalato paṭikūlatā) is the repulsiveness due to the growth of repulsive accumulations such as hair of the head and body hair in the body from the time of its digestion onwards. 'Repulsiveness by discharge' (nissandato paṭikūlatā) is the repulsiveness due to its oozing, dripping, and so on through the nine orifices, hair follicles, or other various wound openings.
