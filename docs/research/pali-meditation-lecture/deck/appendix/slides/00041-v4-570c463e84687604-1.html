Svākkhātatādidhammaguṇārammaṇā anussati <hi rend="bold">dhammānussati</hi>. Suppaṭipannatādisaṃghaguṇārammaṇā anussati <hi rend="bold">saṃghānussati</hi>. Vigatamalamaccheratādivasena attano cāgānussaraṇaṃ <hi rend="bold">cāgānussati</hi>. ‘‘Yehi saddhādīhi samannāgatā devā devattaṃ gatā, tādisā guṇā mayi santī’’ti evaṃ devatā sakkhiṭṭhāne ṭhapetvā attano saddhādiguṇānussaraṇaṃ <hi rend="bold">devatānussati</hi>
8. 持续忆念为随念(anussati)。以阿罗汉等佛陀功德为所缘的随念为佛随念(buddhānussati)。以善说等法功德为所缘的随念为法随念(dhammānussati)。以善行道等僧伽功德为所缘的随念为僧随念(saṃghānussati)。随念自身因无缺等而善清净的戒德为戒随念(sīlānussati)。随念自身因离垢悭等而行布施为舍随念(cāgānussati)。以诸天为证，作如是念：‘诸天因具足信等功德而生为天神，如是功德我亦具有’，由此随念自身信等功德为天随念(devatānussati)。随念作为一切苦之止息的涅槃功德为寂止随念(upasamānussati)。随念作为命根断绝的死亡为死随念(maraṇānussati)。缘于发等身分而转起的念为身至念(kāyagatāsati)。入息与出息为入出息(ānāpāna)，即是吸气与呼气；以之为所缘的念为入出息念(ānāpānassati)。
8. Repeated recollection is recollection (anussati). Recollection with the qualities of the Buddha, such as being an Arahant, as its object, is recollection of the Buddha (buddhānussati). Recollection with the qualities of the Dhamma, such as being well-expounded, as its object, is recollection of the Dhamma (dhammānussati). Recollection with the qualities of the Saṅgha, such as being well-practiced, as its object, is recollection of the Saṅgha (saṃghānussati). Recollecting one’s own quality of virtue, which is perfectly pure by way of being unbroken, etc., is recollection of virtue (sīlānussati).
