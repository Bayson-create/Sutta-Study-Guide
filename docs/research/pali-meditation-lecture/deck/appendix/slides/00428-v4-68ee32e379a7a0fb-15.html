Here, however, since the emphasis is on the method for beginners, the later attainment of the basic jhāna should also be mentioned. 'Having resolved with body-like movement' means having resolved by making the mind move slowly like the body, thinking, 'Just as this body moves slowly, so too should this mind.' 'With the visible body' means with the material body, visible to many due to moving slowly. Its opposite, swift movement, should be understood. 'Merely the object' refers to unproduced form.
