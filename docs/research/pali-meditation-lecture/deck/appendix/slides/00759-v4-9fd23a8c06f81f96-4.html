'Bhagavā' is not a name given by His mother, not given by His father, not given by His brother, not given by His sister, not given by friends and colleagues, not given by relatives and kinsmen, nor by ascetics and brahmins, nor by deities. This name 'Bhagavā' is a designation that is final in liberation, a personally realized term for the Buddhas, the Blessed Ones, that comes together with the attainment of omniscient knowledge at the foot of the Bodhi tree.
