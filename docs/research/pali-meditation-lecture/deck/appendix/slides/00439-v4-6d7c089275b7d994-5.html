In the consciousness aggregate, the diversity of continuity should be understood by way of eye-consciousness and so on, and by way of wholesome and unwholesome consciousnesses. ‘Vissāsaṃ karoti’ (one places trust): having said this, he explains its meaning by saying ‘This is mine,’ and so on. Here, there are three kinds of trust: the trust of craving, the trust of conceit, and the trust of views.
