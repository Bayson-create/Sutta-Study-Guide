Here, among the twenty-three categories of phenomena, whichever category is evident, one should take that and begin contemplation. But one who is able should indeed take all and begin. By 'summarizing by way of a group' means, by means of the five aggregates, having summarized all form, even if differentiated by past, future, present, etc., into a single mass of form; and the same method applies to feelings, and so forth.
