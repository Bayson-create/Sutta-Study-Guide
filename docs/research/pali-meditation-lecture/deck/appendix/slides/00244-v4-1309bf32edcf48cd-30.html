Asati na hontīti itarāni sabbāni kammajesu saṅgahetvā kammavasena udayabbayavidhānaṃ gahitanti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Evaṃ avijjātaṇhupādānakammapaccayānaṃ vasena pañcakkhandhassa udayabbayapavatti hotīti. Kammavasena pana imasmiṃ bhave samudentānipi paṭisandhito paṭṭhāya khaṇe khaṇe kabaḷīkārāhārūpatthambhanaṃ laddhā eva yathā pavattarūpasantatiyo anubandha mānā navanavā rūpasantatiyo samudenti. Aladdhā pana nirujjhanti. Taṃ santatiyaṃ puna anuppādasaṅkhātaṃ ekabhavanirodhaṃ pāpuṇanti, yathā pavattasantatiyo vā chijjanti bhijjanti antaradhāyantīti.</p>
'This earth element is impermanent in the sense of destruction, this water element is impermanent in the sense of destruction,' and so forth. Here, in this body, all that is merely the activity of hardness is called earth. And that activity changes by way of changes in cold and heat, or by way of changes in various foods, or by way of changes in various thoughts, or by way of changes in various postures, or by way of changes in various bodily activities. The meaning is that there is a transition of various continuities. Here, when new continuities of activity appear, the previous continuities of activity perish.
