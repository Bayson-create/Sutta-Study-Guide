清净道论大复注-1 Visuddhimagga-Mahāṭīkā-1 · 849

义注 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 清净道论 Visuddhimagga · 第 6 / 6 页

plain the dispositions and so forth of infinite beings in infinite world-systems—and so on—these infinite, immeasurable, unique, and unsurpassed portions of qualities, divisions of qualities, exist and are found in him. Therefore, because he possesses these portions of qualities with their divisions as described, when `bhāgavā` should be said, having shortened the `ā` vowel, he is called `Bhagavā`. Thus, indeed, he is Bhagavā because he possesses portions of qualities.
