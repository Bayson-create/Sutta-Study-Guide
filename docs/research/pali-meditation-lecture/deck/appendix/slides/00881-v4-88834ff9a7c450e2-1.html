Rāgā me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇ
以三事……乃至七事，想说‘我已入初禅’，却故意妄说‘我已入空解脱……无相解脱……无愿解脱……空定……无相定……无愿定……空等至……无相等至……无愿等至……三明……四念处……四正勤……四神足……五根……五力……七觉支……圣八支道……须陀洹果……斯陀含果……阿那含果……阿罗汉果……乃至……我的贪已舍，我的嗔已舍，我的痴已舍，已吐、已解脱、已断、已弃、已根除、已善根除；我的心于贪已无盖……我的心于嗔已无盖……我的心于痴已无盖’，若听者知晓，则犯波罗夷；若听者不知晓，则犯偷兰遮。事前，彼思：‘我将说妄语’；正说时，彼思：‘我正在说妄语’；说完后，彼思：‘我已说妄语’；隐藏其见，隐藏其忍，隐藏其欲，隐藏其意向。
Even before speaking, it occurs to him, 'I will speak a falsehood.' While speaking, it occurs to him, 'I am speaking a falsehood.' After having spoken, it occurs to him, 'A falsehood has been spoken by me.' Misrepresenting his view, misrepresenting his forbearance, misrepresenting his inclination, and misrepresenting his intention; in three ways... up to seven ways, wishing to say, 'I have entered upon the first jhāna,' for one who knowingly speaks a falsehood, saying, 'I have entered upon the liberation of emptiness… the signless liberation… the desireless liberation… the concentration of emptiness… the signless concentration… the desireless concentration… the attainment
