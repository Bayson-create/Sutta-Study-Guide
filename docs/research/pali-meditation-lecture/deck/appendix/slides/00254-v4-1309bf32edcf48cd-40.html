Here is the method: When ignorance, craving, clinging, and kamma are accumulated in the immediately preceding past existence without being abandoned, the continuities of form, and the continuities of feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness arise in this existence, starting from conception. They arise and produce moment by moment in various groups until the time of death. But when those (ignorance, etc.) have ceased in that very existence by way of being abandoned, those continuities of form and non-form cease.
