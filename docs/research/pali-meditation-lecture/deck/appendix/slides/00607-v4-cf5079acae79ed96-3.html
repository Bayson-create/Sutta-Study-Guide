By the nature of having been and then not being, it is impermanent; by the nature of oppression through rise and fall, it is suffering; by the nature of uncontrollability, it is non-self." Having abandoned attachment in this regard, and also leading others to abandon it, solely out of compassion, until the qualities of a Buddha become as clear as the palm of one’s hand, one establishes beings in the three vehicles through guidance and maturation. And by mastering the jhānas, liberations, concentrations, and attainments, one reaches the very pinnacle of wisdom.
