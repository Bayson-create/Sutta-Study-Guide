t time, when the Dispensation exists, there are no Stream-Enterers. This is the consideration of the ultimate Sangha. The conventional Sangha, however, is a Sangha of worldlings who have received higher ordination by means of a motion followed by three proclamations (ñatticatutthena). This too is rare. Why? Because it is obtained only at the time of the Buddha's arising. For when no Buddha has arisen, even if Paccekabuddhas arise in numbers of hundreds of thousands, higher ordination is not obtained from them.
