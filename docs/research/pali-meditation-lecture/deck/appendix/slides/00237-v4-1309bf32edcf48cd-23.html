rong adherence to the meaning that 'self definitely exists,' 'life definitely exists,' etc., that (over-attachment) is abandoned. And 'strong adherence to that meaning' means a stronger adherence by thinking 'self definitely exists,' 'life definitely exists,' etc. 'Over-attachment' means grasping name and form as 'self,' etc. Grasping of conditions (paccayapariggaha) is also twofold here: brief and detailed. Here, one who grasps the conditions for name by way of base and object, for form by way of mind, temperature, and nutriment, and for both by way of ignorance, craving, clinging, and kamma, grasps briefly.
