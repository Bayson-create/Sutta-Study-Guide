藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 巴利语法汇集 Byākaraṇa-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 6 / 8 页

hāti. Addhā te idampi sutvā nibbeṭhetumasakkontā tuṇhī bhavissanti. Kiñcāpi te aññaṃ gahetabbakāraṇaṃ apassantā evaṃ vadeyyuṃ ‘‘yadi bho <hi rend="bold">mano</hi> saddo napuṃsakaliṅgona hoti, kasmā veyyākaraṇā <hi rend="bold">‘mano</hi>saddo napuṃsakaliṅgo’ti vadantī’’ti? Te vattabbā – yadi tumhe veyyākaraṇamataṃ gahetvā <hi rend="bold">mano</hi>saddassa napuṃsakaliṅgattaṃ rocetha, nanu bhagavāyeva loke asadiso mahāveyyākaraṇo mahāpuriso visārado parappavādamaddano. Bhagavantañhi padakā veyyākaraṇā ambaṭṭhamāṇavapokkharasātisoṇadaṇḍādayo ca brāhmaṇā saccakanigaṇṭhādayo ca paribbājakā <pb ed="M" n="0.0138" /> vādena na sampāpuṇiṃsu, aññadatthu bhagavāyeva mattavāraṇagaṇama
indicated in the neuter. Do you then desire these also to be neuter? Surely, hearing this too, being unable to refute it, they will become silent. And even if they, seeing no other reason to grasp, should say this: 'Sir, if the word 'mano' is not neuter, why do the grammarians say 'the word mano is neuter'?' They should be told: If you, taking the grammarians' view, approve of the word 'mano' being neuter, is not the Blessed One himself the incomparable great grammarian in the world, the great person, confident, the subduer of others' doctrines?
