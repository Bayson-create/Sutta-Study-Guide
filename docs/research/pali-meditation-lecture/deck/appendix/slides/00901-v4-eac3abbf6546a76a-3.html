心义灯-2 Sāratthadīpanī-Ṭīkā-2 · 685

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 律藏复注 Vinayapiṭaka-Ṭīkā · 第 3 / 3 页

tthānaṃ <hi rend="bold">ekaṃ vavatthāna</hi>nti.</p>
recollection of deities, recollection of death, mindfulness of the body, mindfulness of breathing, and recollection of peace. The four divine abidings are: loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity. The four formless states are: the base of infinite space, the base of infinite consciousness, the base of nothingness, and the base of neither perception nor non-perception. The one perception is: the perception of the repulsiveness of food. The one analysis is: the analysis of the four elements.
