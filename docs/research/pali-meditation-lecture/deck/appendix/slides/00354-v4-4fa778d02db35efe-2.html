d places. 'Pabhavā' means the root, the primary causes for the continuity of the succession of cyclic phenomena. Here, the cause must be explained. Otherwise, there would be room for views of divine creation or causelessness. The result must also be explained, lest there be room for annihilationist views. 'Addhuno' means of a long time. 'Nivattetī' means it eradicates. 'Ahetū apaccayā sattā pavattantī' means these beings continue, being without prior causes and without prior conditions.
