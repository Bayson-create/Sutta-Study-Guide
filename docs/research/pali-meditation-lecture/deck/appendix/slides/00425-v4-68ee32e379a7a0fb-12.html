in the work of direct knowledge. 'Such persons' refers to those noble sons who have not done preparatory work before, whose meditation is undeveloped, and who now undertake the work of direct knowledge, thinking, 'I will perform psychic transformations.' 'Psychic transformation' is a representative term. Hearing sounds with the divine ear element, and so on, should also be understood. This is said because it pertains to the method for beginners. Even for those who have mastered the direct knowledges, that fifth jhāna is applied appropriately to the six objects.
