摄阿毗达磨义论 Abhidhammatthasaṅgaho · 19

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 论藏复注 Abhidhammapiṭaka-Ṭīkā · 第 2 / 2 页

ārammaṇasamatikkamanamattaṃ hoti.</p>
quence of the development of purification, while the formless concentration attains it by way of development that transcends the object. For indeed, it is only for the fourth jhāna concentration that has attained absorption that there is the mere transcending of the object.
