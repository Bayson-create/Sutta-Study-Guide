ath, indeed, obtains a name for five reasons: by its own nature, or by its opposite, or by its own quality, or by its object, or by its approach. For if equanimity towards formations, having contemplated formations as impermanent, arises, one is liberated by the signless liberation; if, having contemplated them as suffering, it arises, one is liberated by the desireless liberation; if, having contemplated them as non-self, it arises, one is liberated by the emptiness liberation—this is called naming by its own nature.
