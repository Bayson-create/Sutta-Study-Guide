The meaning is: for a strong reason. “Of them”: of the stream-enterers. “By way of effacement”: by way of thoroughly scraping away the thick side, the coarse part. Spontaneous arising is an arising that occurs having transgressed, having gone beyond the time it occurred previously. Even of the lust, hate, and delusion that arise. “Of them too”: even of the once-returners. “Union in the matter of sexual intercourse is accepted”: it is accepted that for once-returners there is union of husband and wife in the act of sexual intercourse.
