devoted king, placing a bag containing a thousand coins in a golden casket atop an elephant, has a drum beaten in the city, proclaiming, “Whoever knows a four-line verse spoken by the Buddhas, let them take this thousand,” and after the first proclamation, though some hear and some do not, no one takes it, and even after the third proclamation no one takes it, and the king’s men return the bag of a thousand to the royal palace, then learning is said to have disappeared. Thus, when learning disappears, practice and penetration also disappear. The rarity of hearing the True Dhamma should be shown with the story of Dhammasoṇḍaka.
