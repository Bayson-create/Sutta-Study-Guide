Because of the complete absence of defilements that cause failure and the complete perfection of qualities that lead to success, such as the aggregate of virtue, the Arahant, being foremost and chief among those worthy of offerings, is worthy to receive that offering, hence 'foremost worthy of offerings' (aggadakkhiṇeyyo). By 'classification of persons' (puggalabhedo) is meant a brief classification of the four kinds of persons established in fruition.
