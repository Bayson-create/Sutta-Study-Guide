These same five, multiplied by two—the one who attains parinibbāna with prompting (sasaṅkhāraparinibbāyī) and the one who attains parinibbāna without prompting (asaṅkhāraparinibbāyī)—become ten. Similarly, in the Atappa, Sudassa, and Sudassī realms, there are forty-three. However, in the Akaniṭṭha realm, since there is no 'one who goes upstream to the Akaniṭṭha realm,' there are four who attain parinibbāna with prompting and four who attain parinibbāna without prompting, making eight.
