by the power of seeing the emptiness of self or life is called emptiness liberation (suññatāvimokkho). The path that is liberated by demolishing the signs of formations, which are the support for defilements, and by the power of seeing the complete helplessness of phenomena, is called signless liberation (animittovimokkho). The path that is liberated by the power of seeing the complete absence of comfort in formations is called desireless liberation (appaṇihitovimokkho). Contemplation of emptiness (suññatānupassanā) is contemplation of non-self (anattānupassanā).
