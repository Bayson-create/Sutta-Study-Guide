义注 · 义注 Aṭṭhakathā / 经藏义注 Suttapiṭaka-Aṭṭhakathā / 相应部义注 Saṃyuttanikāya-Aṭṭhakathā(Sāratthappakāsinī) · 第 2 / 2 页

kkhābrahmavihāre ānāpāne paricchedākāse catūsu arūpesūti imesu <pb ed="P" n="1.0224" /> pannarasasu ṭhānesu catutthaṃ jhānaṃ samāpajji. Ayampi ca saṅkhepakathāva. Nibbānapuraṃ pavisanto pana bhagavā dhammassāmi sabbāpi catuvīsatikoṭisatasahassasaṅkhā samāpattiyo pavisitvā videsaṃ gacchanto ñātijanaṃ āliṅgetvā viya sabbasamāpattisukhaṃ anubhavitvā paviṭṭho.</p>
nd the ten impurities, in the remaining thirteen instances, he entered the second jhāna… in those very instances, he entered the third jhāna. But in the eight kasiṇas, the divine abiding of equanimity, mindfulness of breathing, the limited space, and the four formless states—in these fifteen instances, he entered the fourth jhāna. And this is just a condensed account. When entering the city of Nibbāna, the Blessed One, the Lord of the Dhamma, having entered all the attainments numbering twenty-four hundred thousand crores, entered after having experienced the bliss of all the attainments, just as one going to a foreign land embraces his relatives.
