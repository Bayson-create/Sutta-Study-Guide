小义释 Cūḷaniddesapāḷi · 1699

根本三藏 · 三藏经文 Tipiṭaka Mūla / 经藏 Suttapiṭaka / 小部 Khuddakanikāya · 第 2 / 2 页

i>nti dhīraṃ paṇḍitaṃ paññavantaṃ buddhimantaṃ ñāṇiṃ vibhāviṃ medhāvinti – saddhiṃ caraṃ sādhuvihāri dhīraṃ.</p>
y means of the attainment of cessation, and one who dwells well by means of the attainment of fruition. `Dhīraṃ` means steadfast, learned, wise, intelligent, knowledgeable, discerning, and sagacious. This is the meaning of the phrase `saddhiṃ caraṃ sādhuvihāri dhīraṃ`.
