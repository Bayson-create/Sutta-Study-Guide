藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 13 / 26 页

nti. Naca aniṭṭhe maggaphalavīthito aññasmiṃ ṭhāne kālamattenāpi tesaṃ tena saha bhinnālambaṇatā aṭṭhakathācariyehi anu matāti. Tasmā īdisesu ṭhānesu paccuppannaṃnāma santativasena addhāvasenaca gahetuṃ yuttanti. Mūlaṭīkāyaṃ pana khaṇikapaccuppanna vaseneva yojitaṃ. Nahi abhidhamme atītārammaṇattikaṃ santati addhānavaseneva vuttanti sakkā vattuṃ. Itarathā paccuppanno dhammo paccuppannassa dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo āsevanapaccayena paccayoti vattabbo siyā. Na tu vutto. Atīto dhammo paccuppannassa dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo āsevanapaccayena paccayoticceva pana vutto, tasmā āvajjanādīni sabbāni cittāni paccekaṃ attanā khaṇavasena sahuppannāni parassa cittā
one reflects on that and does the preliminary work by thinking: 'I will hear the sound of so-and-so' or 'of so-and-sos' and so forth. However, in the commentary, the attainment of the basic jhana again after doing the preliminary work has indeed come. As it is said: 'Having attained the basic jhana for direct knowledge and emerged, if one wishes for a hundred, one does the preliminary work by thinking 'May I be a hundred,' then again attains the basic jhana for direct knowledge and emerges, and then determines. With the determining consciousness itself, it becomes a hundred'. And there, the initial attainment of the basic jhana is for the sake of taking up the preparator
