two arahants, and four persons on the path, making fifty-four. These same fifty-four, multiplied by two according to the vehicle of faith and the vehicle of wisdom, become one hundred and eight persons. By 'here' (ettha) is meant in this classification of persons. By 'all' (sabbesaṃpi) is meant that it is common to all four kinds of persons established in fruition. The meaning is that it is not common to only some, like the attainment of cessation.
