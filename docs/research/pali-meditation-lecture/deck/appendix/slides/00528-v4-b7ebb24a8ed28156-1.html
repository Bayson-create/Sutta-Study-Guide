句身成就 Padarūpasiddhi · 2008

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 巴利语法汇集 Byākaraṇa-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 1 / 1 页

<p rend="bodytext">Chaṭṭhī rañño putto rājaputto. Evaṃ rājapuriso, ācariyapūjako, buddhasāvako, buddharūpaṃ, jinavacanaṃ, samuddaghoso, dhaññānaṃ rāsi dhaññarāsi, pupphagandho, phalaraso, kāyassa lahutā kāyalahutā, maraṇassati, rukkhamūlaṃ, ayassa patto ayopatto, evaṃ suvaṇṇakaṭāhaṃ, pānīyathālakaṃ, sappikumbho.</p>
第六格：国王之子，即‘王子’。如是，‘国王之臣’、‘供养老师者’、‘佛陀弟子’、‘佛像’、‘胜者之言’、‘海之声’。谷物之堆，即‘谷堆’；‘花之香’；‘果之味’。身体之轻快，即‘身轻快’；‘念死’；‘树根’。铁之钵，即‘铁钵’。如是，‘金锅’、‘饮水盘’、‘酥油罐’。
The Sixth (Genitive) Tatpuruṣa: The king’s son is rājaputto. Similarly: rājapuriso (a king's man), ācariyapūjako (a worshipper of the teacher), buddhasāvako (a disciple of the Buddha), buddharūpaṃ (an image of the Buddha), jinavacanaṃ (the word of the Victor), samuddaghoso (the sound of the ocean); a heap of grain is dhaññarāsi; pupphagandho (the fragrance of a flower), phalaraso (the taste of fruit); lightness of the body is kāyalahutā; maraṇassati (mindfulness of death), rukkhamūlaṃ (the root of a tree); an iron bowl is ayopatto. Similarly: suvaṇṇakaṭāhaṃ (a golden pan), pānīyathālakaṃ (a drinking bowl), sappikumbho (a pot of ghee).
