d that the individual for whom the exhaustion of influxes and the exhaustion of life occur neither before nor after each other is called 'equal-headed.' Here, there are two 'heads': the 'head of continuity' and the 'head of defilements.' Of these, the 'head of continuity' is the life faculty. The 'head of defilements' is ignorance. Among these, the death-consciousness destroys the life faculty. The path-consciousness destroys ignorance. There is no simultaneous arising of these two consciousnesses.
