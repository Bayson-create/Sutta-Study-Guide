‘修习佛随念’等六种随念，以及‘修习死随念’、‘修习寂止随念’，此八者是依近行禅而说。‘修习入出息念’、‘修习身至念’、‘修习初禅’等，乃至‘修习非想非非想处定’，此等是依安止禅而说。其中，‘欲如骨锁’者，谓欲以剔净、无肉、沾血的骨锁为譬喻。所谓‘以少味故’(Appassādaṭṭhena)，即是显示‘此中乐味甚少，而过患更多’之义。所谓‘见’(Passanto)，即是以智慧之眼见到‘那只狗只会徒增疲劳与苦恼’。所谓‘远离’(Parivajjeti)，即是远避。此为世尊所说——
The six terms beginning with 'even while developing mindfulness of the Buddha,' and also 'even while developing mindfulness of death,' and 'even while developing mindfulness of peace'—these are spoken by way of access jhāna. 'Even while developing mindfulness of breathing,' 'even while developing mindfulness of the body,' 'even while developing the first jhāna,' and so on, ending with 'even while developing the attainment of the base of neither-perception-nor-non-perception'—these are spoken by way of absorption jhāna.
