Then, after attaining the base of nothingness and emerging, one performs these four kinds of preliminary tasks: ensuring the safety of one's various belongings, being responsive to the Sangha, heeding the Teacher's call, and determining the duration. Then one attains the base of neither-perception-nor-non-perception. After one or two thought-processes have passed, one becomes without mind and touches cessation.
