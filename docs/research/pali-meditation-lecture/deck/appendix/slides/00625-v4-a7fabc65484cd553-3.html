f great benefit in the arising of the bliss of nibbāna. “Having stood in which concentration” means having stood in that concentration which is the basis for supramundane wholesome states, namely the foundational absorption concentration and the concentration leading to emergence. “That” refers to the wisdom of the path and fruition. “Developed by that” means fully developed and cultivated by that aforementioned concentration. Its great fruitfulness and great benefit should be understood in the manner described for concentration.
