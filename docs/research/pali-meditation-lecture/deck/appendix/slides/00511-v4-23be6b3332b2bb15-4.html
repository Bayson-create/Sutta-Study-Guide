'Raṭṭhavaḍḍhane' means 'one who increases the kingdom,' referring to a great elephant. For it is a noble elephant endowed with auspicious marks. In whichever kingdom such an auspicious elephant is honored, in that kingdom the rains fall timely, and various dangers do not arise. Thus, it is regarded in the world as an auspicious elephant.
