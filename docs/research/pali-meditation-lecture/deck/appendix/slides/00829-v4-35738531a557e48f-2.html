Therein, ‘entering the purification of the path’ means: the path itself is the purification because of the cleansing of the stain of the hindrances; ‘entering’ means it has entered that purification of the path. ‘Augmented by equanimity’ means augmented and increased by equanimity as neutrality there. ‘And gladdened by knowledge’ means gladdened, made pure, and cleansed by purifying knowledge.
