Therefore, it should be understood that the four immeasurables are spoken of because if the first three are suitable for someone, then the equanimity that follows them is also suitable. Otherwise, since this equanimity is a release from lust—namely, equanimity as liberation of mind—it should be said to be suitable for one of lustful temperament.
