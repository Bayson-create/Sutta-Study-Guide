e who follow along with the round. People who are by nature manifold (puthu) like dust on the earth are 'worldlings' (puthujjanā). Great worldlings are 'mahāputhujjanā.' The compound is: 'they are followers of the round and they are great worldlings.' Their state is analyzed. This state is also called their plane. In meaning, however, it is simply indifference to the Dhamma of liberation. It is also called lack of aspiration. 'Okkantā' means entered. There are three with fixed destiny: the one with the bodhisatta's fixed destiny, the one with the minor stream-enterer's fixed destiny, and the one with the noble stream-enterer's fixed destiny.
