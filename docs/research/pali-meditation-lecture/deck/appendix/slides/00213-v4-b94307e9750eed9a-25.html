Knowledge of destiny (yathākammupagañāṇa) and knowledge of the future (anāgataṃsañāṇa): these two knowledges are indeed called the accessory knowledges of this divine eye. For there is no separate preliminary work of mind-disciplining for their attainment. When the divine eye knowledge is attained, they are also attained. Here, for one who, having seen beings experiencing suffering in hells or happiness in divine worlds with the divine eye, does the preliminary work, thinking 'I will know their past kamma,' the knowledge of destiny, having that kamma as object, occurs.
