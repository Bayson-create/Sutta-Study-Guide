<p rend="bodytext">Nirodhasamāpatti pana tatiyacatutthaphalaṭṭhānaṃ anupubbanirodhavasena cittacetasikānaṃ dhammānaṃ appavatti. Anāgāmiarahantoyeva hi kāmacchandādisamucchindanena samādhismiṃ paripūrakāritāya samathaphalasamannāgatattā nirodhaṃ samāpajjanti. Tathā hetaṃ samathavipassanānaṃ yuganandhabhāvappavattanavasena dvīhi balehi samannāgatasseva sambhavati, tasmā taṃ samāpajjitukāmena <pb ed="M" n="2.0362" /> anāgāminā, khīṇāsavena vā aṭṭhasamāpattilābhinā rahogatena paṭisallīnena paṭhamajjhānaṃ samāpajjitvā vuṭṭhāya tattha saṅkhārā aniccato dukkhato anattato vipassitabbā, tato dutiyaṃ tatiyaṃ catutthaṃ ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ samāpajjitvā vuṭṭhāya tathev
灭尽定(Nirodhasamāpatti)是第三果与第四果的圣者依次第止息心与心所法，使其不生起的状态。唯有不还者与阿罗汉，因已断除欲贪等，于定中圆满其作用，具足止之果，故能入灭尽定。并且，此定唯有具足止(samatha)与观(vipassanā)双运之力者方能证得。因此，欲入此定的已得八等至(aṭṭhasamāpattilābhinā)的不还者或漏尽者，当独居静处，先入初禅，出定后，当观其中诸行为无常、苦、无我；然后入第二禅、第三禅、第四禅、空无边处、识无边处，出定后亦如是思察其中诸行；其后入无所有处，出定后，当行四种前行：不损坏各种附属物、等待僧团、等待导师召唤、确定时限。
The attainment of cessation, however, is the non-occurrence of mental phenomena and their concomitants by way of successive cessation for one established in the third and fourth fruitions. For only non-returners and arahants attain cessation, due to having eradicated sensual desire and the like, being perfected in concentration, and being endowed with the fruit of serenity. Indeed, this is possible only for one endowed with the two powers, by way of the balanced development of serenity and insight.
