'Lokassā' means from the entire world of beings. The intention is that he uttered a fearless utterance in this way because he was impelled by the nature of things. Therein, 'acchambhi vācaṃ' means a fearless utterance. 'Āsabhiṃ vācaṃ' is another reading, meaning a supreme utterance. 'Nicchāresī' means he uttered.
