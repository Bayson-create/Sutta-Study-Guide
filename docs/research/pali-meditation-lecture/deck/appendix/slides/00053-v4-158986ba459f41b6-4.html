复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 论藏复注 Abhidhammapiṭaka-Ṭīkā · 第 4 / 5 页

tabbā<pb ed="V" n="2.0168" />. Paṭibhāganimittañhi ayaṃ vaḍḍheyya, tañca nesaṃ natthi, tasmā ime asubhādayo tiṃsa kammaṭṭhānāni paricchinnokāsattā, payojanābhāvato, avaḍḍhanato ca na vaḍḍhetabbāni.</p>
—should be attended to by way of the kasiṇa's removal. Beyond that, expanding it yields nothing. Consciousness, being a natural phenomenon, cannot be expanded—only the preliminary meditation can be expanded. The passing away of consciousness is merely its absence. The object of the base of neither-perception-nor-non-perception, being a natural phenomenon, also cannot be expanded. Therefore, these too should not be expanded. The immeasurability of the objects of the formless attainments should be understood as their occurring in the space made vast by the removal of the kasiṇa.
