随灯论诵 Anudīpanīpāṭha · 405

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 23 / 23 页

thus he is “one bound upstream” (uddhaṃsoto). He will definitely go to the Akaniṭṭha realm, thus he is “one bound for Akaniṭṭha” (akaniṭṭhagāmī). One who attains Nibbāna having done the work of insight with prompting and with exertion is one who attains Nibbāna with prompting (sasaṅkhāraparinibbāyī). One who attains Nibbāna by the work of insight with ease, without prompting, is one who attains Nibbāna without prompting (asaṅkhāraparinibbāyī). “Twelve stream-enterers”: the three kinds of stream-enterers, multiplied by the four modes of practice, become twelve. The twelve once-returners are just as stated before.
