戒蕴品新复注-1 Sīlakkhandhavagga-Abhinavaṭīkā-1 · 1293

复注 · 复注 Ṭīkā / 经藏复注 Suttapiṭaka-Ṭīkā / 长部复注 Dīghanikāya-Ṭīkā · 第 2 / 3 页

araṇaṃ, tathā evaṃdhammatāya <hi rend="bold">paṭiccasamuppādamukhena otaraṇaṃ.</hi> Aniccassa dukkhānattabhāvato <hi rend="bold">appaṇihitamukhena, suññatāmukhena otaraṇaṃ.</hi> Sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Ayaṃ paṭiccasamuppādādimukhehi suttatthassa otaraṇalakkhaṇo <hi rend="bold">otaraṇahāro</hi> nāma. Tathā hi vuttaṃ ‘‘yo ca paṭiccuppādo, indriyakhandhā ca dhātuāyatanā’’tiādi (netti. 4.12).</p>
Likewise, by the text 'of feelings... liberated without clinging,' the Blessed One's five Dhamma aggregates beginning with virtue, and the factors of awakening beginning with the foundations of mindfulness, are revealed; thus, entry should also be known by way of these. By the text 'that too is conditioned by contact,' through showing that the designation of eternalism and so on is dependently arisen, entry should be known by way of impermanence. Likewise, because of the nature of things as such, entry should be known by way of dependent origination. Because what is impermanent is suffering and non-self, entry is made by way of the desireless and by way of emptiness. Thi
