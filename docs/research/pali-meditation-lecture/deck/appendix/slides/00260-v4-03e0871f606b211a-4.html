胜义显扬 Paramatthadīpanī-Saṅgahamahāṭīkāpāṭha · 1407

藏外典籍 · 藏外典籍 Añña / 雷迪西亚多著作集 Leḍī-Sayāḍo-Gantha-Saṅgaho · 第 4 / 7 页

Tesu hi uppajjamānesu na vata me ito pubbe evarūpo obhāso uppannapubbo. Evarūpā pīti.La. Upekkhā uppannapubbā addhā maggaṃ pattosmi. Phalaṃ pattosmīti evaṃ assādasahitā amagge maggasaññā aphale phalasaññā uppajjati. Tadā koci abyatto yogāvacaro kammaṭṭhānaṃ vissajjetvā adhimānavasena vikkhipanto vicaratīti. Byatto pana tāvadeva sabhiṃ paṭilabhitvā idāni mama etesu obhāsādīsu etaṃ mama eso ha masmi eso me attāti cittassa nikāmanākāro paññāyati. Naca lokuttaradhammonāma īdisassa nikāmanassa vatthu. Addhā mama saṃkilesadhammo uppanno. So vaḍḍhamāno maṃ niyyānamukhato pātetvā vaṭṭamukhe yojessati. Handa tesveva lakkhaṇattayaṃ āropetvā nikantiṃ sodhetvā yathāpavattaṃ vipassanā
inds of rapture associated with insight consciousness. 'Tranquility' is the two kinds of tranquility. 'Resolution' is faith-resolution. 'Exertion' is energy. 'Happiness' is mental pleasure. 'Knowledge' is insight knowledge. 'Establishment' is mindfulness. 'Equanimity' is both equanimity of neutrality towards things and equanimity of adverting. 'Delight' is the subtle craving that, along with illumination and the rest, makes a home in insight. This alone is exclusively a defilement of insight.
