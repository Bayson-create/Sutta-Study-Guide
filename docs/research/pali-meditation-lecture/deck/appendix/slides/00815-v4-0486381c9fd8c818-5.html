无碍解道义注 Paṭisambhidāmagga-Aṭṭhakathā(Saddhammappakāsinī) · 1026

义注 · 义注 Aṭṭhakathā / 经藏义注 Suttapiṭaka-Aṭṭhakathā / 小部义注 Khuddakanikāya-Aṭṭhakathā · 第 5 / 5 页

or they pervade even a single being by way of complete pervasion; thus, by way of the boundlessness of their pervasion, they are called 'appamaññā'. The four formless attainments are: the attainment of the base of infinite space, the attainment of the base of infinite consciousness, the attainment of the base of nothingness, and the attainment of the base of neither-perception-nor-non-perception. The four analytical knowledges have a meaning that has already been stated.
