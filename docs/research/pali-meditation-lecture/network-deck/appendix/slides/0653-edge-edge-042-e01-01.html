正勤神足根力 → 心神足

关系证据 · 正勤神足根力 → 心神足 · 引文 1/1 · 关系术语：心、citta

Pāli
‘‘Katamañca, bhikkhave, ajjhattaṃ saṃkhittaṃ cittaṃ? Yaṃ, bhikkhave, cittaṃ thinamiddhasahagataṃ thinamiddhasampayuttaṃ – idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, ajjhattaṃ saṃkhittaṃ cittaṃ.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是内在收缩的心？诸比丘，凡与昏沉睡眠俱行、与昏沉睡眠相应的心，诸比丘，这称为内在收缩的心。”
English
And what, bhikkhus, is the internally constricted mind? That mind, bhikkhus, which is accompanied by sloth and torpor, conjoined with sloth and torpor—this, bhikkhus, is called the internally constricted mind.
