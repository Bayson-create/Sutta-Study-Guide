瞋恚

节点证据 · 瞋恚 · 引文 2/2

Pāli
vāya vepullāya.
简体中文
此，多多如理作意——此即是对于未生起的瞋恚之生起，以及已生起的瞋恚之增长、广大之非食。”
English
the frequent practice of wise attention—this is the non-nutriment for the arising of unarisen ill will, or for the increase and abundance of arisen ill will.
