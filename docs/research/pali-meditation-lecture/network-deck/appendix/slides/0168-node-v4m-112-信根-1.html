信根

节点证据 · 信根 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
480 . ‘‘Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṃ…pe… paññindriyaṃ. Katamañca, bhikkhave, saddhindriyaṃ? Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako saddho hoti, saddahati tathāgatassa bodhiṃ – ‘itipi so bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṃ buddho bhagavā’ti – idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, saddhindriyaṃ.
简体中文
480. “诸比丘，有此五根。是哪五个呢？信根……乃至……慧根。诸比丘，什么是信根呢？诸比丘，于此，圣弟子有信心，他相信如来的觉悟：‘彼世尊是阿罗汉、正自觉者、明行足、善逝、世间解、无上调御丈夫、天人导师、佛陀、世尊。’诸比丘，这被称为信根。”
English
480. Monks, there are these five faculties. Which five? The faculty of faith... the faculty of wisdom. And what, monks, is the faculty of faith? Here, monks, a noble disciple has faith; he places his faith in the Tathāgata’s enlightenment: ‘Indeed, the Blessed One is an arahant, perfectly enlightened, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, the Well-Gone, knower of the world, an unsurpassed trainer of persons to be tamed, teacher of gods and humans, the Enlightened One, the Blessed One.’ This, monks, is called the faculty of faith.
