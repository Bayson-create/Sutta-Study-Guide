Pāli
i satova sato (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.) passasati.
简体中文
坐，正直其身，置念于面前。彼正念于出息，正念于入息。”
English
dwelling, sits down; having folded his legs crosswise, set his body erect, and established mindfulness before him. He, ever mindful, breathes out; ever mindful, he breathes in.
