苦圣谛

节点证据 · 苦圣谛 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
373 . ‘‘Katamañcāvuso, dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ? Jātipi dukkhā, jarāpi dukkhā, maraṇampi dukkhaṃ, sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsāpi dukkhā, yampicchaṃ na labhati tampi dukkhaṃ; saṃkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā.
简体中文
373. “贤友们，什么是苦圣谛？生是苦，老是苦，死是苦，愁、悲、苦、忧、恼亦是苦，所求不得亦是苦；简言之，五取蕴是苦。”
English
373. “And what, friends, is the noble truth of suffering? Birth is suffering, aging is suffering, death is suffering; sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair are suffering; not to get what one wants is also suffering; in short, the five aggregates subject to clinging are suffering.
