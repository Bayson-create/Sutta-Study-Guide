随念入门

节点证据 · 随念入门 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Puna caparaṃ tvaṃ, nandiya, dhammaṃ anussareyyāsi – ‘svākkhāto bhagavatā dhammo sandiṭṭhiko akāliko ehipassiko opaneyyiko paccattaṃ veditabbo viññūhī’ti. Iti kho te, nandiya, dhammaṃ ārabbha ajjhattaṃ sati upaṭṭhāpetabbā.
简体中文
“复次，南迪亚，你应当随念法：‘法是世尊所善说的，是现见的、无时的、来见的、导向的、智者们当各自证知的。’南迪亚，你当如此缘于法，于内令念现起。”
English
“Furthermore, Nandiya, you should recollect the Dhamma: ‘The Dhamma is well-proclaimed by the Blessed One, directly visible, immediate, inviting one to come and see, leading onward, to be personally understood by the wise.’ Thus, Nandiya, mindfulness is to be established internally, with the Dhamma as its object.”
