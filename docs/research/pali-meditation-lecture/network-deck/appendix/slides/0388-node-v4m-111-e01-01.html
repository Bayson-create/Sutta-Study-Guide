观神足

节点证据 · 观神足 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Katamā ca, bhikkhave, atilīnā vīmaṃsā? Yā, bhikkhave, vīmaṃsā kosajjasahagatā kosajjasampayuttā – ayaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, atilīnā vīmaṃsā.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是过于萎靡的考察？诸比丘，凡与懈怠俱行、与懈怠相应的考察，诸比丘，这称为过于萎靡的考察。”
English
And what, bhikkhus, is excessively sluggish investigation? That investigation, bhikkhus, which is accompanied by laziness, conjoined with laziness—this, bhikkhus, is called excessively sluggish investigation.
