不善寻思 → 欲寻

关系证据 · 不善寻思 → 欲寻 · 引文 2/2 · 关系术语：欲寻、kāmavitakka

Pāli
ya sannivāreyya. Taṃ kissa hetu? Passati hi so, bhikkhave, gopālako tatonidānaṃ vadhaṃ vā bandhanaṃ vā jāniṃ vā garahaṃ vā. Evameva kho ahaṃ, bhikkhave, addasaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ ādīnavaṃ okāraṃ saṃkilesaṃ, kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ nekkhamme ānisaṃsaṃ vodānapakkhaṃ.
简体中文
English
of the rainy season, in autumn, who would guard his cows in a field dense with crops. He would strike and prod them from here and there with his stick, restraining and checking them. For what reason is this? Because the cowherd sees that from that cause there could be death, bondage, loss, or blame. In the same way, monks, I saw the danger, degradation, and defilement in unwholesome states, and the benefit and purifying aspect of renunciation in wholesome states.
