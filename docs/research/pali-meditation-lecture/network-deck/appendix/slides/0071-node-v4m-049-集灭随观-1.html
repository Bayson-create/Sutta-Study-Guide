集灭随观

节点证据 · 集灭随观 · 引文 1/2

Pāli
5 . Evaṃ me sutaṃ – ekaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṃ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi – ‘‘bhikkhavo’’ti. ‘‘Bhadante’’ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṃ. Bhagavā etadavoca – ‘‘samādhiṃ, bhikkhave, bhāvetha; samāhito, bhikkhave, bhikkhu yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti. Kiñca yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti? Rūpassa samudayañca atthaṅgamañca, vedanāya samudayañca atthaṅgamañca , saññāya samudayañca atthaṅgamañca, saṅkhārānaṃ samudayañca atthaṅgamañca, viññāṇassa samudayañca atthaṅgamañca’’.
简体中文
5. 如是我闻：一时，世尊住在舍卫城祇陀林给孤独园。当时，世尊对比丘们说：‘诸比丘！’‘尊者！’那些比丘回答世尊。世尊说：‘诸比丘，当修习定。诸比丘，得定之比丘能如实了知。如实了知什么呢？色之集与灭，受之集与灭，想之集与灭，行之集与灭，识之集与灭。’”
English
5. Thus I have heard. At one time the Blessed One was dwelling in Sāvatthī, in Jeta's Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika's Park. There the Blessed One addressed the bhikkhus: “Bhikkhus!” “Venerable sir,” those bhikkhus replied to the Blessed One. The Blessed One said this: “Bhikkhus, develop concentration. A concentrated bhikkhu, bhikkhus, understands things as they really are. And what does he understand as it really is?
