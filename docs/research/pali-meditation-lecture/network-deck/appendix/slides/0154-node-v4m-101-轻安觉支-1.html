轻安觉支

节点证据 · 轻安觉支 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā passaddhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā passaddhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā? Atthi, bhikkhave , kāyapassaddhi, cittapassaddhi. Tattha yonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā passaddhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā passaddhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā.
简体中文
诸比丘，何为令未生之轻安觉支生起，已生之轻安觉支修习圆满之食？诸比丘，有身轻安(kāyapassaddhi)、心轻安(cittapassaddhi)。于此，多作如理作意——此为令未生之轻安觉支生起，已生之轻安觉支修习圆满之食。
English
And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of tranquility, or for the fulfillment through development of the arisen enlightenment factor of tranquility? There are, monks, tranquility of body and tranquility of mind. Therein, frequent wise attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of tranquility, or for the fulfillment through development of the arisen enlightenment factor of tranquility.
