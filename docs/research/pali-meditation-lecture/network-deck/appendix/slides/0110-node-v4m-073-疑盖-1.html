节点证据 · 疑盖 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannāya vā vicikicchāya uppādāya, uppannāya vā vicikicchāya bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya? Atthi, bhikkhave, vicikicchāṭṭhānīyā dhammā. Tattha ayonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannāya vā vicikicchāya uppādāya, uppannāya vā vicikicchāya bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是能令未生起的疑生起，或令已生起的疑增长、广大的食？诸比丘，有作为疑之根据的诸法。于彼，多作不如理作意——此即是能令未生起的疑生起，或令已生起的疑增长、广大的食。”
English
“And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen doubt, and for the increase and expansion of arisen doubt? There are, monks, things that are a basis for doubt. In regard to them, frequent improper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen doubt, and for the increase and expansion of arisen doubt.
