掉举与悔

节点证据 · 掉举与悔 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā uddhaccakukkuccassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā uddhaccakukkuccassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya? Atthi, bhikkhave, cetaso avūpasamo. Tattha ayonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā uddhaccakukkuccassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā uddhaccakukkuccassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是未生起的掉举恶作（uddhaccakukkucca）生起，已生起的掉举恶作增长、广大的食呢？诸比丘，有心的不寂静（cetaso avūpasama）。于彼，多修不如理作意——此即是未生起的掉举恶作生起，已生起的掉举恶作增长、广大的食。”
English
“And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen restlessness and remorse, and for the increase and expansion of arisen restlessness and remorse? There is, monks, an unquieted state of mind. In regard to that, frequent improper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen restlessness and remorse, and for the increase and expansion of arisen restlessness and remorse.
