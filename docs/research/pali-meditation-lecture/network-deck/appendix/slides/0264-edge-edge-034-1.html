七觉支成熟 → 定觉支

关系证据 · 七觉支成熟 → 定觉支 · 引文 1/1 · 关系术语：定觉支、samādhi

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā? Atthi, bhikkhave, samathanimittaṃ abyagganimittaṃ. Tattha yonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是令未生起的定觉支得以生起，令已生起的定觉支修习圆满的食呢？诸比丘，有止相、不乱相。于此诸相多作如理作意——此即是令未生起的定觉支得以生起，令已生起的定觉支修习圆满的食。”
English
“And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of concentration, and for the development and fulfillment of the arisen enlightenment factor of concentration? There are, monks, the sign of calm and the sign of non-distraction. In regard to them, frequent proper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of concentration, and for the development and fulfillment of the arisen enlightenment factor of concentration.
