七觉支成熟 → 舍觉支

关系证据 · 七觉支成熟 → 舍觉支 · 引文 1/1 · 关系术语：舍觉支、upekkhā

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā upekkhāsambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā upekkhāsambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā? Atthi, bhikkhave, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgaṭṭhānīyā dhammā. Tattha yonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā upekkhāsambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā upekkhāsambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是令未生起的舍觉支得以生起，令已生起的舍觉支修习圆满的食呢？诸比丘，有舍觉支的所缘法。于彼法多作如理作意——此即是令未生起的舍觉支得以生起，令已生起的舍觉支修习圆满的食。”
English
And what, Bhikkhus, is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of equanimity, or for the fulfillment through development of the arisen enlightenment factor of equanimity? There are, Bhikkhus, states that are a basis for the enlightenment factor of equanimity. Therein, the frequent practice of wise attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of equanimity, or for the fulfillment through development of the arisen enlightenment factor of equanimity.
