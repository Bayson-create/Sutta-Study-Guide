精进神足

节点证据 · 精进神足 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Katamañca, bhikkhave, bahiddhā vikkhittaṃ vīriyaṃ? Yaṃ, bhikkhave, vīriyaṃ bahiddhā pañca kāmaguṇe ārabbha anuvikkhittaṃ anuvisaṭaṃ – idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, bahiddhā vikkhittaṃ vīriyaṃ…pe….
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是向外散乱的精进？诸比丘，凡是缘于外在的五种欲而散乱、流散的精进，诸比丘，这称为向外散乱的精进……乃至……”
English
And what, bhikkhus, is externally scattered energy? That energy, bhikkhus, which is scattered forth and spread out externally with regard to the five strands of sensual pleasure—this, bhikkhus, is called externally scattered energy... and so on...
