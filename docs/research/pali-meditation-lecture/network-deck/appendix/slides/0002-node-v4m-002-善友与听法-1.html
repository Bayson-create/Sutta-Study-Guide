善友与听法

节点证据 · 善友与听法 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Kalyāṇamittassetaṃ , meghiya, bhikkhuno pāṭikaṅkhaṃ kalyāṇasahāyassa kalyāṇasampavaṅkassa yaṃ sīlavā bhavissati, pātimokkhasaṃvarasaṃvuto viharissati, ācāragocarasampanno, aṇumattesu vajjesu bhayadassāvī, samādāya sikkhissati sikkhāpadesu.
简体中文
‘梅吉亚，对于有善友、善伴、善亲近者的比丘，可以期待的是：他将具足戒德，住于守护波罗提木叉律仪，具足正行与行处，于微细过失中见其危险，受持诸学处而修学。’
English
Meghiya, this is to be expected of a bhikkhu who has good friends, good companions, and good intimates: that he will be virtuous, will dwell restrained by the restraint of the Pātimokkha, will be accomplished in conduct and resort, will see danger in the slightest faults, and, having undertaken the training rules, will train in them.
