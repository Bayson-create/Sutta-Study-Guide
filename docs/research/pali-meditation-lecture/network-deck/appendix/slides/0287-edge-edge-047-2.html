正勤神足根力 → 定根

关系证据 · 正勤神足根力 → 定根 · 引文 2/2 · 关系术语：定根、samādhindriya

Pāli
aṃ āgamma tassā nadiyā eko soto tveva saṅkhyaṃ gacchati saṅkhaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ.) . Atthi pana, bhikkhave, pariyāyo yaṃ pariyāyaṃ āgamma tassā nadiyā dve sotāni tveva saṅkhyaṃ gacchanti.
简体中文
English
n; whatever is the power of concentration, that is the faculty of concentration. Whatever is the faculty of wisdom, that is the power of wisdom; whatever is the power of wisdom, that is the faculty of wisdom. Just as, monks, a river slopes to the east, inclines to the east, tends towards the east, and in its middle there is an island. There is, monks, a way, relying on which that river is reckoned as just one stream. And there is, monks, a way, relying on which that river is reckoned as two streams.
