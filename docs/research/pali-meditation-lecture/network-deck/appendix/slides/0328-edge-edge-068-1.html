调节精进 → 精进觉支

关系证据 · 调节精进 → 精进觉支 · 引文 1/1 · 关系术语：līnaṃ、viriyasambojjhaṅga

Pāli
‘‘Yasmiñca kho, bhikkhave, samaye līnaṃ cittaṃ hoti, kālo tasmiṃ samaye dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya, kālo vīriyasambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya, kālo pītisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya. Taṃ kissa hetu? Līnaṃ, bhikkhave, cittaṃ taṃ etehi dhammehi susamuṭṭhāpayaṃ hoti.
简体中文
诸比丘，当心消沉之时，那时适宜修习择法觉支，适宜修习精进觉支，适宜修习喜觉支。何以故？诸比丘，消沉之心易由此等法振作。
English
“And, bhikkhus, at a time when the mind is sluggish, it is the right time for the cultivation of the investigation-of-phenomena factor of awakening, the right time for the cultivation of the energy factor of awakening, and the right time for the cultivation of the rapture factor of awakening. For what reason? Bhikkhus, a sluggish mind is easy to arouse by means of these states.”
