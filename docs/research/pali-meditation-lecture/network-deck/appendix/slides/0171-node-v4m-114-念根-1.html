念根

节点证据 · 念根 · 引文 1/2

Pāli
‘‘Katamañca , bhikkhave, satindriyaṃ? Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako satimā hoti paramena satinepakkena samannāgato, cirakatampi cirabhāsitampi saritā anussaritā. So kāye kāyānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṃ; vedanāsu…pe… citte…pe… dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṃ – idaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, satindriyaṃ.
简体中文
诸位比丘，什么是念根？诸位比丘，于此，圣弟子具念，具备最上的念与慧，能忆持、随忆长久以前所作、长久以前所说之事。他于身随观身而住，热勤、正知、具念，调伏世间的贪与忧；于受……于心……于法随观法而住，热勤、正知、具念，调伏世间的贪与忧。诸位比丘，这被称为念根。
English
“And what, Bhikkhus, is the faculty of mindfulness? Here, Bhikkhus, a noble disciple is mindful, endowed with supreme maturity of mindfulness, one who remembers and recollects again and again what was done and said long ago. He dwells contemplating the body in the body, ardent, clearly comprehending, and mindful, having subdued covetousness and displeasure in the world.
