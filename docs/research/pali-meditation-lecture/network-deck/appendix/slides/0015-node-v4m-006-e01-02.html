远离与独处

节点证据 · 远离与独处 · 引文 2/4

Pāli
yā. kaṃ. ka.) upasaṅkamitāro bhikkhū bhikkhuniyo upāsakā upāsikāyo rājāno rājamahāmattā titthiyā titthiyasāvakā. Tatrānanda, tathāgato vivekaninneneva cittena vivekapoṇena vivekapabbhārena vūpakaṭṭhena nekkhammābhiratena byantībhūtena sabbaso āsavaṭṭhānīyehi dhammehi añ
简体中文
婆夷、国王、王之大臣、外道师、外道师弟子前来，阿难，于此，如来唯以倾向远离、趣向远离、倾于远离、独处、乐于出离、已然远离一切
English
y, there are visitors—monks, nuns, laymen, laywomen, kings, royal ministers, sectarians, or disciples of sectarians—then, Ānanda, the Tathāgata, with a mind inclined to seclusion, tending to seclusion, gravitat
