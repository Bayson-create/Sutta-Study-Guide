节点证据 · 五盖现前 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
232 . Sāvatthinidānaṃ . ‘‘Pañcannañca, bhikkhave, nīvaraṇānaṃ sattannañca bojjhaṅgānaṃ āhārañca anāhārañca desessāmi; taṃ suṇātha. Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā kāmacchandassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā kāmacchandassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya? Atthi, bhikkhave , subhanimittaṃ. Tattha ayonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā kāmacchandassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā kāmacchandassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
232. 舍卫城因缘。“诸比丘，我将为你们宣说五盖（nīvaraṇa）与七觉支的食与非食，且谛听。诸比丘，什么是未生起的欲贪（kāmacchanda）生起，已生起的欲贪增长、广大的食呢？诸比丘，有净相（subhanimitta）。于彼，多修不如理作意（ayonisomanasikāra）——此即是未生起的欲贪生起，已生起的欲贪增长、广大的食。”
English
232. At Sāvatthī. “Monks, I will teach the nutriment and non-nutriment for the five hindrances and the seven factors of enlightenment. Listen to that. And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen sensual desire, and for the increase and expansion of arisen sensual desire? There is, monks, the sign of the beautiful. In regard to that, frequent improper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen sensual desire, and for the increase and expansion of arisen sensual desire.
