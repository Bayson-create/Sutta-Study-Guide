第四禅

节点证据 · 第四禅 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
232 . ‘‘Puna caparaṃ, mahārāja, bhikkhu sukhassa ca pahānā dukkhassa ca pahānā, pubbeva somanassadomanassānaṃ atthaṅgamā adukkhamasukhaṃ upekkhāsatipārisuddhiṃ catutthaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati, so imameva kāyaṃ parisuddhena cetasā pariyodātena pharitvā nisinno hoti, nāssa kiñci sabbāvato kāyassa parisuddhena cetasā pariyodātena apphuṭaṃ hoti.
简体中文
232. 再者，大王，比丘由于舍断乐与舍断苦，以及先前喜悦与忧恼的灭尽，进入并安住于不苦不乐、由舍而念清净的第四禅。他以清净、明亮的心遍满此身而坐，其全身无一处不为清净、明亮的心所遍满。
English
232. Furthermore, great king, a monk, with the abandoning of pleasure and pain, and with the previous disappearance of joy and sorrow, enters and abides in the fourth jhāna, which is neither-painful-nor-pleasant and is characterized by purity of mindfulness due to equanimity. He sits having pervaded this very body with a pure, bright mind, so that there is no part of his entire body unpervaded by the pure, bright mind.
