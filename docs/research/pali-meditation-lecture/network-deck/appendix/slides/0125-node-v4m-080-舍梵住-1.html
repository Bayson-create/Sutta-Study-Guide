舍梵住

节点证据 · 舍梵住 · 引文 1/3

Pāli
‘‘Samaṇo, āvuso, gotamo sāvakānaṃ evaṃ dhammaṃ deseti – ‘etha tumhe, bhikkhave, pañca nīvaraṇe pahāya cetaso upakkilese paññāya dubbalīkaraṇe mettāsahagatena cetasā ekaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharatha, tathā dutiyaṃ, tathā tatiyaṃ, tathā catutthaṃ; iti uddhamadho tiriyaṃ sabbadhi sabbattatāya sabbāvantaṃ lokaṃ mettāsahagatena cetasā vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇena averena abyāpajjena pharitvā viharatha. Karuṇāsahagatena cetasā ekaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharatha, tathā dutiyaṃ, tathā tatiyaṃ, tathā catutthaṃ; iti uddhamadho tiriyaṃ sabbadhi sabbattatāya sabbāvantaṃ lokaṃ karuṇāsahagatena cetasā vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇen
简体中文
“贤友们，沙门乔达摩对弟子们如是说法：‘来，比丘们，你们当舍断心的随烦恼、令慧羸弱的五盖（pañca nīvaraṇe），以慈俱心（mettāsahagatena cetasā）遍满一方而住；如是第二方，如是第三方，如是第四方；如是，上、下、横，于一切处，视一切如己，以广大、崇高、无量、无怨、无害的慈俱心，遍满这包含一切的世界而住。以悲俱心（karuṇāsahagatena cetasā）遍满一方而住；如是第二方，如是第三方，如是第四方；如是，上、下、横，于一切处，视一切如己，以广大、崇高、无量、无怨、无害的悲俱心，遍满这包含一切的世界而住。以喜俱心（muditāsahagatena cetasā）遍满一方而住；如是第二方，如是第三方，如是第四方；如是，上、下、横，于一切处，视一切如己，以广大、崇高、无量、无怨、无害的喜俱心，遍满这包含一切的世界而住。以舍俱心（upekkhāsahagatena cetasā）遍满一方而住；如是第二方，如是第三方，如是第四方；如是，上、下、横，于一切处，视一切如己，以广大、崇高、无量、无怨、无害的舍俱心，遍满这包含一切的世界而住。’”
English
“Friends, the ascetic Gotama teaches the Dhamma to his disciples thus: ‘Come, monks, having abandoned the five hindrances, which are defilements of the mind and cause the weakening of wisdom, dwell pervading one direction with a mind accompanied by loving-kindness; likewise the second, likewise the third, likewise the fourth. Thus, above, below, and across, everywhere, for all as for oneself, dwell pervading the entire world with a mind accompanied by loving-kindness—vast, exalted, immeasurable, without enmity, and without ill will.
