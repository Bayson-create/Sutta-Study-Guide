简体中文
是故，阿难，那时比丘于诸受随观受而住，精勤、正知、具念，调伏世间的贪欲与忧恼。”

ul, having removed covetousness and displeasure in regard to the world. For what reason? For I say, Ānanda, that this is a certain kind of feeling, that is, the proper attention to in-breaths and out-breaths. T
