法念处 → 七觉支成熟

关系证据 · 法念处 → 七觉支成熟 · 引文 1/2 · 关系术语：sattasu bojjhaṅgesu

Pāli
‘‘Iti ajjhattaṃ vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati, bahiddhā vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati, ajjhattabahiddhā vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati. Samudayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati, vayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati, samudayavayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati ‘atthi dhammā’ti vā panassa sati paccupaṭṭhitā hoti yāvadeva ñāṇamattāya paṭissatimattāya anissito ca viharati, na ca kiñci loke upādiyati. Evampi kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati sattasu bojjhaṅgesu.
简体中文
如是，或于内法随观法而住，或于外法随观法而住，或于内外法随观法而住。或于诸法随观集法而住，或于诸法随观灭法而住，或于诸法随观集灭法而住。又，彼‘有法’之念现前，仅为增长智与念故。彼无所依而住，且不执取世间任何事物。诸比丘，比丘即如是于七觉支诸法中随观法而住。
English
Thus he dwells contemplating phenomena in phenomena internally, or he dwells contemplating phenomena in phenomena externally, or he dwells contemplating phenomena in phenomena both internally and externally. He dwells contemplating the nature of arising in phenomena, or he dwells contemplating the nature of passing away in phenomena, or he dwells contemplating the nature of arising and passing away in phenomena.
