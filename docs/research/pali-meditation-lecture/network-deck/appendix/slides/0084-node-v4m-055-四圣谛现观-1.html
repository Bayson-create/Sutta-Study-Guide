四圣谛现观

节点证据 · 四圣谛现观 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Yāvakīvañca me, bhikkhave, imesu catūsu ariyasaccesu evaṃ tiparivaṭṭaṃ dvādasākāraṃ yathābhūtaṃ ñāṇadassanaṃ na suvisuddhaṃ ahosi, neva tāvāhaṃ, bhikkhave , sadevake loke samārake sabrahmake sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya ‘anuttaraṃ sammāsambodhiṃ abhisambuddho’ti paccaññāsiṃ abhisambuddho paccaññāsiṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ.) .
简体中文
“诸比丘，只要我于此四圣谛，如此三转、十二行相的如实智见尚未完全清净，诸比丘，我就决不在有天神、有魔罗、有梵天的世间，于有沙门、婆罗门、有天、有人的众生中，宣称我已现正觉无上正等觉。”
English
Bhikkhus, as long as my knowledge and vision of things as they really are regarding these Four Noble Truths—with their three rounds and twelve aspects—was not thoroughly purified, I did not declare to the world with its devas, Māra, and Brahmā, to this generation with its ascetics and brahmins, its devas and humans, that I had fully awakened to the unsurpassed, perfect enlightenment.
