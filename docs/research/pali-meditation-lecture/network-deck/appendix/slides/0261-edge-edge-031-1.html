七觉支成熟 → 精进觉支

关系证据 · 七觉支成熟 → 精进觉支 · 引文 1/1 · 关系术语：精进觉支、viriya

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā vīriyasambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā vīriyasambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā? Atthi, bhikkhave, ārambhadhātu nikkamadhātu parakkamadhātu. Tattha yonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā vīriyasambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā vīriyasambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是能令未生起的精进觉支(vīriyasambojjhaṅga)生起，或令已生起的精进觉支修习圆满的食？诸比丘，有发起界、出离界、勇猛界。于彼，多作如理作意——此即是能令未生起的精进觉支生起，或令已生起的精进觉支修习圆满的食。”
English
“And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of energy, and for the development and fulfillment of the arisen enlightenment factor of energy? There are, monks, the element of initiative, the element of exertion, and the element of striving. In regard to them, frequent proper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of energy, and for the development and fulfillment of the arisen enlightenment factor of energy.
