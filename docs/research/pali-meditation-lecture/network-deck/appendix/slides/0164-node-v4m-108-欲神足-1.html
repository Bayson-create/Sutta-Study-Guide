欲神足

节点证据 · 欲神足 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Katamo ca, bhikkhave, atilīno chando? Yo, bhikkhave, chando kosajjasahagato kosajjasampayutto – ayaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave, atilīno chando.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是过萎靡的欲？诸比丘，凡与懈怠俱行、与懈怠相应之欲，诸比丘，这称为过萎靡的欲。”
English
And what, monks, is desire that is too slack? Whatever desire is accompanied by laziness, associated with laziness—this, monks, is called desire that is too slack.
