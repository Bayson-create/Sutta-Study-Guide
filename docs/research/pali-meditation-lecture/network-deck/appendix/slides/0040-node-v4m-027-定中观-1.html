定中观

节点证据 · 定中观 · 引文 1/2

Pāli
‘‘Seyyathāpi , bhikkhave, issāso vā issāsantevāsī vā tiṇapurisarūpake vā mattikāpuñje vā yoggaṃ karitvā, so aparena samayena dūrepātī ca hoti akkhaṇavedhī ca mahato ca kāyassa padāletā ; evamevaṃ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sukhassa ca pahānā, dukkhassa ca pahānā, pubbeva somanassadomanassānaṃ atthaṅgamā adukkhamasukhaṃ upekkhāsatipārisuddhiṃ catutthaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati. So yadeva tattha hoti rūpagataṃ vedanāgataṃ…pe… anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā. ‘Catutthampāhaṃ , bhikkhave, jhānaṃ nissāya āsavānaṃ khayaṃ vadāmī’ti, iti yaṃ taṃ vuttaṃ, idametaṃ paṭicca vuttaṃ.
简体中文
“诸比丘，譬如弓箭手或其弟子，在草人或土堆上练习，经过一段时间后，他能远射、箭无虚发，且能射裂巨大的身躯；同样地，诸比丘，比丘因舍离乐、舍离苦，以及先前已灭除喜和忧，进入并安住于不苦不乐、由舍而念清净的第四禅。他在那（禅定）中，凡有任何色法、受法……他即从那个世界成为不还者。‘诸比丘，我依止第四禅而说诸漏的灭尽’，此话就是依此而说的。”
English
Suppose, bhikkhus, an archer or an archer’s apprentice, having practiced on a straw man or a clay mound, at a later time becomes a long-range shooter, a swift piercer, and a splitter of a great body. So too, bhikkhus, with the abandoning of pleasure and pain, and with the previous disappearance of joy and displeasure, a bhikkhu enters and abides in the fourth jhāna, which is neither-painful-nor-pleasant and has purity of mindfulness due to equanimity.
