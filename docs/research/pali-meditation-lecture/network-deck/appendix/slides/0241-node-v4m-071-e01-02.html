昏沉与睡眠

节点证据 · 昏沉与睡眠 · 引文 2/3

Pāli
ssa uppādāya, uppannassa vā thinamiddhassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
、呵欠（vijambhitā）、饭后昏沉（bhattasammada）与心的萎靡（cetaso līnatta）。于彼，多修不如理作意——此即是未生起的昏沉睡眠生起
English
meal, and sluggishness of mind. In regard to these, frequent improper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen sloth and torpor, and for the increase and expansion of arisen sloth and torpor
