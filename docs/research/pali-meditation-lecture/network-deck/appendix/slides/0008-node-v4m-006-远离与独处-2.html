远离与独处

节点证据 · 远离与独处 · 引文 2/2

Pāli
cepi ākaṅkheyya – ‘ajjhattaṃ suññataṃ upasampajja vihareyya’nti, tenānanda, bhikkhunā ajjhattameva cittaṃ saṇṭhapetabbaṃ sannisādetabbaṃ ekodi kātabbaṃ samādahātabbaṃ.
简体中文
English
, Ānanda, if a monk should wish, ‘May I enter and dwell in internal emptiness,’ then that monk should internally establish the mind, settle it, unify it, and concentrate it.
