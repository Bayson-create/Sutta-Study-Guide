不还法（从彼世间不复还）

节点证据 · 不还法（从彼世间不复还） · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Puna caparaṃ, ānanda, bhikkhu sabbaso rūpasaññānaṃ samatikkamā paṭighasaññānaṃ atthaṅgamā nānattasaññānaṃ amanasikārā ‘ananto ākāso’ti ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ upasampajja viharati. So yadeva tattha hoti vedanāgataṃ saññāgataṃ saṅkhāragataṃ viññāṇagataṃ…pe… anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā. Ayampi kho, ānanda, maggo ayaṃ paṭipadā pañcannaṃ orambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyojanānaṃ pahānāya.
简体中文
复次，阿难，比丘遍超一切色想，灭有对想，不作意种种想，思惟‘虚空无边’，具足空无边处而住。彼于其处，凡所有受、想、行、识……从彼世间，为不还法。阿难，此亦是道，此亦是行道，为断五下分结。
English
“Furthermore, Ānanda, a bhikkhu, with the complete transcending of perceptions of form, with the cessation of perceptions of resistance, and by not attending to perceptions of diversity, aware that ‘space is infinite,’ enters and dwells in the base of the infinity of space. He regards whatever phenomena there that pertain to feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness… he is of the nature of not returning from that world. This too, Ānanda, is indeed the path, this is the practice for the abandoning of the five lower fetters.”
