缘起观 → 集灭随观

关系证据 · 缘起观 → 集灭随观 · 引文 2/2 · 关系术语：samudaya、nirodha

Pāli
? ‘‘No hetaṃ, bhante’’. ‘‘Tasmātihānanda, eseva hetu etaṃ nidānaṃ esa samudayo esa paccayo jarāmaraṇassa, yadidaṃ jāti’’.
简体中文
English
for the state of being a reptile—if, Ānanda, for those various beings there were no birth into their respective states. With birth being completely absent, from the cessation of birth, would aging and death be discerned?” “That is not so, venerable sir.” “Therefore, Ānanda, this is the cause, this the source, this the origin, this the condition for aging and death, namely, birth.”
