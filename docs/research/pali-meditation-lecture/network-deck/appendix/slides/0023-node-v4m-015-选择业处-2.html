选择业处

节点证据 · 选择业处 · 引文 2/2

Pāli
taṃ samādhiyati. Ayaṃ vuccati, mahānāma – ‘ariyasāvako visamagatāya pajāya samappatto viharati, sabyāpajjāya pajāya abyāpajjo viharati, dhammasotaṃ samāpanno sīlānussatiṃ bhāveti’’’.
简体中文
English
th a rapturous mind, the body becomes tranquil. One tranquil in body feels pleasure. The mind of one who is pleased becomes concentrated. This, Mahānāma, is called: ‘a noble disciple who dwells peacefully among a turbulent populace, who dwells without affliction among an afflicted populace, who has entered the stream of the Dhamma and develops the recollection of virtues.’”
