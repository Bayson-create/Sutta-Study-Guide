发心与正见

节点证据 · 发心与正见 · 引文 1/2

Pāli
‘‘Puna caparaṃ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sukhassa ca pahānā dukkhassa ca pahānā pubbeva somanassadomanassānaṃ atthaṅgamā adukkhamasukhaṃ upekkhāsatipārisuddhiṃ catutthaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati. Ayaṃ vuccati, bhikkhave…pe… pāpimato.
简体中文
“再者，诸比丘，比丘舍乐、舍苦，并且先前已灭喜、忧，进入并安住于不苦不乐、舍念清净的第四禅。诸比丘，此比丘被称为已使魔罗盲目，
English
“Furthermore, monks, a monk, with the abandoning of pleasure and pain, and with the earlier disappearance of joy and sorrow, enters and dwells in the fourth jhāna, which has neither-pain-nor-pleasure and purity
