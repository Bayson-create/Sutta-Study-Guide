体验心

节点证据 · 体验心 · 引文 1/2

Pāli
‘‘Yasmiṃ samaye, ānanda, bhikkhu ‘cittappaṭisaṃvedī assasissāmī’ti sikkhati, ‘cittappaṭisaṃvedī passasissāmī’ti sikkhati; abhippamodayaṃ cittaṃ…pe… samādahaṃ cittaṃ…pe… ‘vimocayaṃ cittaṃ assasissāmī’ti sikkhati, ‘vimocayaṃ cittaṃ passasissāmī’ti sikkhati – citte cittānupassī, ānanda, bhikkhu tasmiṃ samaye viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṃ. Taṃ kissa hetu? Nāhaṃ, ānanda, muṭṭhassatissa asampajānassa ānāpānassatisamādhibhāvanaṃ vadāmi. Tasmātihānanda, citte cittānupassī bhikkhu tasmiṃ samaye viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṃ.
简体中文
“阿难，当比丘学‘我将体验心而出息’，学‘我将体验心而入息’；令心喜悦……乃至……令心入定……乃至……学‘我将令心解脱而出息’，学‘我将令心解脱而入息’时——阿难，那时比丘于心随观心而住，精勤、正知、具念，调伏世间的贪欲与忧恼。此是何因？阿难，对失念、不正知者，我不说有入出息念定的修习。是故，阿难，那时比丘于心随观心而住，精勤、正知、具念，调伏世间的贪欲与忧恼。”
English
“Ānanda, at a time when a bhikkhu trains, thinking: ‘I will breathe out experiencing the mind,’ and ‘I will breathe in experiencing the mind’; … ‘I will breathe out gladdening the mind,’ … ‘I will breathe out concentrating the mind,’ … ‘I will breathe out liberating the mind,’ and ‘I will breathe in liberating the mind’—at that time, Ānanda, a bhikkhu dwells contemplating the mind in the mind, ardent, clearly comprehending, and mindful, having removed covetousness and displeasure in regard to the world. For what reason? I do not say, Ānanda
