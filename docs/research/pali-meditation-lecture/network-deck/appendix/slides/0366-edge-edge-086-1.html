三相深入 → 厌离

关系证据 · 三相深入 → 厌离 · 引文 1/2 · 关系术语：aniccato、厌离

Pāli
‘‘‘Paṭhamampāhaṃ, bhikkhave, jhānaṃ nissāya āsavānaṃ khayaṃ vadāmī’ti, iti kho panetaṃ vuttaṃ. Kiñcetaṃ paṭicca vuttaṃ? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu vivicceva kāmehi…pe… paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati. So yadeva tattha hoti rūpagataṃ vedanāgataṃ saññāgataṃ saṅkhāragataṃ viññāṇagataṃ, te dhamme aniccato dukkhato rogato gaṇḍato sallato aghato ābādhato parato palokato suññato anattato samanupassati. So tehi dhammehi cittaṃ paṭivāpeti patiṭṭhāpeti (syā.), paṭipādeti (ka.) ma. ni. 2.133 passitabbaṃ . So tehi dhammehi cittaṃ paṭivāpetvā patiṭṭhāpetvā (syā.), paṭipādetvā (ka.) amatāya dhātuyā cittaṃ upasaṃharati – ‘et
简体中文
“‘诸比丘，我说依初禅亦灭尽诸漏’，此话是如此说的。此话是依何而说呢？诸比丘，于此，比丘远离诸欲……成就初禅而住。凡于彼处所有色、受、想、行、识等法，他皆随观为无常、苦、病、痈、箭、祸、疾、他、坏、空、无我。他使心厌离彼等诸法。他使心厌离彼等诸法后，便将心导向不死界：‘此是寂静，此是殊胜，即一切行之止息、一切依之舍离、爱尽、离贪、灭、涅槃。’他安住于此，证得诸漏尽。若未证得诸漏尽，则因对彼法之爱染、对彼法之欢喜，五下分结断尽故，成为化生者，于彼处般涅槃，自彼世界不复还。”
English
‘Bhikkhus, I declare the destruction of the taints by relying on the first jhāna.’ This was said. In reference to what was it said? Here, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu, quite secluded from sensual pleasures… and so on… enters and dwells in the first jhāna. He regards whatever phenomena are found there connected with form, feeling, perception, volitional formations, and consciousness as impermanent, as suffering, as a disease, as a boil, as a dart, as misery, as an affliction, as alien, as disintegrating, as empty, as non-self. He turns his mind away
