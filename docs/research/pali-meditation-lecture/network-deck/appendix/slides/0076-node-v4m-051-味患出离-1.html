味患出离

节点证据 · 味患出离 · 引文 1/2

Pāli
‘‘Ye hi keci, bhikkhave, samaṇā vā brāhmaṇā vā evaṃ viññāṇaṃ abhiññāya, evaṃ viññāṇasamudayaṃ abhiññāya, evaṃ viññāṇanirodhaṃ abhiññāya, evaṃ viññāṇanirodhagāminiṃ paṭipadaṃ abhiññāya; evaṃ viññāṇassa assādaṃ abhiññāya, evaṃ viññāṇassa ādīnavaṃ abhiññāya, evaṃ viññāṇassa nissaraṇaṃ abhiññāya viññāṇassa nibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya paṭipannā, te suppaṭipannā. Ye suppaṭipannā, te imasmiṃ dhammavinaye gādhanti.
简体中文
“诸比丘，凡是任何沙门或婆罗门，如是了知识，如是了知识的生起，如是了知识的灭尽，如是了知导向识灭尽之道；如是了知识的乐味，如是了知识的过患，如是了知识的出离，为了对识厌离、离贪、灭尽而修行，他们是善修行者。凡是善修行者，就在此法与律中得以确立。
English
“Bhikkhus, whatever ascetics or brahmins who, having directly known consciousness in this way, having directly known the origin of consciousness, having directly known the cessation of consciousness, having directly known the way leading to the cessation of consciousness; having directly known the gratification of consciousness, having directly known the danger of consciousness, having directly known the escape from consciousness, are practicing for disenchantment, dispassion, and cessation regarding consciousness—they are practicing well.
