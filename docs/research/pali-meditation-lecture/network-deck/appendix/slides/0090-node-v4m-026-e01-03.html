禅那熟练

节点证据 · 禅那熟练 · 引文 3/5

Pāli
haṭṭho ubhato parihīno, seyyathāpi sā gāvī pabbateyyā bālā abyattā akhettaññū akusalā visame pabbate carituṃ’’’.
简体中文
乃至……证入初禅而住。诸比丘，此比丘被称为‘两头皆失，两头皆损’，犹如那头山牛，愚笨、不熟练、不识牧场、不善于在崎岖的山上行走。
English
quite secluded from sensual pleasures, secluded from unwholesome states… I were to enter and dwell in the first jhāna?' He is not able to, quite secluded from sensual pleasures… enter and dwell in the first jhā
