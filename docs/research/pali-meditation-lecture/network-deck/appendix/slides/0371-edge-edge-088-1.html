离贪 → 灭

关系证据 · 离贪 → 灭 · 引文 1/1 · 关系术语：virāgo、nirodho

Pāli
‘‘Katamañcāvuso, dukkhanirodhaṃ dukkhanirodho (syā. kaṃ.) ariyasaccaṃ? Yo tassāyeva taṇhāya asesavirāganirodho cāgo paṭinissaggo mutti anālayo, idaṃ vuccatāvuso – ‘dukkhanirodhaṃ dukkhanirodho (syā. kaṃ.) ariyasaccaṃ’’’.
简体中文
贤友，云何为苦灭圣谛？即于彼爱无余离贪而灭，舍、遣、解脱、无执。贤友，此谓‘苦灭圣谛’。
English
“And what, friends, is the Noble Truth of the Cessation of Suffering? It is the remainderless fading away and cessation of that very craving, its relinquishment, its renunciation, its release, its non-attachment. This, friends, is called the Noble Truth of the Cessation of Suffering.”
