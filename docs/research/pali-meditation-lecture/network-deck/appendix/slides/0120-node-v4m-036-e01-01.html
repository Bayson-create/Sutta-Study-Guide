受念处

节点证据 · 受念处 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
250 . ‘‘Tisso imā, bhikkhave, vedanā. Katamā tisso? Sukhā vedanā, dukkhā vedanā, adukkhamasukhā vedanā – imā kho, bhikkhave, tisso vedanāti.
简体中文
250. “诸比丘，有此三种受。是哪三种呢？乐受、苦受、不苦不乐受。诸比丘，此即是三种受。”
English
250. “Bhikkhus, there are these three feelings. What are the three? Pleasant feeling, painful feeling, and neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. These, bhikkhus, are the three feelings.”
