阿罗汉果

节点证据 · 阿罗汉果 · 引文 2/4

Pāli
manussaṃ sayaṃ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedeti. So dhammaṃ deseti ādikalyāṇaṃ majjhekalyāṇaṃ pariyosānakalyāṇaṃ sātthaṃ sabyañjanaṃ, kevalaparipuṇṇaṃ parisuddhaṃ brahmacariyaṃ pakāseti.
简体中文
含天、魔、梵的世界，以及包含沙门、婆罗门、天、人的众生，由自己证知、作证后而宣说。他所宣说的法，初善、中善、后善，义理圆满，
English
n Unsurpassed Trainer of persons to be tamed, a Teacher of gods and humans, an Enlightened One, a Blessed One. He, having realized it for himself through direct knowledge, makes known this world with its devas,
