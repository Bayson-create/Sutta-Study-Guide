空无边处

节点证据 · 空无边处 · 引文 1/2

Pāli
337 . ‘‘‘Viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ, viññāṇañcāyatana’nti vuccati. Katamaṃ nu kho viññāṇañcāyatananti? Tassa mayhaṃ, āvuso, etadahosi – ‘idha bhikkhu sabbaso ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ samatikkamma anantaṃ viññāṇanti viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ upasampajja viharati. Idaṃ vuccati viññāṇañcāyatana’nti. So khvāhaṃ, āvuso, sabbaso ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ samatikkamma anantaṃ viññāṇanti viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ upasampajja viharāmi . Tassa mayhaṃ, āvuso, iminā vihārena viharato ākāsānañcāyatanasahagatā saññāmanasikārā samudācaranti.
简体中文
337. “‘识无边处，识无边处’，如是说。那么，什么是识无边处呢？贤友，我曾作是念：‘在此，比丘完全超越空无边处，思惟“识无边”，证入并安住于识无边处。这被称为识无边处。’贤友，我确实完全超越空无边处，思惟‘识无边’，证入并安住于识无边处。贤友，我如是住时，与空无边处相应的想与作意生起。”
English
337. “‘The sphere of infinite consciousness, the sphere of infinite consciousness,’ it is said. What, then, is the sphere of infinite consciousness? Then, friends, this thought occurred to me: ‘Here, a bhikkhu, having entirely transcended the sphere of infinite space, (perceiving) “Consciousness is infinite,” enters and abides in the sphere of infinite consciousness.
