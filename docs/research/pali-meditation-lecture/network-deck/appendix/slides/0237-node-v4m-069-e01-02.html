欲贪

节点证据 · 欲贪 · 引文 2/2

Pāli
dassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
意——此即是对于未生起的欲贪之生起，以及已生起的欲贪之增长、广大之非食。”
English
ractice of wise attention—this is the non-nutriment for the arising of unarisen sensual desire, or for the increase and abundance of arisen sensual desire.
