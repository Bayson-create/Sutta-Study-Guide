戒清净

节点证据 · 戒清净 · 引文 2/2

Pāli
aññū (ka.) , satisampajaññena samannāgato, santuṭṭho.
简体中文
行具足，守护诸根门，具足念与正知，知足。
English
dowed with wholesome bodily and verbal action, has a purified livelihood, is perfected in virtue, has guarded doors in his sense faculties, is endowed with mindfulness and clear comprehension, and is content.
