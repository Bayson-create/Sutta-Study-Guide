受想灭定

节点证据 · 受想灭定 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Kathaṃ panāyye, saññāvedayitanirodhasamāpattiyā vuṭṭhānaṃ hotī’’ti?
简体中文
“尊者，那么，从想受灭定的出定是如何发生的呢？”
English
“But how, venerable lady, does emergence from the attainment of the cessation of perception and feeling come about?”
