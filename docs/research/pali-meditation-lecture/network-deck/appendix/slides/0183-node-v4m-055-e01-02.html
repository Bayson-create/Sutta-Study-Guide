四圣谛现观

节点证据 · 四圣谛现观 · 引文 2/2

Pāli
uddho’ti paccaññāsiṃ abhisambuddho paccaññāsiṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ.) .
简体中文
、婆罗门、有天、有人的众生中，宣称我已现正觉无上正等觉。”
English
with its devas, Māra, and Brahmā, to this generation with its ascetics and brahmins, its devas and humans, that I had fully awakened to the unsurpassed, perfect enlightenment.
