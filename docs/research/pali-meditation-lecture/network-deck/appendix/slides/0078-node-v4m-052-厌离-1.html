厌离

节点证据 · 厌离 · 引文 1/3

Pāli
‘‘Evameva kho, bhikkhave, avijjūpanisā saṅkhārā, saṅkhārūpanisaṃ viññāṇaṃ, viññāṇūpanisaṃ nāmarūpaṃ, nāmarūpūpanisaṃ saḷāyatanaṃ, saḷāyatanūpaniso phasso, phassūpanisā vedanā, vedanūpanisā taṇhā, taṇhūpanisaṃ upādānaṃ, upādānūpaniso bhavo, bhavūpanisā jāti, jātūpanisaṃ dukkhaṃ, dukkhūpanisā saddhā, saddhūpanisaṃ pāmojjaṃ, pāmojjūpanisā pīti, pītūpanisā passaddhi, passaddhūpanisaṃ sukhaṃ, sukhūpaniso samādhi, samādhūpanisaṃ yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṃ, yathābhūtañāṇadassanūpanisā nibbidā, nibbidūpaniso virāgo, virāgūpanisā vimutti, vimuttūpanisaṃ khayeñāṇa’’nti. Tatiyaṃ.
简体中文
“诸比丘，正是如此，无明为行之依，行为识之依，识为名色之依，名色为六处之依，六处为触之依，触为受之依，受为爱之依，爱为取之依，取为有之依，有为生之依，生为苦之依，苦为信之依，信为悦之依，悦为喜之依，喜为轻安之依，轻安为乐之依，乐为定之依，定为如实知见之依，如实知见为厌离之依，厌离为离贪之依，离贪为解脱之依，解脱为尽智之依。”
English
“Just so, bhikkhus, formations have ignorance as their supporting condition; consciousness has formations as its supporting condition; name-and-form has consciousness as its supporting condition; the six sense bases have name-and-form as their supporting condition; contact has the six sense bases as its supporting condition; feeling has contact as its supporting condition; craving has feeling as its supporting condition; clinging has craving as its supporting condition; existence has clinging as its supporting condition; birth has existence
