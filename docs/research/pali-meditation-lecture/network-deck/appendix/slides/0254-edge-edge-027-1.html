安般十六阶 → 随观灭

关系证据 · 安般十六阶 → 随观灭 · 引文 1/2 · 关系术语：灭、nirodhānupassī

Pāli
‘‘Yasmiṃ samaye, ānanda, bhikkhu aniccānupassī…pe… virāgānupassī…pe… nirodhānupassī…pe… ‘paṭinissaggānupassī assasissāmī’ti sikkhati, ‘paṭinissaggānupassī passasissāmī’ti sikkhati – dhammesu dhammānupassī, ānanda, bhikkhu tasmiṃ samaye viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṃ. So yaṃ taṃ hoti abhijjhādomanassānaṃ pahānaṃ taṃ paññāya disvā sādhukaṃ ajjhupekkhitā hoti. Tasmātihānanda, dhammesu dhammānupassī bhikkhu tasmiṃ samaye viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṃ.
简体中文
“阿难，当比丘随观无常……乃至……随观离贪……乃至……随观灭尽……乃至……学‘我将随观舍弃而出息’，学‘我将随观舍弃而入息’——阿难，那时比丘于诸法随观法而住，精勤、正知、具念，调伏世间的贪欲与忧恼。他以智慧见到那贪欲与忧恼的舍断，并善加舍住。因此，阿难，那时比丘于诸法随观法而住，精勤、正知、具念，调伏世间的贪欲与忧恼。”
English
“Ānanda, at a time when a bhikkhu trains, thinking: ‘I will breathe out contemplating impermanence,’ … ‘I will breathe out contemplating dispassion,’ … ‘I will breathe out contemplating cessation,’ … ‘I will breathe out contemplating relinquishment,’ and ‘I will breathe in contemplating relinquishment’—at that time, Ānanda, a bhikkhu dwells contemplating phenomena in phenomena, ardent, clearly comprehending, and mindful, having removed covetousness and displeasure in regard to the world.
