七觉支成熟 → 轻安觉支

关系证据 · 七觉支成熟 → 轻安觉支 · 引文 1/1 · 关系术语：轻安觉支、passaddhi

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā passaddhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā passaddhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā? Atthi, bhikkhave, kāyappassaddhi cittappassaddhi . Tattha yonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā passaddhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā passaddhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是令未生起的轻安觉支得以生起，令已生起的轻安觉支修习圆满的食呢？诸比丘，有身轻安、心轻安。于此多作如理作意——此即是令未生起的轻安觉支得以生起，令已生起的轻安觉支修习圆满的食。”
English
“And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of tranquility, and for the development and fulfillment of the arisen enlightenment factor of tranquility? There are, monks, tranquility of body and tranquility of mind. In regard to them, frequent proper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of tranquility, and for the development and fulfillment of the arisen enlightenment factor of tranquility.
