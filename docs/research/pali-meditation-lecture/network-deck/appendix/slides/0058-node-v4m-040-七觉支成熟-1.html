七觉支成熟

节点证据 · 七觉支成熟 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, ayaṃ kāyo āhāraṭṭhitiko, āhāraṃ paṭicca tiṭṭhati, anāhāro no tiṭṭhati; evameva kho, bhikkhave, ime satta bojjhaṅgā āhāraṭṭhitikā, āhāraṃ paṭicca tiṭṭhanti, anāhārā no tiṭṭhantī’’ti. Dutiyaṃ.
简体中文
诸比丘，譬如这身体依食而住，缘食而立，无食则不立；亦复如是，诸比丘，此七觉支亦依食而住，缘食而立，无食则不立。”第二。
English
Just as, bhikkhus, this body is sustained by nutriment, subsists in dependence on nutriment, and does not subsist without nutriment; even so, bhikkhus, these seven enlightenment factors are sustained by nutriment, subsist in dependence on nutriment, and do not subsist without nutriment.
