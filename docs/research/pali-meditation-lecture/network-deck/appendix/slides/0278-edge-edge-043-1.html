正勤神足根力 → 观神足

关系证据 · 正勤神足根力 → 观神足 · 引文 1/2 · 关系术语：观、vīmaṃsā

Pāli
‘‘Kathaṃ bhāvitā ca, bhikkhave, cattāro iddhipādā kathaṃ bahulīkatā mahapphalā honti mahānisaṃsā? Idha, bhikkhave , bhikkhu chandasamādhippadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṃ iddhipādaṃ bhāveti – ‘iti me chando na ca atilīno bhavissati, na ca atippaggahito bhavissati, na ca ajjhattaṃ saṃkhitto bhavissati, na ca bahiddhā vikkhitto bhavissati’. Pacchāpuresaññī ca viharati – ‘yathā pure tathā pacchā, yathā pacchā tathā pure; yathā adho tathā uddhaṃ, yathā uddhaṃ tathā adho; yathā divā tathā rattiṃ yathā rattiṃ tathā divā’. Iti vivaṭena cetasā apariyonaddhena sappabhāsaṃ cittaṃ bhāveti. Vīriyasamādhi…pe… cittasamādhi…pe… vīma
简体中文
“诸比丘，四神足如何修习、如何多作，乃有大果、大益？诸比丘，于此，比丘修习欲三摩地勤行成就神足，作意：‘我此欲不至于过萎靡，不至于过高昂，不至于于内收摄，不至于于外散乱。’并且安住于前后想：‘如前如后，如后如前；如下如上，如上如下；如昼如夜，如夜如昼。’如此，他以敞开、无覆盖之心修习光明之心。修习精进三摩地……心三摩地……观三摩地勤行成就神足，作意：‘我此观不至于过萎靡，不至于过高昂，不至于于内收摄，不至于于外散乱。’并且安住于前后想：‘如前如后，如后如前；如下如上，如上如下；如昼如夜，如夜如昼。’如此，他以敞开、无覆盖之心修习光明之心。”
English
And how, monks, are the four bases of power, when developed and cultivated, of great fruit and great benefit? Here, monks, a monk develops the basis of power that is endowed with concentration founded on desire and the formations of striving, thinking: 'Thus my desire will be neither too slack nor too exerted, neither constricted internally nor scattered externally.' And he dwells perceiving what is after as what is before: 'As before, so after; as after, so before. As below, so above; as above, so below. As by day, so by night; as by night
