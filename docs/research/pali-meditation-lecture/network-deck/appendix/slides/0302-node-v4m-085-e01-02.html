vanāpāripūriṃ gacchati.
简体中文
彼时，比丘修习喜觉支；于彼时，比丘的喜觉支到达修习圆满。

lops the enlightenment factor of rapture, and for him the enlightenment factor of rapture goes to fulfillment by development.
