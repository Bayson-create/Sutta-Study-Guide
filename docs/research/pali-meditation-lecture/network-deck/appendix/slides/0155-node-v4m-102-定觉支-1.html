定觉支

节点证据 · 定觉支 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, anāhāro anuppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā? Atthi, bhikkhave, samathanimittaṃ abyagganimittaṃ. Tattha amanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamanāhāro anuppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是非食，能令未生的定觉支不生起，或令已生的定觉支不能修习圆满呢？诸比丘，有止相、不散乱相。于彼处，多次不作意——这即是非食，能令未生的定觉支不生起，或令已生的定觉支不能修习圆满。”
English
And what, bhikkhus, is the non-nourishment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of concentration, or for the fulfillment through development of the arisen enlightenment factor of concentration? There is, bhikkhus, the sign of serenity and the sign of non-distraction. Therein, the frequent practice of non-attention—this is the non-nourishment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of concentration, or for the fulfillment through development of the arisen enlightenment factor of concentration.
