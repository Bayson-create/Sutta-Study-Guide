随观无常

节点证据 · 随观无常 · 引文 2/2

Pāli
nupassī tasmiṃ samaye bhikkhu viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṃ.
简体中文
English
rly comprehending, and mindful, having subdued covetousness and displeasure for the world. Having seen with wisdom the abandoning of that covetousness and displeasure, he closely observes with equanimity. Therefore, monks, at that time a monk dwells contemplating phenomena in phenomena, ardent, clearly comprehending, and mindful, having subdued covetousness and displeasure for the world.
