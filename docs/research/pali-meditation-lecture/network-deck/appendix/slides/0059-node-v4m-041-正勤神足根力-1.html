正勤神足根力

节点证据 · 正勤神足根力 · 引文 1/1

Pāli
832 . ‘‘Cattārome, bhikkhave, iddhipādā bhāvitā bahulīkatā mahapphalā honti mahānisaṃsā’’.
简体中文
832. “诸比丘，此四神足若已修习、已多作，则有大果、大益。”
English
832. “Monks, these four bases of psychic power, when developed and cultivated, are of great fruit and great benefit.”
