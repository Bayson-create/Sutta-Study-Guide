mn10 · 106

Pāli
106 . ‘‘Ekāyano ayaṃ, bhikkhave, maggo sattānaṃ visuddhiyā, sokaparidevānaṃ pariddavānaṃ (sī. pī.) samatikkamāya, dukkhadomanassānaṃ atthaṅgamāya, ñāyassa adhigamāya, nibbānassa sacchikiriyāya, yadidaṃ cattāro satipaṭṭhānā.
简体中文
106. 诸比丘，此是唯一之道，为诸有情之清净，为超越愁悲，为灭尽苦忧，为证得正理，为现证涅槃，此即是四念处。
English
106. “Bhikkhus, this is the one and only way for the purification of beings, for the overcoming of sorrow and lamentation, for the disappearance of suffering and displeasure, for the attainment of the noble path, for the realization of Nibbāna—namely, the four establishments of mindfulness.”
