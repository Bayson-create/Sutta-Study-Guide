Pāli
‘‘Evaṃ bhāvitā kho, bhikkhave, ānāpānassati evaṃ bahulīkatā cattāro satipaṭṭhāne paripūreti.
简体中文
诸比丘，如是修习、如是多作的入出息念，确实能圆满四念处。
English
Monks, mindfulness of breathing thus developed and cultivated fulfills the four foundations of mindfulness.
