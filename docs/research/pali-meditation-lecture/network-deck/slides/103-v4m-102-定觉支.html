根本经主证据 · 三语逐句

sn46.51 · 605

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, anāhāro anuppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā? Atthi, bhikkhave, samathanimittaṃ abyagganimittaṃ. Tattha amanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamanāhāro anuppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā samādhisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是非食，能令未生的定觉支不生起，或令已生的定觉支不能修习圆满呢？诸比丘，有止相、不散乱相。于彼处，多次不作意——这即是非食，能令未生的定觉支不生起，或令已生的定觉支不能修习圆满。”
English
And what, bhikkhus, is the non-nourishment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of concentration, or for the fulfillment through development of the arisen enlightenment factor of concentration?
