识无边处

节点证据 · sn40.6 · 续页 1/3 · s0304m_mul:1424

Pāli
anantaṃ viññāṇanti viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ upasampajja viharati. Idaṃ vuccati viññāṇañcāyatana’nti. So khvāhaṃ, āvuso, sabbaso ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ samatikkamma anantaṃ viññāṇanti viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ upasampajja viharāmi . Tassa mayhaṃ, āvuso, iminā vihārena viharato ākāsānañcāyatanasahagatā saññāmanasikārā sa
简体中文
完全超越空无边处，思惟“识无边”，证入并安住于识无边处。这被称为识无边处。’贤友，我确实完全超越空无边处，思惟‘识无边’，证入并安住于识无边处。
English
hen, friends, this thought occurred to me: ‘Here, a bhikkhu, having entirely transcended the sphere of infinite space, (perceiving) “Consciousness is infinite,” enters and abides in the sphere of infinite consciousness.
