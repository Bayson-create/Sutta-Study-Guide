Pāli
‘‘Yo ca mettaṃ bhāvayati, appamāṇaṃ paṭissato; Tanū tanu (sī.) saṃyojanā honti, passato upadhikkhayaṃ.
简体中文
“若人有正念，修习无量慈，得见依止尽，结缚乃稀薄。”
English
For one who, ever mindful, develops boundless loving-kindness, their fetters are attenuated, seeing the destruction of acquisitions.
