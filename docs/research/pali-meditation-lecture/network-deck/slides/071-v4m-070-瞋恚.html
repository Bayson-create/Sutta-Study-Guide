Pāli

“诸比丘，对于未生起的瞋恚(byāpāda)之生起，以及已生起的瞋恚之增长、广大，其非食为何？诸比丘
English
And what, Bhikkhus, is the non-nutriment for the arising of unarisen ill will, or for the increase and abundance of arisen ill will? There is, Bhikkhu
