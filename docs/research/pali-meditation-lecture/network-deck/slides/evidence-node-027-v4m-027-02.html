定中观

节点证据 · an9.36 · 续页 2/3 · s0404m2_mul:287

Pāli
ve, jhānaṃ nissāya āsavānaṃ khayaṃ vadāmī’ti, iti yaṃ taṃ vuttaṃ, idametaṃ paṭicca vuttaṃ.
简体中文
有任何色法、受法……他即从那个世界成为不还者。‘诸比丘，我依止第四禅而说诸漏的灭尽’，此话就是依此而说的。”
English
hich is neither-painful-nor-pleasant and has purity of mindfulness due to equanimity. He thoroughly contemplates whatever phenomena are comprised therein—included in form, feeling… and so on… not subject to returning fro
