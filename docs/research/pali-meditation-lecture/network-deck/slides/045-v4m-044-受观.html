Pāli
‘‘Na vedanaṃ vedayati sapañño, Sukhampi dukkhampi bahussutopi; Ayañca dhīrassa puthujjanena, Mahā ayaṃ (syā. kaṃ. ka.) viseso kusalassa hoti.
简体中文
有慧者、多闻者，不感受乐受与苦受。此即贤者、善巧之圣弟子与凡夫的大差别。
English
“The wise, the very learned one, does not experience feeling, whether pleasant or painful. This is the great distinction of the steadfast and skilled
