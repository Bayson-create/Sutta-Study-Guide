根本经主证据 · 三语逐句

sn51.20 · 1558

Pāli
‘‘Kathañca , bhikkhave, bhikkhu vivaṭena cetasā apariyonaddhena sappabhāsaṃ cittaṃ bhāveti? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno ālokasaññā suggahitā hoti divāsaññā svādhiṭṭhitā. Evaṃ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu vivaṭena cetasā apariyonaddhena sappabhāsaṃ cittaṃ bhāveti.
简体中文
“诸比丘，比丘如何以敞开、无覆之心修习光明之心？诸比丘，于此，比丘善受持光明想，善安立白昼想。诸比丘，比丘如此以敞开、无覆之心修习光明之心。”
English
And how, monks, does a monk develop a luminous mind with a mind that is open and unenveloped? Here, monks, a monk's perception of light is well grasped, and the perception of daytime is well established. In this way, monks, a monk develops a luminous mind with a mind that is open and unenveloped.
