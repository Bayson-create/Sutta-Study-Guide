简体中文
了知：‘我生起了这个害寻。它既导致恼害自己，也导致恼害他人，还导致恼害双方；它障碍智慧，是苦恼品类，不导向涅槃。’诸比丘，当我反思‘它导致恼害
English
t thus: ‘This thought of violence has arisen in me. It leads to my own affliction, to the affliction of others, and to the affliction of both; it obstructs wisdom, is on the side of distress, and does not lead to Nibbāna
