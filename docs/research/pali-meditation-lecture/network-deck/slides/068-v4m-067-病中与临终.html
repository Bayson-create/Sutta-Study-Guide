sn46.16 · 468

Pāli
Idamavocāyasmā cundo. Samanuñño satthā ahosi. Vuṭṭhahi ca bhagavā tamhā ābādhā. Tathāpahīno ca bhagavato so ābādho ahosīti. Chaṭṭhaṃ.
简体中文
具寿纯陀说了此语。导师随喜赞同。世尊从那场病中起身。世尊的那个病痛就这样消退了。第六。
English
The Venerable Cunda said this. The Teacher approved. And the Blessed One arose from that illness. And that illness of the Blessed One was thus abandoned. The Sixth.
