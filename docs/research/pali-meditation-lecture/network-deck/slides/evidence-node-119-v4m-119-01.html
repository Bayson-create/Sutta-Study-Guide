念力

节点证据 · sn48.43 · 续页 1/5 · s0305m_mul:1246

Pāli
ayaṃ kho, bhikkhave, pariyāyo yaṃ pariyāyaṃ āgamma tassā nadiyā dve sotāni tveva saṅkhyaṃ gacchanti. Evameva kho, bhikkhave, yaṃ saddhindriyaṃ taṃ saddhābalaṃ, yaṃ saddhābalaṃ taṃ saddhindriyaṃ; yaṃ vīriyindriyaṃ taṃ vīriyabalaṃ, yaṃ vīriyabalaṃ taṃ vīriyindriyaṃ; yaṃ satindriyaṃ taṃ satibalaṃ , ya
简体中文
丘们，此即是理趣，依此理趣，那条河可计为二流。同样地，比丘们，信根即是信力，信力即是信根；精进根即是精进力，精进力即是精进根；念根即是念力，
English
and the water at the southern end—this, indeed, bhikkhus, is the reason by which that river comes to be reckoned as ‘two streams’. In this very same way, bhikkhus, that which is the faculty of faith is the power of faith
