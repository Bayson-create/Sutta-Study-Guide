节点证据 · mn119 · 续页 1/4 · s0203m_mul:440

Pāli
sampajānakārī hoti, asite pīte khāyite sāyite sampajānakārī hoti, uccārapassāvakamme sampajānakārī hoti, gate ṭhite nisinne sutte jāgarite bhāsite tuṇhībhāve sampajānakārī hoti. Tassa evaṃ appamattassa ātāpino pahitattassa viharato ye gehasitā sarasaṅkappā te pahīyanti. Tesaṃ pahānā ajjhattameva cit
简体中文
僧伽梨、钵、衣时保持正知，于食、饮、嚼、尝时保持正知，于大小便时保持正知，于行、住、坐、卧、醒、语、默时保持正知。他如是住于不放逸、热忱、策
English
and looking around; he acts with clear comprehension when bending and stretching; he acts with clear comprehension when wearing his outer robe, bowl, and other robes; he acts with clear comprehension when eating, drinkin
