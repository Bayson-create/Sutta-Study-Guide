sn55.2 · 998

Pāli
998 . ‘‘Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato ariyasāvako sotāpanno hoti avinipātadhammo niyato sambodhiparāyaṇo.
简体中文
998. “诸比丘，具足四法的圣弟子，是入流者，不堕恶趣法，决定趣向正觉。
English
998. “Monks, a noble disciple endowed with four qualities is a stream-enterer, not subject to falling into states of woe, certain, with enlightenment as his destination.
