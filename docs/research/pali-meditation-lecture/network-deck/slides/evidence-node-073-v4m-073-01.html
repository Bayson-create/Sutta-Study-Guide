Pāli
kārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannāya vā vicikicchāya uppādāya, uppannāya vā vicikicchāya bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
诸法。于彼，多作不如理作意——此即是能令未生起的疑生起，或令已生起的疑增长、广大的食。”
English
t are a basis for doubt. In regard to them, frequent improper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen doubt, and for the increase and expansion of arisen doubt.
