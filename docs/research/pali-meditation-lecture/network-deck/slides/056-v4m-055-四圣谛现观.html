Pāli
‘‘Yāvakīvañca me, bhikkhave, imesu catūsu ariyasaccesu evaṃ tiparivaṭṭaṃ dvādasākāraṃ yathābhūtaṃ ñāṇadassanaṃ na suvisuddhaṃ ahosi, neva tāvāhaṃ, bhikkhave , sadevake loke samārak
简体中文
“诸比丘，只要我于此四圣谛，如此三转、十二行相的如实智见尚未完全清净，诸比丘，我就决不在有
English
Bhikkhus, as long as my knowledge and vision of things as they really are regarding these Four Noble Truths—with their three rounds and twelve aspects
