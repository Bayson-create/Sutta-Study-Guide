瞋恚

节点证据 · sn46.51 · 续页 1/1 · s0305m_mul:596

Pāli
kāro – ayamanāhāro anuppannassa vā byāpādassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā byāpādassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
，有慈心解脱(mettācetovimutti)。于此，多多如理作意——此即是对于未生起的瞋恚之生起，以及已生起的瞋恚之增长、广大之非食。”
English
s, the liberation of mind through loving-kindness. Therein, the frequent practice of wise attention—this is the non-nutriment for the arising of unarisen ill will, or for the increase and abundance of arisen ill will.
