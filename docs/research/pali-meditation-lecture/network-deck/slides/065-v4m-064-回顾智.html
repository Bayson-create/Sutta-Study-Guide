Pāli
244 . ‘‘So evaṃ samāhite citte parisuddhe pariyodāte anaṅgaṇe vigatūpakkilese mudubhūte kammaniye ṭhite āneñjappatte pubbenivāsānussatiñāṇāya cittaṃ abhinīharati abhininnāmeti. So
简体中文
244. 当心如此安定、清净、洁白、无垢、远离随烦恼、柔软、适业、稳固、达到不动时，他将心引导
English
244. When his mind is thus concentrated, purified, bright, spotless, rid of defilement, pliable, workable, steady, and has attained imperturbability,
