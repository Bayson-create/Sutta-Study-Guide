节点证据 · ud4.1 · 续页 1/1 · s0503m_mul:271

Pāli
tesu vajjesu bhayadassāvī, samādāya sikkhissati sikkhāpadesu.
简体中文
律仪，具足正行与行处，于微细过失中见其危险，受持诸学处而修学。’
English
d by the restraint of the Pātimokkha, will be accomplished in conduct and resort, will see danger in the slightest faults, and, having undertaken the training rules, will train in them.
