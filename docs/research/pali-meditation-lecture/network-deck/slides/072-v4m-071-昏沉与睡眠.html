根本经主证据 · 三语逐句

sn46.51 · 585

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā thinamiddhassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā thinamiddhassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya? Atthi, bhikkhave, arati tandi vijambhitā bhattasammado cetaso ca līnattaṃ. Tattha ayonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā thinamiddhassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā thinamiddhassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
“诸比丘，什么是未生起的昏沉睡眠（thīnamiddha）生起，已生起的昏沉睡眠增长、广大的食呢？诸比丘，有不乐（arati）、倦怠（tandi）、呵欠（vijambhitā）、饭后昏沉（bhattasammada）与心的萎靡（cetaso līnatta）。于彼，多修不如理作意——此即是未生起的昏沉睡眠生起，已生起的昏沉睡眠增长、广大的食。”
English
“And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen sloth and torpor, and for the increase and expansion of arisen sloth and torpor?
