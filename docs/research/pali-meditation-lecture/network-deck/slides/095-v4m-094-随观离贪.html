Pāli
‘‘Kathaṃ bhāvitā, ānanda, satta bojjhaṅgā kathaṃ bahulīkatā vijjāvimuttiṃ paripūrenti? Idhānanda, bhikkhu satisambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti vivekanissitaṃ virāganissitaṃ nirodhanissitaṃ vossaggapariṇāmiṃ, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti …pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti vivekanissitaṃ virāganissitaṃ nirodhanissitaṃ vossaggapariṇāmiṃ. Evaṃ bhāvitā kho, ānanda, satta bojjhaṅgā evaṃ bahulīkatā vijjāvimuttiṃ paripūre
简体中文
“阿难，七觉支如何修习、如何多修习，才能圆满明与解脱呢？阿难，在此，比丘修习念觉支，依于远离，依于离贪，依于灭，导向舍弃；修习择法觉支……修习舍觉支，依于远离，依于离贪，依于灭，导向舍弃。阿难，如是修习、如是多修习七觉支，便能圆满明与解脱。”第三。
English
How, Ānanda, are the seven factors of enlightenment developed and cultivated so as to fulfill knowledge and liberation? Here, Ānanda, a bhikkhu develops the enlightenment factor of mindfulness, which is based on seclusion, based on dispassion, based on cessation, culminating in relinquishment.
