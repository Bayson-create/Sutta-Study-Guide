嗔恚对治

节点证据 · sn46.51 · 续页 1/1 · s0305m_mul:584

Pāli
āro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā byāpādassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā byāpādassa bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya.
简体中文
paṭighanimitta）。于彼，多修不如理作意——此即是未生起的嗔恚生起，已生起的嗔恚增长、广大的食。”
English
ign of aversion. In regard to that, frequent improper attention—this is the nutriment for the arising of unarisen ill will, and for the increase and expansion of arisen ill will.
