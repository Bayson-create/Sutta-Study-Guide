昏沉睡眠

节点证据 · an7.61 · 续页 1/1 · s0403m3_mul:377

Pāli
anetaṃ vijjati yaṃ te evaṃ viharato taṃ middhaṃ pahīyetha.
简体中文
随观。有此可能：你如是安住，那昏沉即得以断除。
English
ou have heard it and learned it; you should mentally review it. It is possible that by dwelling thus, that drowsiness of yours may be abandoned.
