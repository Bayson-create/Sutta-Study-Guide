简体中文
232. 舍卫城因缘。“诸比丘，我将为你们宣说五盖（nīvaraṇa）与七觉支的食与非食，且谛听。诸比丘
English
232. At Sāvatthī. “Monks, I will teach the nutriment and non-nutriment for the five hindrances and the seven factors of enlightenment. Listen to that.
