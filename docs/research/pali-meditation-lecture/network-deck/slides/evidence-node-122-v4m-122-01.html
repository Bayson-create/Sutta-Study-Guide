简体中文
话是这样说的，此乃依何而说呢？诸比丘，于此，有比丘舍乐、舍苦，先已灭除喜、忧，进入并安住于不苦不乐、舍念清净的第四禅。他对其中所有色、受、想
English
destruction of the taints by relying on the fourth jhāna.’ Indeed, this was said. And in reference to what was it said? Here, bhikkhus, with the abandoning of pleasure and pain, and with the previous disappearance of joy
