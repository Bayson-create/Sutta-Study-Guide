根本经主证据 · 三语逐句

sn46.2 · 400

Pāli
‘‘Ko ca, bhikkhave, āhāro anuppannassa vā pītisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā pītisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā? Atthi, bhikkhave, pītisambojjhaṅgaṭṭhānīyā dhammā. Tattha yonisomanasikārabahulīkāro – ayamāhāro anuppannassa vā pītisambojjhaṅgassa uppādāya, uppannassa vā pītisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūriyā.
简体中文
诸比丘，何为令未生之喜觉支生起，已生之喜觉支修习圆满之食？诸比丘，有喜觉支所缘法(pītisambojjhaṅgaṭṭhānīyā dhammā)。于此，多作如理作意——此为令未生之喜觉支生起，已生之喜觉支修习圆满之食。
English
And what, monks, is the nutriment for the arising of the unarisen enlightenment factor of rapture, or for the fulfillment through development of the arisen enlightenment factor of rapture?
